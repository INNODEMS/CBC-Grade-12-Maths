Exercise 4.

The graph of \(y = \sin x\) is mapped onto \(y = 3\sin 2x\text{.}\) Describe both stretches, and find the first maximum point of \(y = 3\sin 2x\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
Stretch factor 3 with the \(x\)-axis invariant; stretch factor \(\dfrac{1}{2}\) with the \(y\)-axis invariant. First maximum \((45^\circ, 3)\text{.}\)
