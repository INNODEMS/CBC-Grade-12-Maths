Example 12.3.5 Goats: Quartiles by Calculation.

Estimate the median and the quartiles of the masses of the \(50\) goats at Garissa market in Example 12.2.6.
🔗
Solution.
Step 1: positions. \(n = 50\text{,}\) so \(Q_1\) is at \(50 \div 4 = 12.5\text{,}\) the median at \(25\) and \(Q_3\) at \(37.5\text{.}\)
🔗
Step 2: \(Q_1\text{.}\) The cumulative frequency first reaches \(12.5\) in the class 25–29, so \(L = 24.5\text{,}\) \(CF = 10\text{,}\) \(F = 12\text{,}\) \(c = 5\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} Q_1 = 24.5 + \frac{12.5 - 10}{12} \times 5 = 25.54\ldots \approx 25.5 \,\text{kg} \end{equation*}
🔗
Step 3: median. Position \(25\) is in 30–34: \(L = 29.5\text{,}\) \(CF = 22\text{,}\) \(F = 14\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} \text{median} = 29.5 + \frac{25 - 22}{14} \times 5 = 30.57\ldots \approx 30.6 \,\text{kg} \end{equation*}
🔗
Step 4: \(Q_3\text{.}\) Position \(37.5\) is in 35–39: \(L = 34.5\text{,}\) \(CF = 36\text{,}\) \(F = 9\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} Q_3 = 34.5 + \frac{37.5 - 36}{9} \times 5 = 35.33\ldots \approx 35.3 \,\text{kg} \end{equation*}
