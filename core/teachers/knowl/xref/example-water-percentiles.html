Example 12.3.17 Household Water Use.

The water used in a day, \(w\) litres, by \(90\) households in Machakos is shown below. Find \(P_{10}\) and \(P_{90}\text{,}\) and the 10 to 90 percentile range.
🔗
Solution.
The cumulative frequencies are \(8, 23, 47, 67, 80, 87, 90\text{.}\) \(P_{10}\) is at \(10 \times 90 \div 100 = 9\) and \(P_{90}\) at \(81\text{.}\)
\begin{align*} P_{10} \amp = 20 + \frac{9 - 8}{15} \times 20 \approx 21.3 \,\text{litres}\\ P_{90} \amp = 100 + \frac{81 - 80}{7} \times 20 \approx 102.9 \,\text{litres} \end{align*}
The 10 to 90 percentile range is \(102.86 - 21.33 \approx 81.5\,\text{litres}\text{:}\) the middle \(80\) per cent of households used amounts spread over about \(81.5\,\text{litres}\text{.}\)
