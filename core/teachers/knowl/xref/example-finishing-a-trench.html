Example 3.2.13 Finishing a Trench.

\(10\) workers dig \(300\,\text{m}\) of a \(750\,\text{m}\) water trench in \(6\) days. How many workers are needed to dig the rest in \(5\) days?
🔗
Solution.
The job so far is \(10 \times 6 = 60\) worker-days for \(300\,\text{m}\text{,}\) so one worker digs \(300 \div 60 = 5\,\text{m}\) a day.
🔗
The rest is \(750 - 300 = 450\,\text{m}\text{.}\) In \(5\) days one worker digs \(25\,\text{m}\text{,}\) so \(450 \div 25 = 18\) workers are needed.
