Example 12.3.26 Goats: Deciles and Percentiles from the Curve.

Use the curve to estimate \(D_1\text{,}\) \(D_9\) and \(P_{80}\) of the goat masses.
🔗
Solution.
Go across from \(50 \div 10 = 5\text{,}\) \(45\) and \(80 \times 50 \div 100 = 40\text{.}\)
🔗
\(D_1 \approx 20.9\,\text{kg}\text{,}\) \(P_{80} \approx 36.7\,\text{kg}\) and \(D_9 = 39.5\,\text{kg}\text{.}\) By calculation, for example, \(P_{80} = 34.5 + \dfrac{40 - 36}{9} \times 5 = 36.72\text{.}\)
