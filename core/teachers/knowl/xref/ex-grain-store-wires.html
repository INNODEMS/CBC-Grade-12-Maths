Exercise 5.

A grain store at a cereals depot in Eldoret is a cuboid \(ABCDEFGH\) with \(AB = 15\,\text{m}\text{,}\) \(BC = 9\,\text{m}\) and \(AE = 5\,\text{m}\text{.}\) Wires run from the ceiling corner \(G\) to the floor corners \(A\) and \(C\text{.}\) Find the angle \(AGC\) between the wires.
🔗
Answer.
\(74.0^{\circ}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(GC\) is vertical, so triangle \(AGC\) has a right angle at \(C\text{.}\) \(AC = \sqrt{225 + 81} = \sqrt{306}\) and \(GC = 5\text{,}\) so \(\tan AGC = \dfrac{\sqrt{306}}{5}\) and angle \(AGC \approx 74.0^{\circ}\text{.}\)
