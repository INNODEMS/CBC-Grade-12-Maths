Example 4.3.10 Beehives and Cages.

A youth group in Kitui makes \(x\) beehives and \(y\) chicken cages a month. A beehive uses \(7\) planks and a cage \(3\) planks; the group has \(35\) planks. A beehive needs \(3\) hours on the group’s saw and a cage \(7\) hours; the saw is free for \(31\) hours. The profit is Ksh \(900\) on a beehive and Ksh \(700\) on a cage. How many of each should the group make?
🔗

Solution.

The model is \(7x + 3y \le 35\text{,}\) \(3x + 7y \le 31\text{,}\) \(x \ge 0\text{,}\) \(y \ge 0\text{,}\) with \(x\) and \(y\) whole numbers, and \(P = 900x + 700y\text{.}\)
🔗
Step 1: the vertices. Solve \(7x + 3y = 35\) and \(3x + 7y = 31\text{:}\) multiply the first by \(7\) and the second by \(3\) and subtract, \(40x = 152\text{,}\) so \(x = 3.8\) and \(y = 2.8\text{.}\) The vertices are \((0, 0)\text{,}\) \((5, 0)\text{,}\) \((3.8, 2.8)\) and \(\left(0, \tfrac{31}{7}\right) \approx (0, 4.43)\text{.}\)
🔗
Step 2: the objective at each vertex. \(P = 0\text{,}\) \(4500\text{,}\) \(5380\) and \(3100\text{.}\) The best vertex is \((3.8, 2.8)\text{,}\) but the group cannot make \(3.8\) beehives.
🔗
Step 3: do not just round. Rounding gives \((4, 3)\text{,}\) which needs \(7 \times 4 + 3 \times 3 = 37\) planks: more than \(35\text{,}\) so \((4, 3)\) is outside R.
🔗
Step 4: sample the whole-number points near the vertex. Take \(x = 2, 3, 4, 5\) and \(y = 1, 2, 3, 4\text{.}\) The points of this block that are in R, with their profits, are
\begin{equation*} (2, 1)\colon 2500, \quad (2, 2)\colon 3200, \quad (2, 3)\colon 3900, \quad (3, 1)\colon 3400, \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} (3, 2)\colon 4100, \quad (3, 3)\colon 4800, \quad (4, 1)\colon 4300, \quad (4, 2)\colon 5000\text{.} \end{equation*}
The others break a constraint (for example \((5, 1)\) needs \(38\) planks). The vertex \((5, 0)\) gives only \(4500\text{.}\)
🔗
Step 5: check. \((4, 2)\) uses \(28 + 6 = 34\) planks and \(12 + 14 = 26\) saw hours, so it is in R. The region is small, so Figure 4.3.11 shows all \(22\) of its whole-number points: none beats \((4, 2)\text{.}\) The group should make \(4\) beehives and \(2\) cages, for a profit of Ksh \(5000\text{.}\)
