Exercise 1.

Find the distance along the parallel \(30^\circ\,\text{N}\) between the meridians \(10^\circ\,\text{E}\) and \(50^\circ\,\text{E}\text{,}\) in nautical miles and in kilometres.
🔗
Answer.
\(2078.5\,\text{nm}\text{;}\) \(3852.9\,\text{km}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Difference in longitude \(40^\circ = 2400'\text{.}\) \(2400 \cos 30^\circ = 2078.5\,\text{nm}\text{;}\) \(\tfrac{40}{360} \times 40\,040 \times \cos 30^\circ = 3852.9\,\text{km}\text{.}\)
