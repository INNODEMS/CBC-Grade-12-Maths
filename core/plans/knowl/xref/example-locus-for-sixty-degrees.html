Example 7.5.9 The Locus for \(60^{\circ}\).

\(AB = 7\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Construct the locus of a point \(P\) on one side of \(AB\) such that \(\angle APB = 60^{\circ}\text{.}\) Find the distance of the centre from \(AB\) and the radius of the arc.
🔗

Solution.

Step 1: Construct the perpendicular bisector of \(AB\text{,}\) meeting it at \(M\text{.}\)
🔗
Step 2: At \(A\text{,}\) on \(P\)’s side, draw a line making \(90^{\circ} - 60^{\circ} = 30^{\circ}\) with \(AB\text{.}\) It meets the bisector at the centre \(O\text{.}\)
🔗
Step 3: Draw the arc with centre \(O\) through \(A\) and \(B\text{,}\) on \(P\)’s side (Figure 7.5.6).
🔗
In the right-angled triangle \(OMA\text{,}\) \(AM = 3.5\,\text{cm}\) and \(\angle OAM = 30^{\circ}\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} OM = 3.5\tan 30^{\circ} = 2.02, \qquad OA = \frac{7}{2\sin 60^{\circ}} = 4.04 \end{equation*}
The centre is \(2.0\,\text{cm}\) from \(AB\) and the radius is \(4.0\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Check: \(\angle AOB = 180^{\circ} - 2 \times 30^{\circ} = 120^{\circ} = 2 \times 60^{\circ}\text{.}\)
