Exercise 2.

A region is the set of points less than \(5\) units from \(A(0, 0)\) and nearer to \(B(6, 0)\) than to \(A\text{.}\) Test the points \((4, 2)\text{,}\) \((2, 1)\) and \((4, 4)\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\((4, 2)\) is in the region; \((2, 1)\) is not (nearer to \(A\)); \((4, 4)\) is not (about \(5.66\) units from \(A\)).
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The perpendicular bisector of \(AB\) is \(x = 3\text{,}\) so “nearer to \(B\)” means \(x \gt 3\text{.}\) \((4, 2)\text{:}\) \(4^2 + 2^2 = 20 \lt 25\) and \(x = 4 \gt 3\text{:}\) in. \((2, 1)\text{:}\) \(x = 2 \lt 3\text{:}\) out. \((4, 4)\text{:}\) \(16 + 16 = 32 \gt 25\text{:}\) out.
