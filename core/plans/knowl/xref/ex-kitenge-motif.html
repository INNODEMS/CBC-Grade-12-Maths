Exercise 5.

A kitenge print repeats a motif, the triangle with vertices \((2, 1)\text{,}\) \((4, 1)\) and \((3, 3)\text{,}\) together with its mirror image in the \(y\)-axis. Write down the matrix of the reflection in the \(y\)-axis and use it to find the vertices of the mirror image.
🔗
Answer.
\(\begin{bmatrix} -1 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp 1 \end{bmatrix}\text{;}\) \((-2, 1)\text{,}\) \((-4, 1)\text{,}\) \((-3, 3)\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\begin{bmatrix} -1 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp 1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 2 \amp 4 \amp 3 \\ 1 \amp 1 \amp 3 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} -2 \amp -4 \amp -3 \\ 1 \amp 1 \amp 3 \end{bmatrix}\text{.}\)
