Exercise 1.

For \(y = x^2\) from \(x = 0\) to \(x = 2\text{,}\) use \(2\) strips to find the trapezoidal and mid-ordinate estimates of the area. Then find the exact area.
🔗
Answer.
\(T = 3\text{,}\) \(M = 2.5\text{,}\) exact \(\frac{8}{3} \approx 2.67\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Ordinates \(0, 1, 4\text{:}\) \(T = \tfrac{1}{2}(0 + 4 + 2) = 3\text{.}\) Mid-ordinates \(0.25, 2.25\text{:}\) \(M = 2.5\text{.}\) Exact: \(\left[\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^2 = \frac{8}{3}\text{.}\)
