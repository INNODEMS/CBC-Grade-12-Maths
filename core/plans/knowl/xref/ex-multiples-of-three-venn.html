Exercise 1.

\(\xi = \{1, 2, \ldots, 20\}\text{,}\) \(A\) is the set of multiples of \(3\) and \(B\) is the set of odd numbers in \(\xi\text{.}\) Draw the Venn diagram. List \(A \cap B\) and the elements outside both circles.
🔗
Answer.
\(A\) only \(\{6, 12, 18\}\text{;}\) \(A \cap B = \{3, 9, 15\}\text{;}\) \(B\) only \(\{1, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19\}\text{;}\) outside both \(\{2, 4, 8, 10, 14, 16, 20\}\text{.}\)
