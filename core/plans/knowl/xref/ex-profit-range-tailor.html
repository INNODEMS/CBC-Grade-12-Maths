Exercise 5. Challenge: How Far Can the Shirt Profit Move?

The tailor’s profit is \(P = kx + 800y\) shillings on the region of Example 4.3.30. For which values of \(k\) is \((28, 6)\) still an optimal solution?
🔗
Hint.
Use the gradient check: \(-\tfrac{k}{800}\) must lie between the gradients of the two edges that meet at \((28, 6)\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(\tfrac{400}{3} \le k \le \tfrac{2400}{7}\text{,}\) that is about \(133.33 \le k \le 342.86\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The edges at \((28, 6)\) have gradients \(-\tfrac{3}{7}\) and \(-\tfrac{1}{6}\text{.}\) We need \(\tfrac{1}{6} \le \tfrac{k}{800} \le \tfrac{3}{7}\text{,}\) so \(\tfrac{800}{6} \le k \le \tfrac{2400}{7}\text{,}\) that is \(\tfrac{400}{3} \le k \le \tfrac{2400}{7}\) (about \(133.33\) to \(342.86\)). At either end the search line lies along an edge and two vertices tie.
