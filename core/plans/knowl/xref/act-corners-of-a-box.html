Learner Experience 10.2.2 Corners of a Box.

A carton \(40\,\text{cm}\) long, \(30\,\text{cm}\) wide and \(20\,\text{cm}\) high has one corner at the origin \(O\text{.}\) It is lettered like the cuboid in Example 10.1.11: base \(OABC\) with \(A\) on the \(x\)-axis and \(C\) on the \(y\)-axis, and \(D\text{,}\) \(E\text{,}\) \(F\text{,}\) \(G\) above \(O\text{,}\) \(A\text{,}\) \(B\text{,}\) \(C\text{.}\) Distances are in centimetres.
🔗
  1. The position vector of a point is the column of moves from \(O\) to the point. Write the position vectors of \(A\text{,}\) \(B\) and \(F\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(\overrightarrow{OA} = \begin{pmatrix} 40 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}\text{,}\) \(\overrightarrow{OB} = \begin{pmatrix} 40 \\ 30 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}\text{,}\) \(\overrightarrow{OF} = \begin{pmatrix} 40 \\ 30 \\ 20 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Find \(\overrightarrow{AF}\) by counting the moves along the edges from \(A\) to \(F\text{.}\) Then work out \(\overrightarrow{OF} - \overrightarrow{OA}\text{.}\) What do you notice?
    🔗
    Solution.
    Counting: \(0\) along \(x\text{,}\) \(30\) along \(y\text{,}\) \(20\) up, so \(\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 30 \\ 20 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\) Subtracting gives the same column.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Write \(\overrightarrow{FA}\text{.}\) How is it related to \(\overrightarrow{AF}\text{?}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ -30 \\ -20 \end{pmatrix} = -\overrightarrow{AF}\text{:}\) the same moves in reverse.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  4. Compare \(\overrightarrow{DE}\) with \(\overrightarrow{OA}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Both are \(\begin{pmatrix} 40 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\) They are equal vectors: parallel edges of the same length, pointing the same way.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  5. Find \(\overrightarrow{BD}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(\overrightarrow{OD} - \overrightarrow{OB} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ 20 \end{pmatrix} - \begin{pmatrix} 40 \\ 30 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -40 \\ -30 \\ 20 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
