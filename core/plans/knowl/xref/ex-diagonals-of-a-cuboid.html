Exercise 1.

A cuboid measures \(12\,\text{cm}\) by \(9\,\text{cm}\) by \(8\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Find the length of a diagonal of its \(12\,\text{cm}\) by \(9\,\text{cm}\) base, and of its space diagonal.
🔗
Answer.
\(15\,\text{cm}\text{;}\) \(17\,\text{cm}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Base diagonal \(\sqrt{144 + 81} = \sqrt{225} = 15\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Space diagonal \(\sqrt{225 + 64} = \sqrt{289} = 17\,\text{cm}\text{.}\)
