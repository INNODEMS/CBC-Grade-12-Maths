Exercise 1.

Find the vertices of the region R of Exercise 4.2.1.3: \(8x + 7y \le 112\text{,}\) \(2x + 7y \le 70\text{,}\) \(x \ge 0\text{,}\) \(y \ge 0\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\((0, 0)\text{,}\) \((14, 0)\text{,}\) \((7, 8)\text{,}\) \((0, 10)\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Subtracting \(2x + 7y = 70\) from \(8x + 7y = 112\) gives \(6x = 42\text{,}\) so \(x = 7\) and \(y = 8\text{.}\) On the \(x\)-axis the first line comes first, at \((14, 0)\text{;}\) on the \(y\)-axis the second comes first, at \((0, 10)\text{.}\) The vertices are \((0, 0)\text{,}\) \((14, 0)\text{,}\) \((7, 8)\) and \((0, 10)\text{.}\)
