Example 10.3.9 Changing the Form.

(a) Write \(\begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 7 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}\) in terms of \(\mathbf{i}\text{,}\) \(\mathbf{j}\) and \(\mathbf{k}\text{.}\) (b) Write \(5\mathbf{i} - 3\mathbf{k}\) as a column. (c) Write the position vector of \(P(4, -1, 6)\) in terms of \(\mathbf{i}\text{,}\) \(\mathbf{j}\) and \(\mathbf{k}\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

(a) \(-2\mathbf{i} + 7\mathbf{j}\text{:}\) the \(\mathbf{k}\) component is \(0\text{,}\) so it is left out.
🔗
(b) There is no \(\mathbf{j}\) term, so the middle component is \(0\text{:}\) \(\begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 0 \\ -3 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\)
🔗
(c) \(\overrightarrow{OP} = 4\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 6\mathbf{k}\text{.}\)
