Exercise 1.

A particle has constant acceleration \(a = 4\,\text{m/s}^2\text{.}\) When \(t = 0\) its velocity is \(3\,\text{m/s}\) and it is at the origin. Find \(v\) and \(s\) in terms of \(t\text{,}\) and \(s\) when \(t = 5\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(v = 4t + 3\text{;}\) \(s = 2t^2 + 3t\text{;}\) \(65\,\text{m}\)
