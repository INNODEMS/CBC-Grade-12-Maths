Learner Experience 12.3.2 Where Is the 10th Learner?

The table repeats the journey times to school, in minutes (to the nearest minute), of \(40\) learners in Nakuru from Learner Experience 12.2.2, with the cumulative frequencies.
🔗
  1. Imagine the 40 learners in order of journey time. In which class is the 10th learner? The 20th? The 30th?
    🔗
    Solution.
    15–24 (the cumulative frequency first reaches 10 there), 25–34 and 35–44.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  2. How many learners come before the class 15–24? So which learner in that class is the 10th learner overall?
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(4\) learners, so the 10th learner is the 6th of the \(9\) in the class.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  3. The class 15–24 really runs from \(14.5\) to \(24.5\) minutes. Assume its \(9\) learners are spread evenly across it. Estimate the 10th learner’s time, to 1 decimal place.
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(14.5 + \dfrac{6}{9} \times 10 = 21.2\) minutes. Since \(10 = 40 \div 4\text{,}\) this is the lower quartile. Point out the strip in Figure 12.3.4.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  4. In the same way, estimate the times of the 20th and 30th learners.
    🔗
    Solution.
    20th: \(24.5 + \dfrac{20 - 13}{13} \times 10 = 29.9\) minutes (the median). 30th: \(34.5 + \dfrac{30 - 26}{8} \times 10 = 39.5\) minutes (the upper quartile).
