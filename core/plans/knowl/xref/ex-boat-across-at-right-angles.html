Exercise 1.

A boat is steered straight across a river at \(3.5\,\text{m/s}\) through the water. The current is \(1.2\,\text{m/s}\text{.}\) Find the ground speed and the drift angle.
🔗
Answer.
\(3.7\,\text{m/s}\text{;}\) \(18.9^\circ\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\sqrt{3.5^2 + 1.2^2} = \sqrt{13.69} = 3.7\,\text{m/s}\text{;}\) \(\tan d = 1.2 \div 3.5\text{,}\) \(d = 18.9^\circ\text{.}\)
