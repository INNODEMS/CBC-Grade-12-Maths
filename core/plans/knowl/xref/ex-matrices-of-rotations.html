Exercise 2.

Write down the matrices of the rotations about \(O\) of \(90^\circ\) anticlockwise and of \(180^\circ\text{.}\) Use them to find the images of \((-3, 2)\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(\begin{bmatrix} 0 \amp -1 \\ 1 \amp 0 \end{bmatrix}\text{,}\) image \((-2, -3)\text{;}\) \(\begin{bmatrix} -1 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp -1 \end{bmatrix}\text{,}\) image \((3, -2)\)
