Example 5.1.28 Reflection in the Line \(y = -x\).

Find the matrix of the reflection in the line \(y = -x\text{,}\) and use it to find the image of \((3, 1)\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

Reflecting in \(y = -x\) sends \((1, 0)\) to \((0, -1)\) and \((0, 1)\) to \((-1, 0)\text{.}\) These are the columns:
\begin{equation*} M = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \amp -1 \\ -1 \amp 0 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad M \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ -3 \end{pmatrix} \end{equation*}
The image is \((-1, -3)\text{.}\) This agrees with the Grade 10 rule for this mirror line: the coordinates swap and change sign.
