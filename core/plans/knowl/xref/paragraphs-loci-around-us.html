Loci Around Us.

Loci are used wherever things must be placed fairly or kept at a safe distance.
🔗
  • A borehole or a dispensary that serves two villages fairly is placed on the perpendicular bisector of the line joining them.
    🔗
    🔗
  • A mobile mast or a radio transmitter covers the inside of a circle; where two masts overlap, the coverage regions intersect.
    🔗
    🔗
  • Safety strips along power lines and pipelines are the regions between two parallel lines.
    🔗
    🔗
  • The offside rule in football uses a line parallel to the goal line and an inequality.
    🔗
    🔗
  • The tip of a windscreen wiper or a robot arm moves on an arc of a circle.
    🔗
    🔗
  • Surveyors find the centre of a round water tank from the perpendicular bisectors of two chords.
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
This answers the question we asked at the start of the chapter (Key Inquiry Questions 7.1.1): we use loci to decide where things can be placed, and where they can move, when they must obey rules about distances and angles.
