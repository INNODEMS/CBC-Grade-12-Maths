Exercise 5.

The point \(P(3, 4, 0)\) divides \(AB\) internally in the ratio \(2 : 3\text{,}\) and \(A\) is \((1, 0, -2)\text{.}\) Find \(B\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(B(6, 10, 3)\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\overrightarrow{AP} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 4 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} = \frac{2}{5}\overrightarrow{AB}\text{,}\) so \(\overrightarrow{AB} = \frac{5}{2}\begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 4 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 10 \\ 5 \end{pmatrix}\) and \(B\) is \((1 + 5, 0 + 10, -2 + 5) = (6, 10, 3)\text{.}\)
