Exercise 6. Challenge: A Quick Subtraction.

Show that \((a + b)^2 - (a - b)^2 = 4ab\text{,}\) and use it to work out \(53^2 - 47^2\) without a calculator.
🔗
Hint.
Put \(a = 50\) and \(b = 3\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(600\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\((a^2 + 2ab + b^2) - (a^2 - 2ab + b^2) = 4ab\text{.}\) With \(a = 50\text{,}\) \(b = 3\text{:}\) \(53^2 - 47^2 = 4 \times 50 \times 3 = 600\text{.}\)
