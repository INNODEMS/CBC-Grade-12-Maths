Example 6.3.8 The Arch Again.

Use the mid-ordinate rule with \(3\) strips to estimate the area bounded by \(y = 8 + 2x - \tfrac{1}{2}x^2\text{,}\) the \(x\)-axis and the lines \(x = 0\) and \(x = 6\text{.}\) The exact area is \(48\) and the trapezoidal estimate in Example 6.2.10 was \(46\text{.}\) Compare.
🔗

Solution.

\(h = (6 - 0) \div 3 = 2\text{.}\) The strips are \(0\) to \(2\text{,}\) \(2\) to \(4\) and \(4\) to \(6\text{,}\) with middles at \(x = 1, 3, 5\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{equation*} A \approx 2 \times (9.5 + 9.5 + 5.5) = 2 \times 24.5 = 49 \end{equation*}
The curve bends downwards, so the mid-ordinate estimate \(49\) is too big and the trapezoidal estimate \(46\) too small; the exact area \(48\) lies between them, nearer the mid-ordinate value.
