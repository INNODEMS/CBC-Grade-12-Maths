Example 2.2.10 Expanding \((x - 2)^5\).

Expand \((x - 2)^5\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

The row for power 5 is 1, 5, 10, 10, 5, 1. Put \(a = x\) and \(b = -2\text{:}\)
\begin{align*} (x - 2)^5 \amp = x^5 + 5x^4(-2) + 10x^3(-2)^2 + 10x^2(-2)^3 + 5x(-2)^4 + (-2)^5\\ \amp = x^5 - 10x^4 + 40x^3 - 80x^2 + 80x - 32 \end{align*}
Check with \(x = 1\text{:}\) \((1 - 2)^5 = -1\) and \(1 - 10 + 40 - 80 + 80 - 32 = -1\text{.}\)
