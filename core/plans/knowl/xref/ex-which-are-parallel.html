Exercise 1.

Which of these vectors are parallel to \(\begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \\ -3 \end{pmatrix}\text{?}\) Give \(k\) for each one that is. (a) \(\begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ 3 \\ -9 \end{pmatrix}\) (b) \(\begin{pmatrix} -4 \\ -2 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix}\) (c) \(\begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 2 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}\) (d) \(\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0.5 \\ -1.5 \end{pmatrix}\)
🔗
Answer.
(a) yes, \(k = 3\) (b) yes, \(k = -2\text{,}\) opposite direction (c) no (d) yes, \(k = \frac{1}{2}\)
