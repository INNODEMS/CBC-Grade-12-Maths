Exercise 7.

A maize miller in Eldoret finds that the cost \(C\) (in thousands of Ksh) of milling \(x\) tonnes changes at the rate \(\dfrac{dC}{dx} = 3x^2 - 12x + 40\text{.}\) Her fixed costs, when \(x = 0\text{,}\) are \(50\) thousand Ksh. Find \(C\) in terms of \(x\text{,}\) and the cost of milling \(5\) tonnes.
🔗
Answer.
\(C = x^3 - 6x^2 + 40x + 50\text{;}\) Ksh \(225\,000\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(C = x^3 - 6x^2 + 40x + c\) and \(C(0) = c = 50\text{.}\) Then \(C(5) = 125 - 150 + 200 + 50 = 225\) thousand Ksh, that is Ksh \(225\,000\text{.}\)
