Exercise 4. Challenge: Is the Centre Fixed?

Can the number in all three sets be found from the three set totals, the overall total and the number in none alone? Explain.
🔗
Answer.
No. Different diagrams fit the same numbers. A diagram with \(1\) person in all three sets and \(1\) in \(C\) only, and a diagram with \(1\) person in \(A\) and \(C\) only and \(1\) in \(B\) and \(C\) only, both give \(n(A) = 1\text{,}\) \(n(B) = 1\text{,}\) \(n(C) = 2\) and \(2\) people in at least one set, but their centres are \(1\) and \(0\text{.}\) The overlap counts are needed.
