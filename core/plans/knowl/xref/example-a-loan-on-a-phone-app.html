Example 2.3.30 A Loan on a Phone App.

Wanjiku borrows Ksh \(2000\) through a phone app that charges \(1\%\) interest a day, compounded daily. She repays after 10 days. Estimate the amount she owes, using four terms.
🔗

Solution.

She owes \(2000(1.01)^{10}\text{.}\) With \(x = 0.01\text{,}\) the row for power 10 starts 1, 10, 45, 120:
\begin{equation*} 1 + 0.1 + 0.0045 + 0.00012 = 1.10462 \end{equation*}
so she owes about \(2000 \times 1.10462\) = Ksh \(2209.24\) (a calculator also gives Ksh \(2209.24\)). The interest is about Ksh \(209\) in only 10 days: more than \(10 \times 1\% = 10\%\) of the loan, because of the later terms.
🔗
Before borrowing on a phone, read the daily rate and work out the real cost. At \(1\%\) a day, a loan kept for a month costs about \(35\%\) of the amount borrowed.
