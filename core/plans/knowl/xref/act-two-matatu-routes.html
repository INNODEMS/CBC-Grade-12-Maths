Learner Experience 12.4.2 Two Matatu Routes.

The times, in minutes, of \(11\) matatu journeys on each of two routes into Nairobi were recorded.
🔗
  1. Put each route’s times in order. Find the range of each: the longest time minus the shortest.
    🔗
    Solution.
    A: \(25, 26, 27, 28, 29, 30, 31, 32, 33, 35, 45\text{,}\) range \(20\text{.}\) B: \(22, 24, 25, 26, 28, 30, 32, 34, 36, 38, 40\text{,}\) range \(18\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Find the median and the quartiles of each route.
    🔗
    Solution.
    Positions \(3\text{,}\) \(6\text{,}\) \(9\text{.}\) A: \(27, 30, 33\text{.}\) B: \(25, 30, 36\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  3. For each route find \(Q_3 - Q_1\) and half of it.
    🔗
    Solution.
    A: \(6\) and \(3\text{.}\) B: \(11\) and \(5.5\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  4. One Route A trip took \(45\) minutes because the matatu broke down. Which of your measures does this one trip affect?
    🔗
    Solution.
    Only the range. Without it, A’s range would be \(10\text{;}\) the quartiles hardly move.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  5. Which route is more reliable? Give a reason.
    🔗
    Solution.
    Route A: its middle half of journeys lies within \(6\) minutes, against \(11\) for B. The range wrongly suggests the routes are alike.
