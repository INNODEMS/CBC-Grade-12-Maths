Exercise 5. Challenge: Design Your Own Problem.

Write a linear programming problem about two products of a business in your area, with two resource constraints and a profit. Solve it and check your answer.
🔗
Hint.
Choose the answer first: pick a vertex such as \((5, 4)\text{,}\) then choose two lines through it.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
Answers vary.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Any problem is acceptable if its model, vertices and best plan are correct and the answer is checked against every constraint. One answer: a roadside kiosk makes \(x\) cups of uji and \(y\) plates of chips a morning; its cooking gas allows \(7x + 2y \le 43\) and the cook’s time allows \(x + 6y \le 29\text{;}\) the profit is \(P = 20x + 25y\) shillings. The vertices are \((0, 0)\text{,}\) \(\left(\tfrac{43}{7}, 0\right)\text{,}\) \((5, 4)\) and \(\left(0, \tfrac{29}{6}\right)\text{,}\) with \(P = 0\text{,}\) about \(123\text{,}\) \(200\) and about \(121\text{,}\) so the best plan is \(5\) cups of uji and \(4\) plates of chips, Ksh \(200\text{.}\)
