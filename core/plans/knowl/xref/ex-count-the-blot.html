Exercise 1.

An ink blot is drawn on 1 cm squares. Count the full squares and the part squares, and estimate the area of the blot.
🔗
Answer.
About \(29\,\text{cm}^2\) (\(17\) full and about \(24\) part squares).
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(F = 17\) and \(P = 24\) (a count within one or two squares of these is fine), so the estimate is \(17 + \tfrac{1}{2} \times 24 = 29\) squares, about \(29\,\text{cm}^2\text{.}\)
