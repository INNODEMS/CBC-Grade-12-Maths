Example 2.2.29 The Coefficient of \(x^4\) in \((3 - x)^6\).

Find the coefficient of \(x^4\) in the expansion of \((3 - x)^6\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

Number 4 of the row for power 6 is \(15\text{.}\) The term is \(15 \times 3^2 \times (-x)^4 = 15 \times 9 \times x^4 = 135x^4\text{,}\) so the coefficient is \(135\text{.}\) An even power of \(-1\) is \(+1\text{.}\)
