Exercise 6. Challenge: A Sixty-Degree Region.

\(AB = 6\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Describe the region on one side of \(AB\) where angle \(APB \ge 60^{\circ}\text{.}\) Is the point \(5\,\text{cm}\) in front of the midpoint \(M\) of \(AB\) in it?
🔗
Hint.
Find the centre and radius of the arc on which angle \(APB = 60^{\circ}\text{,}\) then how far in front of \(M\) the arc reaches.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
The region between \(AB\) and the arc of radius \(3.46\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) arc included (solid). The arc is \(5.20\,\text{cm}\) in front of \(M\text{,}\) so the point is in the region (angle about \(61.9^{\circ}\)).
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The arc has radius \(\dfrac{6}{2\sin 60^{\circ}} \approx 3.46\,\text{cm}\) and its centre \(O\) is on the perpendicular bisector, \(OM = \dfrac{3}{\tan 60^{\circ}} \approx 1.73\,\text{cm}\) from \(M\text{.}\) So the arc reaches \(1.73 + 3.46 \approx 5.20\,\text{cm}\) in front of \(M\text{.}\) Points inside the arc see \(AB\) at more than \(60^{\circ}\text{,}\) and \(\ge\) includes the arc. The point \(5\,\text{cm}\) in front of \(M\) is inside: angle \(2\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{3}{5}\right) \approx 61.9^{\circ}\text{.}\)
