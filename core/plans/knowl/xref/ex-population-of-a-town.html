Exercise 1.

A town in Nakuru County has \(80\,000\) people, and its population grows by \(2\%\) a year. Estimate the population after 5 years using (a) three terms (b) four terms of an expansion.
🔗
Answer.
(a) \(88\,320\) (b) \(88\,326.4\text{,}\) about \(88\,326\) people
🔗
🔗
Solution.
(a) \(80\,000(1 + 0.1 + 0.004) = 88\,320\text{.}\) (b) Adding \(80\,000 \times 10 \times 0.000008 = 6.4\) gives \(88\,326.4\text{,}\) about \(88\,326\) people (the exact value is \(88\,326.46\)).
