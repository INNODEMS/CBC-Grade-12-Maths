Exercise 5.

Group project. Choose a straight edge of the school field. With a tape, measure the width of the field at right angles to that edge every \(5\,\text{m}\text{,}\) including both ends. Estimate the area with both rules, draw the field to scale on squared paper and count squares. Present your three estimates and say which you trust most.
🔗
Answer.
Answers depend on the field. Check: equal spacing, both rules used, a scale drawing, and a sentence comparing the estimates.
