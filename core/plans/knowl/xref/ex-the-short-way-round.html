Exercise 6. Challenge: The Short Way Round.

P is at \((20^\circ\,\text{N},\ 150^\circ\,\text{E})\) and Q is at \((20^\circ\,\text{N},\ 150^\circ\,\text{W})\text{.}\) Find their difference in longitude, and explain why it is not \(300^\circ\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
Go across the \(180^\circ\) meridian.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(60^\circ\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The meridians are on opposite sides of Greenwich, and \(150 + 150 = 300\) is more than \(180\text{.}\) Going the other way, each meridian is \(30^\circ\) from the \(180^\circ\) meridian, so they are \(30^\circ + 30^\circ = 60^\circ\) apart, which is \(360^\circ - 300^\circ\text{.}\) The difference in longitude is the smaller angle, \(60^\circ\text{.}\)
