Example 1.1.9 Factors and Membership.

List \(B = \{\text{factors of } 54\}\text{.}\) Is \(6 \in B\text{?}\) Is \(4 \in B\text{?}\) Find \(n(B)\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

The factors of \(54\) come in pairs: \(1 \times 54\text{,}\) \(2 \times 27\text{,}\) \(3 \times 18\text{,}\) \(6 \times 9\text{.}\) So \(B = \{1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18, 27, 54\}\text{.}\) Then \(6 \in B\text{,}\) \(4 \notin B\) and \(n(B) = 8\text{.}\)
