Exercise 2.

A triangle has vertices \((1, 0)\text{,}\) \((3, 1)\) and \((2, 3)\text{.}\) Find its image under \(\begin{bmatrix} 1 \amp -2 \\ 2 \amp 1 \end{bmatrix}\text{,}\) and draw the triangle and its image on one grid.
🔗
Answer.
\((1, 2)\text{,}\) \((1, 7)\text{,}\) \((-4, 7)\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\begin{bmatrix} 1 \amp -2 \\ 2 \amp 1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 1 \amp 3 \amp 2 \\ 0 \amp 1 \amp 3 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \amp 1 \amp -4 \\ 2 \amp 7 \amp 7 \end{bmatrix}\text{,}\) so the image has vertices \((1, 2)\text{,}\) \((1, 7)\) and \((-4, 7)\text{.}\)
