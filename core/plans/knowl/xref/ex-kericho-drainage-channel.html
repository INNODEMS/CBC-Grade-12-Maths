Exercise 5.

A drainage channel on a tea estate in Kericho is \(10\,\text{m}\) wide. Its depth \(x\) metres from one side is \(d = 0.02x(10 - x)\) metres. Using strips \(2\,\text{m}\) wide, find the mid-ordinate and trapezoidal estimates of the cross-section area, the exact area, and the percentage error of each estimate.
🔗
Answer.
\(M = 3.4\,\text{m}^2\text{,}\) \(T = 3.2\,\text{m}^2\text{,}\) exact \(\frac{10}{3} \approx 3.33\,\text{m}^2\text{;}\) \(2\%\) and \(4\%\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Mid-depths at \(x = 1, 3, 5, 7, 9\text{:}\) \(0.18, 0.42, 0.5, 0.42, 0.18\text{,}\) total \(1.7\text{,}\) so \(M = 3.4\text{.}\) Depths at \(x = 0, 2, \ldots, 10\text{:}\) \(0, 0.32, 0.48, 0.48, 0.32, 0\text{,}\) so \(T = 1 \times 2 \times 1.6 = 3.2\text{.}\) Exact: \(\left[0.1x^2 - \frac{0.02x^3}{3}\right]_0^{10} = 10 - \frac{20}{3} = \frac{10}{3}\text{.}\) Percentage errors: \(\frac{1/15}{10/3} \times 100\% = 2\%\) for \(M\) and \(\frac{2/15}{10/3} \times 100\% = 4\%\) for \(T\text{.}\)
