Example 9.2.7 A Cosine Table.

Make a table of values of \(y = 2.5\cos x\) for \(0^\circ \le x \le 360^\circ\) at steps of \(30^\circ\text{.}\) State the amplitude and the period.
🔗

Solution.

\(x\) \(0^\circ\) \(30^\circ\) \(60^\circ\) \(90^\circ\) \(120^\circ\) \(150^\circ\) \(180^\circ\) \(210^\circ\) \(240^\circ\) \(270^\circ\) \(300^\circ\) \(330^\circ\) \(360^\circ\)
\(2.5\cos x\) \(2.5\) \(2.17\) \(1.25\) \(0\) \(-1.25\) \(-2.17\) \(-2.5\) \(-2.17\) \(-1.25\) \(0\) \(1.25\) \(2.17\) \(2.5\)
🔗
The graph rises to 2.5 and falls to \(-2.5\text{:}\) the amplitude is 2.5. It repeats after \(360^\circ\text{:}\) the period is \(360^\circ\text{.}\)
