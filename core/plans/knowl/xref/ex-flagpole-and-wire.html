Exercise 5.

A flagpole \(QR\) stands vertically on level ground at a school in Nyeri, with \(Q\) at the top. A wire runs from \(Q\) to a peg \(S\) on the ground.
  1. Name the projection of the wire \(QS\) on the ground.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. What is the projection of the pole itself?
    🔗
    🔗
  3. A straight path on the ground does not pass through \(S\) or \(R\text{.}\) Is the wire skew to the path? Explain.
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Answer.
(a) \(RS\) (b) the point \(R\) (c) yes
🔗
🔗
Solution.
(a) \(S\) is on the ground and \(Q\) is directly above \(R\text{,}\) so the projection is \(RS\text{.}\) (b) The pole is perpendicular to the ground, so it projects to its foot, the point \(R\text{.}\) (c) The wire meets the ground only at \(S\text{,}\) which is not on the path, so the wire and the path never meet. The wire rises while the path is level, so they are not parallel. They are skew.
