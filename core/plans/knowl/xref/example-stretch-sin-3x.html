Example 9.4.8 A Stretch Parallel to the \(x\)-Axis.

Describe the transformation that maps \(y = \sin x\) onto \(y = \sin 3x\text{,}\) and find the image of \((90^\circ, 1)\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

A stretch with the \(y\)-axis invariant, stretch factor \(\dfrac{1}{3}\text{.}\) The point \((90^\circ, 1)\) maps to \((30^\circ, 1)\text{.}\) The period becomes \(120^\circ\text{.}\)
