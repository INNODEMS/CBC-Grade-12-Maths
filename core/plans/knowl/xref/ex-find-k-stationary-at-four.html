Exercise 6.

The curve \(y = x^3 + kx^2 + 5\) has a stationary point at \(x = 4\text{.}\) Find \(k\) and the coordinates of both stationary points.
🔗
Answer.
\(k = -6\text{;}\) \((0, 5)\) and \((4, -27)\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 3x^2 + 2kx\text{.}\) At \(x = 4\text{:}\) \(48 + 8k = 0\text{,}\) so \(k = -6\text{.}\) Then \(\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 3x^2 - 12x = 3x(x - 4)\text{,}\) which is \(0\) at \(x = 0\) and \(x = 4\text{;}\) \(y = 5\) and \(y = 64 - 96 + 5 = -27\text{.}\)
