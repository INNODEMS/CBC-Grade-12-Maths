Exercise 5.

In triangle \(ABC\text{,}\) angle \(A = 70^{\circ}\) and angle \(B = 50^{\circ}\text{.}\) The bisectors of angles \(B\) and \(C\) meet at \(I\text{.}\) Find angle \(BIC\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(125^{\circ}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Angle \(C = 180^{\circ} - 70^{\circ} - 50^{\circ} = 60^{\circ}\text{.}\) In triangle \(BIC\text{,}\) angle \(IBC = 25^{\circ}\) and angle \(ICB = 30^{\circ}\text{,}\) so angle \(BIC = 180^{\circ} - 25^{\circ} - 30^{\circ} = 125^{\circ}\text{.}\)
