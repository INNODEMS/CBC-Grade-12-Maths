Exercise 6. Challenge: Round a Triangle.

Find the length of the locus of points outside an equilateral triangle of side \(5\,\text{cm}\) that are \(1\,\text{cm}\) from the nearest side or corner of the triangle. Give your answer correct to 1 decimal place.
🔗
Hint.
What does the locus look like near each corner, and what do the three corner arcs add up to?
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(15 + 2\pi \approx 21.3\,\text{cm}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Along each side the locus is a segment of \(5\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) parallel to the side and \(1\,\text{cm}\) outside it. At each corner the nearest point of the triangle is the corner itself, so the locus is an arc of radius \(1\,\text{cm}\) centred at the corner. Each arc turns through \(360^{\circ} - 90^{\circ} - 90^{\circ} - 60^{\circ} = 120^{\circ}\text{,}\) and the three arcs together make a whole circle. The length is \(3 \times 5 + 2\pi \times 1 = 15 + 2\pi \approx 21.3\,\text{cm}\text{.}\)
