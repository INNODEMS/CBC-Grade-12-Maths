Exercise 8. Challenge: A Letter in the Bracket.

Find \(\displaystyle\int (x + a)^2\,dx\text{,}\) where \(a\) is a constant, and check your answer by differentiating.
🔗
Hint.
Expand the bracket first; treat \(a\) as a number.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(\frac{x^3}{3} + ax^2 + a^2x + c\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\((x + a)^2 = x^2 + 2ax + a^2\text{,}\) which integrates to \(\frac{x^3}{3} + ax^2 + a^2x + c\text{.}\) Check: the derivative is \(x^2 + 2ax + a^2\text{.}\)
