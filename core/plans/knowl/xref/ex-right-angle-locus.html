Exercise 2.

\(AB = 5\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Construct the locus of a point \(P\text{,}\) on either side of \(AB\text{,}\) such that \(\angle APB = 90^{\circ}\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
The circle with diameter \(AB\text{:}\) centre the midpoint of \(AB\text{,}\) radius \(2.5\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) without the points \(A\) and \(B\text{.}\)
