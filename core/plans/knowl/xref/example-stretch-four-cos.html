Example 9.4.7 A Stretch Parallel to the \(y\)-Axis.

Describe the transformation that maps \(y = \cos x\) onto \(y = 4\cos x\text{,}\) and find the image of \((0, 1)\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

A stretch with the \(x\)-axis invariant, stretch factor 4. The point \((0, 1)\) maps to \((0, 4)\text{.}\) The amplitude becomes 4; the period stays \(360^\circ\text{.}\)
