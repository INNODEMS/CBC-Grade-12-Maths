Example 7.2.9 Inside, On or Outside.

The locus of points \(10\) units from \(C(-2, 4)\) is a circle. Decide whether each of \(P(4, 12)\text{,}\) \(Q(6, -1)\) and \(R(-10, -3)\) is inside, on or outside it.
🔗

Solution.

Compare \(\Delta x^2 + \Delta y^2\) with \(r^2 = 100\text{:}\)
\begin{align*} P: \amp\quad 6^2 + 8^2 = 36 + 64 = 100, \amp \text{on the circle}\\ Q: \amp\quad 8^2 + 5^2 = 64 + 25 = 89, \amp \text{inside}\\ R: \amp\quad 8^2 + 7^2 = 64 + 49 = 113, \amp \text{outside} \end{align*}
