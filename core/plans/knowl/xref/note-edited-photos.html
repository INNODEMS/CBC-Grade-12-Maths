Note 5.7.7.

Because an app can stretch, shear or flip a photo in a moment, a picture shared online may not show people or places as they really are. A face can be made thinner, a crowd made to look bigger, or a scene flipped so that it seems to be somewhere else. Before you believe or share an image, check where it came from, and never edit and share a photo of someone else without their permission.
