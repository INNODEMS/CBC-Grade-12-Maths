Example 2.3.8 Estimating \((1.03)^5\).

Use the expansion of \((1 + x)^5\) to estimate \((1.03)^5\text{,}\) correct to 4 decimal places.
🔗

Solution.

\(1.03 = 1 + 0.03\text{,}\) so put \(x = 0.03\) into \(1 + 5x + 10x^2 + 10x^3 + 5x^4 + x^5\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} 1 + 0.15 + 0.009 + 0.00027 + \cdots \end{equation*}
The next term is \(5 \times 0.03^4 = 0.00000405\text{,}\) smaller than \(0.00001\text{,}\) so stop. The sum is \(1.15927\text{,}\) so \((1.03)^5 \approx 1.1593\text{.}\) A calculator gives \(1.1592740743\text{,}\) which also rounds to \(1.1593\text{.}\)
