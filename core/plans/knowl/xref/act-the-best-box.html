Learner Experience 13.3.2 The Best Box.

Squares of side \(x\) cm are cut from the four corners of a square sheet of manila paper \(24\,\text{cm}\) by \(24\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) and the sides are folded up to make an open box (Figure 13.3.3).
🔗
  1. Build your group’s box. Measure it and work out its volume.
    🔗
    Solution.
    For \(x = 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\text{:}\) \(V = 484, 800, 972, 1024, 980, 864\,\text{cm}^3\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Which cut gives the largest volume so far?
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(x = 4\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Show that \(V = x(24 - 2x)^2 = 4x^3 - 96x^2 + 576x\text{.}\) Why must \(0 \lt x \lt 12\text{?}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    The base is \((24 - 2x)\) cm square and the height is \(x\) cm. The cut must be positive, and two cuts must fit along an edge of \(24\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) so \(x \lt 12\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  4. \(\dfrac{dV}{dx} = 12(x - 4)(x - 12)\text{.}\) Which solution of \(\dfrac{dV}{dx} = 0\) gives a box?
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(x = 4\text{.}\) At \(x = 12\) the base has side \(0\text{:}\) there is no box.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  5. Use \(\dfrac{d^2V}{dx^2}\) to show that \(x = 4\) gives the greatest volume. Give the dimensions and volume of the best box.
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(\dfrac{d^2V}{dx^2} = 24x - 192 = -96 \lt 0\) at \(x = 4\text{.}\) The best box is \(16\,\text{cm}\) by \(16\,\text{cm}\) by \(4\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) with volume \(1024\,\text{cm}^3\text{.}\)
