Exercise 1.

For \(y = x^2\) from \(x = 0\) to \(x = 3\text{,}\) find the trapezoidal estimate with \(3\) strips, the exact area, the absolute error and the percentage error.
🔗
Answer.
\(T = 9.5\text{;}\) exact \(9\text{;}\) absolute error \(0.5\text{;}\) \(5.56\%\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Ordinates \(0, 1, 4, 9\text{:}\) \(T = \tfrac{1}{2}(0 + 9 + 2 \times 5) = 9.5\text{.}\) Exact \(\left[\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^3 = 9\text{.}\) Absolute error \(0.5\text{;}\) \(\frac{0.5}{9} \times 100\% \approx 5.56\%\text{.}\)
