Exercise 6.

\((a + bx)^3 = 8 + 36x + cx^2 + dx^3\text{,}\) where \(a\) and \(b\) are positive. Find \(a\text{,}\) \(b\text{,}\) \(c\) and \(d\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(a = 2\text{,}\) \(b = 3\text{,}\) \(c = 54\text{,}\) \(d = 27\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The constant term is \(a^3 = 8\text{,}\) so \(a = 2\text{.}\) The term in \(x\) is \(3a^2bx = 12bx\text{,}\) so \(12b = 36\) and \(b = 3\text{.}\) Then \(c = 3ab^2 = 54\) and \(d = b^3 = 27\text{.}\)
