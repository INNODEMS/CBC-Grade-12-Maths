Exercise 4.

List the whole-number points of the region \(x \ge 1\text{,}\) \(y \ge 1\text{,}\) \(2x + 7y \le 30\text{,}\) \(y \lt 3\text{.}\) How many are there?
🔗
Answer.
\(19\) points: \((1, 1)\) to \((11, 1)\) and \((1, 2)\) to \((8, 2)\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(y \lt 3\) leaves \(y = 1\) or \(y = 2\text{.}\) With \(y = 1\text{:}\) \(2x \le 23\text{,}\) so \(x = 1\) to \(11\text{:}\) \(11\) points. With \(y = 2\text{:}\) \(2x \le 16\text{,}\) so \(x = 1\) to \(8\text{:}\) \(8\) points. Altogether \(19\) points.
