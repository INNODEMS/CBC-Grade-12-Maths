Exercise 1.

A boat moves at \(3.4\,\text{m/s}\) in still water. The current is \(1.6\,\text{m/s}\text{.}\) At what angle upstream must the boat be steered to go straight across, and what is its ground speed?
🔗
Answer.
\(28.1^\circ\text{;}\) \(3.0\,\text{m/s}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\sin d = 1.6 \div 3.4\text{,}\) \(d = 28.1^\circ\text{;}\) \(\sqrt{3.4^2 - 1.6^2} = \sqrt{9} = 3.0\,\text{m/s}\text{.}\)
