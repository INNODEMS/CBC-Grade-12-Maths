Exercise 2.

An aircraft flies on a course of \(180^\circ\) with an airspeed of \(210\,\text{knots}\text{.}\) A wind of \(36\,\text{knots}\) blows from \(090^\circ\text{.}\) Find the ground speed, the drift angle and the track.
🔗
Answer.
\(213.1\,\text{knots}\text{;}\) \(9.7^\circ\text{;}\) \(189.7^\circ\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The wind blows towards \(270^\circ\text{,}\) at right angles to the course. \(\sqrt{210^2 + 36^2} = 213.1\,\text{knots}\text{;}\) \(\tan d = 36 \div 210\text{,}\) \(d = 9.7^\circ\text{.}\) Pushed west of south, so the track is \(180^\circ + 9.7^\circ = 189.7^\circ\text{.}\)
