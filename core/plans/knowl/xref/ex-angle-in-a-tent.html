Exercise 2.

For the tent in Figure 8.1.14 (\(AB = 2.4\,\text{m}\text{,}\) \(CA = CB = 1.5\,\text{m}\text{,}\) \(AD = 3\,\text{m}\)), find angle \(DAF\text{,}\) between the bottom edge \(AD\) and the line \(AF\) from a front corner to the back of the ridge.
🔗
Answer.
\(26.6^{\circ}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(AD\) is at right angles to the back end \(DEF\text{,}\) which contains \(DF\text{,}\) so angle \(ADF = 90^{\circ}\text{.}\) \(DF = 1.5\,\text{m}\) and \(AD = 3\,\text{m}\text{,}\) so \(\tan DAF = \dfrac{1.5}{3}\) and angle \(DAF \approx 26.6^{\circ}\text{.}\)
