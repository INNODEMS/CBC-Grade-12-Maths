Example 10.2.12 The Vector Between Two Points.

\(A\) is \((2, 5, -1)\) and \(B\) is \((6, 3, 4)\text{.}\) Find \(\overrightarrow{AB}\) and \(\overrightarrow{BA}\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

End minus start:
\begin{equation*} \overrightarrow{AB} = \mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a} = \begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ 3 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} - \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 5 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ -2 \\ 5 \end{pmatrix} \end{equation*}
and \(\overrightarrow{BA} = -\overrightarrow{AB} = \begin{pmatrix} -4 \\ 2 \\ -5 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\)
