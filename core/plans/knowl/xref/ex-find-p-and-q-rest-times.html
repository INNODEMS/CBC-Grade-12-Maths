Exercise 6.

A particle moves so that \(s = t^3 + pt^2 + qt\) metres after \(t\) seconds. It is at rest at \(t = 1\) and \(t = 7\text{.}\) Find \(p\) and \(q\text{,}\) and the acceleration at \(t = 4\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(p = -12\text{,}\) \(q = 21\text{;}\) \(a(4) = 0\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(v = 3t^2 + 2pt + q\) must be \(3(t - 1)(t - 7) = 3t^2 - 24t + 21\text{.}\) Then \(a = 6t - 24\text{,}\) which is \(0\) at \(t = 4\text{.}\)
