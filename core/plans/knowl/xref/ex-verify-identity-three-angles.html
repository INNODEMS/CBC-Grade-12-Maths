Exercise 1.

Verify that \(\sin^2 x + \cos^2 x = 1\) for \(x = 40^\circ\text{,}\) \(125^\circ\) and \(300^\circ\text{.}\) Give each ratio to 4 decimal places.
🔗
Answer.
\(40^\circ\text{:}\) \(0.6428\text{,}\) \(0.7660\text{;}\) \(125^\circ\text{:}\) \(0.8192\text{,}\) \(-0.5736\text{;}\) \(300^\circ\text{:}\) \(-0.8660\text{,}\) \(0.5000\text{.}\) Each sum of squares is \(1.0000\) to 4 decimal places.
