Exercise 5.

\(A(1, 2, 3)\text{,}\) \(B(3, 3, 1)\) and \(C(9, m, n)\) are collinear. Find \(m\) and \(n\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(m = 6\text{,}\) \(n = -5\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\overrightarrow{AB} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}\) and \(\overrightarrow{AC} = \begin{pmatrix} 8 \\ m - 2 \\ n - 3 \end{pmatrix} = k\overrightarrow{AB}\text{.}\) The first components give \(k = 4\text{,}\) so \(m - 2 = 4\) and \(n - 3 = -8\text{:}\) \(m = 6\text{,}\) \(n = -5\text{.}\)
