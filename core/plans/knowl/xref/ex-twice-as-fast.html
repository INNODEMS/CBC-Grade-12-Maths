Exercise 6. Challenge: Twice as Fast.

Two workers together take \(6\) days to do a job. One works twice as fast as the other. How long would each take alone?
🔗
Hint.
If the slower worker does \(r\) of the job a day, the faster does \(2r\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(9\) days and \(18\) days
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(r + 2r = \tfrac{1}{6}\text{,}\) so \(r = \tfrac{1}{18}\text{.}\) The slower takes \(18\) days and the faster \(9\) days.
