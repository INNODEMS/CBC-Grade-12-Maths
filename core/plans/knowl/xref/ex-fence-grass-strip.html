Exercise 5.

At a school, the grass is cut within \(1.5\,\text{m}\) of a straight fence \(30\,\text{m}\) long, on both sides and round its ends. Find the area cut, correct to 1 decimal place.
🔗
Answer.
\(97.1\,\text{m}^2\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
A rectangle \(30\,\text{m}\) by \(3\,\text{m}\) and two half-discs of radius \(1.5\,\text{m}\text{:}\) \(90 + \pi \times 1.5^2 = 90 + 2.25\pi \approx 97.1\,\text{m}^2\text{.}\)
