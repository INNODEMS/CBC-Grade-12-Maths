Exercise 2.

Find \(P_{15}\) and \(P_{85}\) for the goat masses.
🔗
Answer.
\(P_{15} \approx 22.7\,\text{kg}\text{,}\) \(P_{85} \approx 38.1\,\text{kg}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Positions \(7.5\) and \(42.5\text{.}\) \(P_{15} = 19.5 + \dfrac{7.5 - 3}{7} \times 5 = 22.71\text{;}\) \(P_{85} = 34.5 + \dfrac{42.5 - 36}{9} \times 5 = 38.11\text{.}\)
