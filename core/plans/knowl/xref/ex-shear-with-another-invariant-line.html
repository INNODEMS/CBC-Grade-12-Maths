Exercise 6. Challenge: A Shear With Another Invariant Line (Extension).

\(M = \begin{bmatrix} -3 \amp 16 \\ -1 \amp 5 \end{bmatrix}\) is the matrix of a shear whose invariant line is not an axis.
  1. Find \(\det M\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Show that \((4, 1)\) is fixed by \(M\text{,}\) and that every point on the line \(x = 4y\) is fixed.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Find the image of \((0, 1)\) and show that it moved parallel to the line \(x = 4y\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Hint.
A fixed point satisfies \(M\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
(a) \(1\) (b) \((4, 1) \to (4, 1)\) (c) \((16, 5)\text{,}\) which moved by \(\begin{pmatrix} 16 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} = 4\begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
(a) \(\det M = (-3)(5) - (16)(-1) = -15 + 16 = 1\text{,}\) so areas are unchanged.
🔗
(b) \(M\begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -12 + 16 \\ -4 + 5 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\) A point on \(x = 4y\) is \((4t, t)\text{,}\) and \(M\begin{pmatrix} 4t \\ t \end{pmatrix} = t\,M\begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 4t \\ t \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\)
🔗
(c) \(M\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 16 \\ 5 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\) The point moved by \(\begin{pmatrix} 16 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}\text{,}\) which is \(4\begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}\text{,}\) a vector along the line \(x = 4y\text{.}\)
