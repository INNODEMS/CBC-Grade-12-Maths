Exercise 6. Challenge: A Corner Cut From a Cube.

A cube \(ABCDEFGH\) has edges of \(6\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Find the angle between the plane \(BDG\) and the base \(ABCD\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
The common line is \(BD\text{.}\) The line from \(C\) at right angles to \(BD\) meets it at the centre \(X\) of the base.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(54.7^{\circ}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
At \(X\text{,}\) \(XC\) lies in the base and is at right angles to \(BD\) (the diagonals of a square cross at right angles). Triangle \(BGD\) is equilateral, so \(XG\text{,}\) from \(G\) to the midpoint of \(BD\text{,}\) is also at right angles to \(BD\text{.}\) The angle is angle \(GXC\text{,}\) with the right angle at \(C\text{.}\) \(XC = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{72} = 3\sqrt{2}\text{,}\) so \(\tan GXC = \dfrac{6}{3\sqrt{2}}\) and the angle is about \(54.7^{\circ}\text{.}\)
