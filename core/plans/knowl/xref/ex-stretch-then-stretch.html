Exercise 6. Challenge: Stretch Then Stretch.

  1. A stretch with factor \(2\) and the \(x\)-axis invariant is followed by a stretch with factor \(2\) and the \(y\)-axis invariant. Which single transformation has the same effect?
    🔗
    🔗
  2. A stretch with factor \(4\) and the \(x\)-axis invariant is followed by a stretch with factor \(\frac{1}{4}\) and the \(x\)-axis invariant. What is the single transformation?
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Hint.
Multiply the matrices, second times first.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
(a) The enlargement, centre \(O\text{,}\) scale factor \(2\text{.}\) (b) The identity: every point ends where it started.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
(a) \(\begin{bmatrix} 2 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp 1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 1 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp 2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 2 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp 2 \end{bmatrix}\text{,}\) the enlargement, centre \(O\text{,}\) scale factor \(2\text{.}\)
🔗
(b) \(\begin{bmatrix} 1 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp \frac{1}{4} \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 1 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp 4 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp 1 \end{bmatrix}\text{,}\) the identity. The second stretch is the inverse of the first.
