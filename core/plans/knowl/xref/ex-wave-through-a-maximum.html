Exercise 8. Challenge: A Wave Through a Maximum.

The wave \(y = a\sin bx\) has greatest value 2.5, and its first maximum after \(x = 0\) is at \(x = 30^\circ\text{.}\) Find \(a\text{,}\) \(b\) and the period.
🔗
Hint.
The first maximum of \(y = \sin bx\) is where \(bx = 90^\circ\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(a = 2.5\text{,}\) \(b = 3\text{,}\) period \(120^\circ\text{.}\)
