Example 3.4.25 Tea Grades.

A tea packer mixes grade PF1 tea at Ksh \(520\) a kilogram with grade BP1 at Ksh \(680\) a kilogram, so that the blend costs Ksh \(590\) a kilogram. In what ratio are they mixed?
🔗

Solution.

PF1 is \(590 - 520 = 70\) below the target and BP1 is \(680 - 590 = 90\) above it. Crossing over:
🔗
PF1 : BP1 \(= 90 : 70 = 9 : 7\text{.}\)
🔗
Check: \((9 \times 520 + 7 \times 680) \div 16 = (4680 + 4760) \div 16 = 590\text{.}\)
