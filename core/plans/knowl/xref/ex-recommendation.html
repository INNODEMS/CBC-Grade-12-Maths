Exercise 4.

Use Example 12.4.55 to write a two-sentence recommendation for a farmer choosing between the methods of Farm P and Farm Q.
🔗
Answer.
Answers vary; a good answer names a centre and a spread, for example: “Farm Q’s methods give a higher median yield, but Farm P’s yields vary less. If you cannot afford a poor season, use Farm P’s methods.”
