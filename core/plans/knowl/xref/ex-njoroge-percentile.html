Exercise 5.

Njoroge found \(P_{15}\) of the goat masses as \(26.6\,\text{kg}\text{.}\) What was his mistake?
🔗
Answer.
He used position \(15\) instead of \(15\%\) of \(50\text{,}\) which is \(7.5\text{.}\) The correct value is \(22.7\,\text{kg}\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Position \(15\) gives \(24.5 + \dfrac{15 - 10}{12} \times 5 = 26.6\text{,}\) which is \(D_3\text{.}\) \(P_{15}\) is at \(15 \times 50 \div 100 = 7.5\text{,}\) giving \(22.7\,\text{kg}\text{.}\)
