Exercise 9. Challenge: An Unknown Coefficient.

Given that \(\displaystyle\int_{1}^{3} (ax + 2)\,dx = 20\text{,}\) find the constant \(a\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
The integral works out to \(4a + 4\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(a = 4\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\left[ \frac{ax^2}{2} + 2x \right]_{1}^{3} = \left(\frac{9a}{2} + 6\right) - \left(\frac{a}{2} + 2\right) = 4a + 4\text{.}\) So \(4a + 4 = 20\) and \(a = 4\text{.}\)
