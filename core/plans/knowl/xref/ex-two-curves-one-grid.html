Exercise 6. Challenge: Two Curves on One Grid.

The heights, in cm (to the nearest cm), of \(30\) boys and \(30\) girls in a Grade 12 class are shown below. Draw both cumulative frequency curves on one grid and compare the two groups.
🔗
Height (cm) 150–154 155–159 160–164 165–169 170–174 175–179
Boys 2 5 9 8 4 2
Girls 4 8 10 5 2 1
Hint.
Read both curves at \(164.5\,\text{cm}\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
Boys’ cf \(2, 7, 16, 24, 28, 30\text{;}\) girls’ cf \(4, 12, 22, 27, 29, 30\text{.}\) The girls’ curve lies to the left, so the girls are generally shorter: \(22\) girls but only \(16\) boys are shorter than \(164.5\,\text{cm}\text{.}\)
