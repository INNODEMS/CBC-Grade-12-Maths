Exercise 5.

A map of a county is 12 cm wide and 15 cm tall on a phone screen. Amani stretches it to 1.5 times its width, keeping its height. Find the new width, and the areas of the map before and after the stretch.
🔗
Answer.
\(18\,\text{cm}\text{;}\) \(180\,\text{cm}^2\) and \(270\,\text{cm}^2\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Put the left edge of the map on the \(y\)-axis: this is a stretch with stretch factor \(1.5\) and the \(y\)-axis invariant. New width \(1.5 \times 12 = 18\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Areas \(12 \times 15 = 180\,\text{cm}^2\) and \(18 \times 15 = 270\,\text{cm}^2 = 1.5 \times 180\,\text{cm}^2\text{.}\)
