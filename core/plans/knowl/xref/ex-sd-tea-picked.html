Exercise 1.

The mass of tea, \(m\) kg, picked in a day by each of \(40\) pickers on an estate in Kericho is shown below. Estimate the mean and standard deviation.
🔗
Mass (kg) \(0 \le m \lt 10\) \(10 \le m \lt 20\) \(20 \le m \lt 30\) \(30 \le m \lt 40\) \(40 \le m \lt 50\)
Frequency (\(f\)) 4 10 15 8 3
Answer.
\(\bar{x} = 24\,\text{kg}\text{,}\) \(\sigma \approx 10.68\,\text{kg}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Midpoints \(5, 15, 25, 35, 45\text{;}\) \(\Sigma fx = 960\text{,}\) \(\Sigma fx^2 = 27\,600\text{.}\) \(\bar{x} = 960 \div 40 = 24\text{;}\) \(\sigma^2 = 690 - 576 = 114\text{;}\) \(\sigma \approx 10.68\,\text{kg}\text{.}\)
