Exercise 5. Challenge: A Current Too Strong.

A fisherman’s dugout moves at \(1.1\,\text{m/s}\) in still water. After heavy rain the river flows at \(1.3\,\text{m/s}\text{.}\) Explain why the dugout cannot reach the point straight opposite.
🔗
Hint.
Work out \(\sin d\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(\sin d = 1.3 \div 1.1 = 1.18\text{,}\) which is impossible.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
To go straight across, \(\sin d = 1.3 \div 1.1 = 1.18\) (to 2 d.p.). The sine of an angle is never more than \(1\text{,}\) so no course works: the current is faster than the dugout, which is always carried downstream.
