Exercise 6. Challenge: Adding Two Waves.

Make a table of values of \(y = 2\sin x + \cos x\) for \(0^\circ \le x \le 360^\circ\) at steps of \(45^\circ\) (2 decimal places) and plot it. What kind of graph is it? Use your plot to estimate its greatest value and where it occurs.
🔗
Hint.
Plot the points and look at the shape.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(1, 2.12, 2, 0.71, -1, -2.12, -2, -0.71, 1\text{.}\) The graph is a single wave, like a sine wave. Its greatest value is about \(2.2\text{,}\) at about \(x = 63^\circ\) (exactly \(\sqrt{5} \approx 2.24\) at \(63.4^\circ\)).
