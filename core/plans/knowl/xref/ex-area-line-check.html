Exercise 4.

Find the area between \(y = 8 - 2x\) and the axes, from \(x = 0\) to \(x = 4\text{,}\) by integration. Check your answer with the area of a triangle.
🔗
Answer.
\(16\) square units; \(\frac{1}{2} \times 4 \times 8 = 16\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\left[ 8x - x^2 \right]_{0}^{4} = 32 - 16 = 16\text{.}\) The region is a triangle with base \(4\) and height \(8\text{.}\)
