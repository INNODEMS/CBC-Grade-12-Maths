Exercise 7. Challenge: Every Cubic Crosses.

Explain why every curve \(y = x^3 + bx^2 + cx + d\) crosses the \(x\)-axis at least once.
🔗
Hint.
Think about large positive and large negative \(x\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
For large negative \(x\text{,}\) \(y\) is large and negative; for large positive \(x\text{,}\) \(y\) is large and positive. The curve has no breaks, so it must cross \(y = 0\) somewhere in between.
