Exercise 7. Challenge: A Spider on a Box.

A closed box is \(30\,\text{cm}\) by \(20\,\text{cm}\) by \(10\,\text{cm}\text{.}\)
  1. Find the length of its space diagonal.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. A spider at one corner crawls over the outside of the box to the opposite corner. Find the length of the shortest crawl.
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Hint.
Unfold two faces flat, so that the crawl becomes a straight line. There are three ways to choose the two faces.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
(a) \(\sqrt{1400} \approx 37.42\,\text{cm}\) (b) \(\sqrt{1800} \approx 42.43\,\text{cm}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
(a) \(\sqrt{30^2 + 20^2 + 10^2} = \sqrt{1400} \approx 37.42\,\text{cm}\text{.}\)
🔗
(b) Unfolded, the two faces make a rectangle, and the crawl is its diagonal. The three unfoldings give rectangles \(30\) by \(20 + 10\text{,}\) \(20\) by \(30 + 10\) and \(10\) by \(30 + 20\text{,}\) with diagonals \(\sqrt{1800} \approx 42.43\text{,}\) \(\sqrt{2000} \approx 44.72\) and \(\sqrt{2600} \approx 50.99\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) The shortest crawl is \(42.43\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) longer than the space diagonal, as it must be.
