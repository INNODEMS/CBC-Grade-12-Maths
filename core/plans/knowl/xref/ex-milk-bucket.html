Exercise 4. Extension: A Milk Bucket.

An open cylindrical milk bucket must hold \(1000\pi\,\text{cm}^3\text{.}\) Find the radius and height that use the least metal.
🔗
Answer.
\(r = 10\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) \(h = 10\,\text{cm}\text{;}\) \(300\pi \approx 942.5\,\text{cm}^2\) of metal
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\pi r^2 h = 1000\pi\) gives \(h = \dfrac{1000}{r^2}\text{,}\) so \(A = \pi r^2 + 2\pi r h = \pi r^2 + \dfrac{2000\pi}{r}\text{.}\) \(\dfrac{dA}{dr} = 2\pi r - \dfrac{2000\pi}{r^2} = 0\) at \(r^3 = 1000\text{.}\)
