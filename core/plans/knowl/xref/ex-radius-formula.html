Exercise 6. Challenge: The Radius Formula.

Show that the radius of the constant angle arc for an angle \(\theta \lt 90^{\circ}\) is \(\dfrac{AB}{2\sin\theta}\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
Use triangle \(OMA\text{,}\) where \(M\) is the midpoint of \(AB\text{,}\) and find angle \(AOM\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(OA = \dfrac{AM}{\sin\theta} = \dfrac{AB}{2\sin\theta}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\angle AOB = 2\theta\) and \(OM\) bisects it (triangle \(OAB\) is isosceles), so \(\angle AOM = \theta\text{.}\) In the right-angled triangle \(OMA\text{,}\) \(\sin\theta = \dfrac{AM}{OA}\) with \(AM = \dfrac{AB}{2}\text{,}\) so \(OA = \dfrac{AB}{2\sin\theta}\text{.}\)
