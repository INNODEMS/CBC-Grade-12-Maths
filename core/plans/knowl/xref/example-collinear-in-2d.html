Example 10.6.9 Three Points in a Plane.

Show that \(A(-2, 1)\text{,}\) \(B(1, 3)\) and \(C(7, 7)\) are collinear, and find the ratio \(AB : BC\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

\begin{align*} \overrightarrow{AB} \amp = \begin{pmatrix} 1 - (-2) \\ 3 - 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix}\\ \overrightarrow{BC} \amp = \begin{pmatrix} 7 - 1 \\ 7 - 3 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} = 2\begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} = 2\overrightarrow{AB} \end{align*}
\(\overrightarrow{BC}\) is parallel to \(\overrightarrow{AB}\text{,}\) and the two vectors share the point \(B\text{,}\) so \(A\text{,}\) \(B\) and \(C\) are collinear. Since \(\overrightarrow{BC} = 2\overrightarrow{AB}\text{,}\) \(AB : BC = 1 : 2\text{.}\)
