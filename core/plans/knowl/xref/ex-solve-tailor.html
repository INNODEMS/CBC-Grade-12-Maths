Exercise 3.

Use the five steps to solve the tailor’s problem of Learner Experience 4.1.21 from the start. Set out every step.
🔗
Answer.
\(28\) shirts and \(6\) dresses, Ksh \(13\,200\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Model: \(3x + 7y \le 126\text{,}\) \(x + 6y \le 64\text{,}\) \(x, y \ge 0\text{,}\) whole; \(P = 300x + 800y\text{.}\) Vertices (Example 4.2.32): \((0, 0)\text{,}\) \((42, 0)\text{,}\) \((28, 6)\text{,}\) \((0, 10.67)\text{.}\) Test: \(0\text{,}\) \(12\,600\text{,}\) \(13\,200\text{,}\) \(8533.33\text{.}\) Answer: \(28\) shirts and \(6\) dresses a week, Ksh \(13\,200\text{.}\) Check: cloth \(84 + 42 = 126\text{,}\) machine \(28 + 36 = 64\text{.}\)
