Example 14.5.10 Predicting the Error.

The trapezoidal estimate of \(\displaystyle\int_0^2 x^3\,dx\) with \(2\) strips is \(T_2 = 5\text{.}\) Predict \(T_4\) and \(T_8\) without calculating them.
🔗

Solution.

The exact value is \(4\text{,}\) so the error of \(T_2\) is \(5 - 4 = 1\text{.}\) Doubling the strips divides the error by \(4\text{:}\) the errors of \(T_4\) and \(T_8\) are \(0.25\) and \(0.0625\text{,}\) and they stay positive. So \(T_4 = 4.25\) and \(T_8 = 4.0625\text{,}\) the values found in Learner Experience 14.5.2.
