Example 7.5.10 A Right Angle at \(P\).

\(AB = 9\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Describe the locus of a point \(P\) on one side of \(AB\) such that \(\angle APB = 90^{\circ}\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

The angle in a semicircle is \(90^{\circ}\text{,}\) so \(P\) lies on the semicircle with diameter \(AB\text{:}\) centre the midpoint \(M\) of \(AB\text{,}\) radius \(4.5\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) not including \(A\) and \(B\) (panel (a) of Figure 7.5.12). The construction rule agrees: the line at \(A\) makes \(90^{\circ} - 90^{\circ} = 0^{\circ}\) with \(AB\text{,}\) so it meets the bisector at \(M\) itself.
