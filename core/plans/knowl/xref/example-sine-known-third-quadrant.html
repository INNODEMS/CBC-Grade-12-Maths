Example 9.1.28 Sine Known, Third Quadrant.

Given that \(\sin x = -\dfrac{5}{13}\) and \(180^\circ \lt x \lt 270^\circ\text{,}\) find the exact values of \(\cos x\) and \(\tan x\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

\(\cos^2 x = 1 - \dfrac{25}{169} = \dfrac{144}{169}\text{,}\) so \(\cos x = \pm\dfrac{12}{13}\text{.}\) In the third quadrant cosine is negative: \(\cos x = -\dfrac{12}{13}\text{.}\)
\begin{equation*} \tan x = \frac{-5/13}{-12/13} = \frac{5}{12} \end{equation*}
Tangent is positive in the third quadrant, as it should be.
