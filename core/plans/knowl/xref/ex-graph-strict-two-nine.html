Exercise 2.

Graph \(2x + 9y \gt 18\text{,}\) shading the unwanted side.
🔗
Answer.
A dashed line through \((9, 0)\) and \((0, 2)\text{,}\) shaded on the side containing the origin
🔗
🔗
Solution.
With \(y = 0\text{,}\) \(x = 9\text{;}\) with \(x = 0\text{,}\) \(y = 2\text{.}\) The line is dashed (\(\gt\)). \((0, 0)\) gives \(0 \gt 18\text{,}\) false, so the origin is on the unwanted side: shade the side containing the origin.
