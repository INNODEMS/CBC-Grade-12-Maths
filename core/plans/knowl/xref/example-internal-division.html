Example 10.7.8 Internal Division.

\(A\) is the point \((3, -2, 5)\) and \(B\) is \((9, 7, -1)\text{.}\) Find the point \(P\) that divides \(AB\) internally in the ratio \(1 : 2\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

Step 1: \(\overrightarrow{AB} = \begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ 9 \\ -6 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\)
🔗
Step 2: \(AP\) is \(1\) part out of \(1 + 2 = 3\text{,}\) so \(\overrightarrow{AP} = \dfrac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AB} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 3 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\)
🔗
Step 3: \(\overrightarrow{OP} = \overrightarrow{OA} + \overrightarrow{AP} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ -2 \\ 5 \end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 3 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 1 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}\text{,}\) so \(P\) is \((5, 1, 3)\text{.}\)
