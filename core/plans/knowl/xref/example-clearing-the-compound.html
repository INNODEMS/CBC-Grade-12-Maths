Example 3.2.8 Clearing the Compound.

\(12\) learners clear the school compound in \(5\) hours. How long would \(20\) learners take, working at the same rate?
🔗

Solution.

More learners take less time: inverse proportion.
🔗
\(12\) learners take \(5\) hours.
🔗
1 learner would take \(12 \times 5 = 60\) hours: the job is \(60\) learner-hours.
🔗
\(20\) learners take \(60 \div 20 = 3\) hours.
🔗
