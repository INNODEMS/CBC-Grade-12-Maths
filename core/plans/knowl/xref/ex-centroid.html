Exercise 6. Challenge: The Centroid.

In triangle \(ABC\text{,}\) \(M\) is the midpoint of \(BC\text{.}\) Show that the point \(G\) dividing \(AM\) internally in the ratio \(2 : 1\) has position vector \(\frac{1}{3}(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c})\text{.}\) Explain why the three medians of the triangle meet at \(G\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
The answer is symmetric in \(\mathbf{a}\text{,}\) \(\mathbf{b}\) and \(\mathbf{c}\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(\mathbf{g} = \dfrac{\mathbf{a} + 2\mathbf{m}}{3}\) with \(\mathbf{m} = \frac{1}{2}(\mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c})\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
By the ratio theorem, \(\mathbf{g} = \dfrac{1\mathbf{a} + 2\mathbf{m}}{3} = \dfrac{\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c}}{3}\text{.}\) The same working for the median from \(B\) (to the midpoint of \(CA\)) or from \(C\) gives the same expression, because it does not change when \(\mathbf{a}\text{,}\) \(\mathbf{b}\) and \(\mathbf{c}\) are swapped. So the point two thirds of the way along each median is the same point \(G\text{,}\) and all three medians pass through it.
