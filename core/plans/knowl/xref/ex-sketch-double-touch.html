Exercise 6. Challenge: Two Squared Factors.

Sketch \(y = x^2(x + 2)^2\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
Both factors are squared.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
Touches the \(x\)-axis at \(x = -2\) and \(x = 0\text{;}\) minimum points \((-2, 0)\) and \((0, 0)\text{;}\) maximum point \((-1, 1)\text{;}\) rises at both ends.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(y = x^4 + 4x^3 + 4x^2\text{,}\) so \(\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 4x^3 + 12x^2 + 8x = 4x(x + 1)(x + 2)\text{,}\) zero at \(x = -2, -1, 0\text{.}\)
