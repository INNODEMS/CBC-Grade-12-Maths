Exercise 4. Challenge: The Ship’s Clock.

A ship sails due west along the equator, covering \(300\,\text{nm}\) each day. Its clock is set to local time once a day. By how much is the clock changed each day, and is it put forward or back?
🔗
Hint.
How many degrees of longitude is \(300\,\text{nm}\) along the equator?
🔗
🔗
Answer.
Back \(20\) minutes each day
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(300\,\text{nm} = 300' = 5^\circ\) a day. \(5 \times 4 = 20\) minutes. The ship moves west, where local time is behind, so the clock is put back \(20\) minutes each day.
