Exercise 3.

The tailor’s profits change to Ksh \(500\) a shirt and Ksh \(300\) a dress, so \(P = 500x + 300y\) on the same region (Example 4.3.30). Use a search line to find the new best plan.
🔗
Answer.
\(42\) shirts and no dresses, Ksh \(21\,000\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The search lines have gradient \(-\tfrac{5}{3}\text{,}\) steeper than the cloth edge (gradient \(-\tfrac{3}{7}\)). Draw \(500x + 300y = 1500\) through \((3, 0)\) and \((0, 5)\) and slide it away from the origin. The last point of R it touches is \((42, 0)\text{,}\) where the cloth edge meets the \(x\)-axis. \(P = 21\,000\) there, compared with \(15\,800\) at \((28, 6)\text{:}\) \(42\) shirts and no dresses.
