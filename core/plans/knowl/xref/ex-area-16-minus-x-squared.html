Exercise 1.

Find the area enclosed by the curve \(y = 16 - x^2\) and the \(x\)-axis.
🔗
Answer.
\(\frac{256}{3} \approx 85.33\) square units
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The curve crosses the axis at \(x = \pm 4\text{.}\) \(\left[ 16x - \frac{x^3}{3} \right]_{-4}^{4} = \frac{128}{3} - \left(-\frac{128}{3}\right) = \frac{256}{3}\text{.}\)
