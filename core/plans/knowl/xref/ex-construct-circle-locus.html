Exercise 1.

Mark a point \(O\text{.}\) Construct the locus of points \(3.5\,\text{cm}\) from \(O\text{,}\) and show the points less than \(3.5\,\text{cm}\) from \(O\) by shading the points that are not. Should the boundary be solid or dashed?
🔗
Answer.
A circle, centre \(O\text{,}\) radius \(3.5\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) with the outside shaded, the inside left clear and the circle dashed (points exactly \(3.5\,\text{cm}\) away are not included).
