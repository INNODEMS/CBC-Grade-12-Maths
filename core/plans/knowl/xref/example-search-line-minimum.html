Example 4.3.31 Search Line Towards the Origin.

Use a search line to find the cheapest fertiliser plan of Example 4.3.9: minimise \(C = 4000x + 7000y\) subject to \(x + y \ge 7\text{,}\) \(x + 6y \ge 17\text{,}\) \(x \ge 0\text{,}\) \(y \ge 0\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

Draw the line of equal cost \(4000x + 7000y = 56\,000\) through \((14, 0)\) and \((0, 8)\text{;}\) it crosses R. For a minimum, slide it towards the origin. The last point of R it touches is the vertex \((5, 2)\text{,}\) where \(x + y = 7\) meets \(x + 6y = 17\text{.}\)
🔗
Check: the search line’s gradient \(-\tfrac{4}{7} \approx -0.57\) lies between \(-1\) (for \(x + y = 7\)) and \(-\tfrac{1}{6}\) (for \(x + 6y = 17\)). The minimum cost is \(C = 20\,000 + 14\,000 = 34\,000\text{:}\) \(5\) bags of A and \(2\) of B, Ksh \(34\,000\text{.}\)
