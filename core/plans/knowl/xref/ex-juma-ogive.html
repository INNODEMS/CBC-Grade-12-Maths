Exercise 5.

Juma drew a cumulative frequency curve for the goat masses in Example 12.2.9, but he plotted the cumulative frequencies \(3, 10, 22, 36, 45, 50\) at \(17, 22, 27, 32, 37, 42\text{.}\) What did he do wrong, and how does it affect a median read from his graph?
🔗
Answer.
He plotted at the class midpoints. His curve is half a class (\(2.5\,\text{kg}\)) too far left, so his median is about \(2.5\,\text{kg}\) too small.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(17, 22, \ldots\) are the midpoints of the classes, not the upper class boundaries \(19.5, 24.5, \ldots\text{.}\) Every point is \(2.5\,\text{kg}\) to the left of where it should be, so any value read across from the cumulative frequency axis, including the median, comes out about \(2.5\,\text{kg}\) too small.
