Example 2.2.28 The Coefficient of \(x^3\) in \((1 + 2x)^7\).

Find the coefficient of \(x^3\) in the expansion of \((1 + 2x)^7\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

The row for power 7 is 1, 7, 21, 35, 35, 21, 7, 1; number 3 (counting from 0) is \(35\text{.}\) The term is \(35 \times 1^4 \times (2x)^3 = 35 \times 8x^3 = 280x^3\text{,}\) so the coefficient is \(280\text{.}\)
