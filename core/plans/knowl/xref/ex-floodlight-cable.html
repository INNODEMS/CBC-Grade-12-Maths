Exercise 6.

A floodlight stands on top of a \(15\,\text{m}\) pole at the centre of a square playground of side \(40\,\text{m}\) in Eldoret. A cable runs straight from the top of the pole to one corner of the playground. Find the length of the projection of the cable on the ground, and the length of the cable.
🔗
Answer.
\(\sqrt{800} \approx 28.28\,\text{m}\text{;}\) \(\sqrt{1025} \approx 32.02\,\text{m}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The projection runs from the centre of the square to a corner: half a diagonal. The diagonal squared is \(40^2 + 40^2 = 3200\text{,}\) so the projection is \(\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{3200} = \sqrt{800} \approx 28.28\,\text{m}\text{.}\) The cable is \(\sqrt{800 + 15^2} = \sqrt{1025} \approx 32.02\,\text{m}\text{.}\)
