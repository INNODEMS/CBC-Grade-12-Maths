Exercise 6. Challenge: Diagonals and Unit Vectors.

  1. Show that the space diagonal of a cube of edge \(s\) has length \(s\sqrt{3}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Find a unit vector in the direction of \(4\mathbf{i} - 4\mathbf{j} + 7\mathbf{k}\text{,}\) and check that its magnitude is \(1\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Hint.
Divide the vector by its magnitude.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
(a) \(\sqrt{s^2 + s^2 + s^2} = s\sqrt{3}\) (b) \(\frac{4}{9}\mathbf{i} - \frac{4}{9}\mathbf{j} + \frac{7}{9}\mathbf{k}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
(a) The diagonal from \(O\) to \((s, s, s)\) has length \(\sqrt{s^2 + s^2 + s^2} = \sqrt{3s^2} = s\sqrt{3}\text{.}\)
🔗
(b) \(|4\mathbf{i} - 4\mathbf{j} + 7\mathbf{k}| = \sqrt{16 + 16 + 49} = 9\text{.}\) Dividing by \(9\) gives \(\frac{4}{9}\mathbf{i} - \frac{4}{9}\mathbf{j} + \frac{7}{9}\mathbf{k}\text{,}\) which points the same way. Check: \(\frac{16}{81} + \frac{16}{81} + \frac{49}{81} = 1\text{,}\) so its magnitude is \(1\text{.}\)
