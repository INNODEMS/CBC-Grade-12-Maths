Exercise 5.

For which values of \(k\) does \(x^3 - 12x = k\) have three solutions?
🔗
Answer.
\(-16 \lt k \lt 16\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 3x^2 - 12 = 0\) at \(x = \pm 2\text{;}\) the maximum value is \(y(-2) = 16\) and the minimum value is \(y(2) = -16\text{.}\) Three solutions need \(-16 \lt k \lt 16\text{.}\)
