Exercise 5.

A faulty printer at a cyber café in Kisumu distorts every design by the matrix \(\begin{bmatrix} 5 \amp 3 \\ 3 \amp 2 \end{bmatrix}\text{.}\) Two corners of a printed design are at \((8, 5)\) and \((13, 8)\text{.}\) Where were these corners in the original design?
🔗
Answer.
\((1, 1)\) and \((2, 1)\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The determinant is \(10 - 9 = 1\text{,}\) so the inverse is \(\begin{bmatrix} 2 \amp -3 \\ -3 \amp 5 \end{bmatrix}\text{.}\) Then \(\begin{bmatrix} 2 \amp -3 \\ -3 \amp 5 \end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} 8 \amp 13 \\ 5 \amp 8 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \amp 2 \\ 1 \amp 1 \end{bmatrix}\text{.}\)
