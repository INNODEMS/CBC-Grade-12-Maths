Exercise 6. Challenge: Design a Transformation.

Find the matrix that maps the unit square \(OABC\) onto the parallelogram with corners \(O\text{,}\) \((4, 1)\text{,}\) \((5, 3)\) and \((1, 2)\text{,}\) with \(A(1, 0)\) going to \((4, 1)\text{.}\) Find the area of the parallelogram.
🔗
Hint.
The columns of the matrix are the images of \((1, 0)\) and \((0, 1)\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(\begin{bmatrix} 4 \amp 1 \\ 1 \amp 2 \end{bmatrix}\text{;}\) area \(7\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(A(1, 0) \to (4, 1)\) and \(C(0, 1) \to (1, 2)\text{,}\) so the matrix is \(\begin{bmatrix} 4 \amp 1 \\ 1 \amp 2 \end{bmatrix}\text{;}\) check \(B(1, 1) \to (5, 3)\text{.}\) Its determinant is \(8 - 1 = 7\) and the unit square has area \(1\text{,}\) so the parallelogram has area \(7\text{.}\)
