Example 8.5.9 A Diagonal of a Water Tank.

A water tank at a school in Machakos is a cuboid \(ABCDEFGH\) with \(AB = 3.6\,\text{m}\text{,}\) \(BC = 2.4\,\text{m}\) and \(AE = 1.5\,\text{m}\text{.}\) Find the angle between the diagonal \(AG\) and (a) the base \(ABCD\text{;}\) (b) the face \(ABFE\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

(a) \(AG\) meets the base at \(A\text{.}\) \(G\) is vertically above \(C\text{,}\) so the perpendicular from \(G\) is \(GC\) and the projection is \(AC\text{.}\) \(AC^2 = 3.6^2 + 2.4^2 = 18.72\text{.}\) In triangle \(AGC\text{,}\) with the right angle at \(C\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} \tan GAC = \frac{1.5}{\sqrt{18.72}} \end{equation*}
so the angle is about \(19.1^{\circ}\text{.}\)
🔗
(b) The perpendicular from \(G\) to the face \(ABFE\) is the edge \(GF\text{,}\) so the projection of \(AG\) on that face is \(AF\text{.}\) \(AF^2 = 3.6^2 + 1.5^2 = 15.21\text{,}\) so \(AF = 3.9\,\text{m}\text{.}\) In triangle \(AFG\text{,}\) with the right angle at \(F\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} \tan GAF = \frac{GF}{AF} = \frac{2.4}{3.9} \end{equation*}
so the angle is about \(31.6^{\circ}\text{.}\) The same line makes different angles with different planes.
