Example 2.1.34 Expanding \((1 - 3x)^4\).

Expand \((1 - 3x)^4\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

Put \(a = 1\) and \(b = -3x\) into the pattern for power 4:
\begin{align*} (1 - 3x)^4 \amp = 1 + 4(-3x) + 6(-3x)^2 + 4(-3x)^3 + (-3x)^4\\ \amp = 1 - 12x + 54x^2 - 108x^3 + 81x^4 \end{align*}
The bracket starts with 1, so the expansion is written in ascending powers of \(x\text{.}\) The odd powers of \(-3x\) are negative.
