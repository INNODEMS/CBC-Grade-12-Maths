Exercise 5.

A design was distorted by the matrix \(\begin{bmatrix} 3 \amp 1 \\ 1 \amp 1 \end{bmatrix}\text{.}\) One corner of the distorted design is \((7, 3)\text{,}\) and the distorted design has area \(18\) square units. Find the original corner and the original area.
🔗
Answer.
\((2, 1)\text{;}\) \(9\) square units
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\det = 3 - 1 = 2\text{,}\) so the inverse is \(\frac{1}{2}\begin{bmatrix} 1 \amp -1 \\ -1 \amp 3 \end{bmatrix}\text{,}\) and \(\frac{1}{2}\begin{bmatrix} 1 \amp -1 \\ -1 \amp 3 \end{bmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 7 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} = \frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\) The distortion multiplied areas by \(2\text{,}\) so the original area was \(18 \div 2 = 9\) square units.
