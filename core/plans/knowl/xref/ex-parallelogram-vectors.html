\(OABC\) is a parallelogram with \(\overrightarrow{OA} = \mathbf{a}\) and \(\overrightarrow{OC} = \mathbf{c}\text{,}\) and \(M\) is the midpoint of \(AB\text{.}\) Write \(\overrightarrow{OB}\text{,}\) \(\overrightarrow{AC}\) and \(\overrightarrow{OM}\) in terms of \(\mathbf{a}\) and \(\mathbf{c}\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{c}\text{;}\) \(\mathbf{c} - \mathbf{a}\text{;}\) \(\mathbf{a} + \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{c}\)
