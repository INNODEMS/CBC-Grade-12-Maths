Exercise 6.

A square floor tile should be \(30\,\text{cm}\) wide but is cut \((30 + 0.2)\) cm wide. Use \((30 + x)^2\) to find its area and the extra area.
🔗
Answer.
\(912.04\,\text{cm}^2\text{;}\) extra \(12.04\,\text{cm}^2\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\((30 + 0.2)^2 = 900 + 2(30)(0.2) + 0.2^2 = 900 + 12 + 0.04 = 912.04\,\text{cm}^2\text{.}\) The tile should be \(900\,\text{cm}^2\text{,}\) so the extra area is \(12.04\,\text{cm}^2\text{.}\)
