Exercise 4. Challenge: Chords Through a Point.

\(P\) is a fixed point inside a circle with centre \(O\text{.}\) Find the locus of the midpoints of all the chords that pass through \(P\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
If \(M\) is the midpoint of such a chord, what is angle \(OMP\text{?}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
The circle with diameter \(OP\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The line from \(O\) to the midpoint \(M\) of a chord is perpendicular to the chord, and the chord passes through \(P\text{,}\) so angle \(OMP = 90^{\circ}\text{.}\) So \(OP\) subtends a right angle at \(M\text{:}\) a constant angle locus, the circle with diameter \(OP\) (it includes \(O\text{,}\) the midpoint of the diameter through \(P\text{,}\) and \(P\text{,}\) the midpoint of the chord perpendicular to \(OP\)).
