Example 7.7.8 Describing a Region.

\(A\) and \(B\) are \(4\,\text{cm}\) apart. Describe the required region in Figure 7.7.9 with inequalities.
🔗

Solution.

The unshaded region is inside the circle, and the circle is solid, so its points are included: \(PA \le 3\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) The unshaded region is on \(B\)’s side of the bisector, where points are nearer to \(B\text{,}\) and the bisector is dashed, so it is not included: \(PB \lt PA\text{.}\) The region is the set of points \(P\) with \(PA \le 3\,\text{cm}\) and \(PB \lt PA\text{.}\)
