Example 7.6.8 Two Conditions at Once.

\(AB = 5\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Construct the points that are \(4\,\text{cm}\) from \(A\) and \(3\,\text{cm}\) from \(B\text{.}\) What is angle \(APB\) at each of them?
🔗

Solution.

The points \(4\,\text{cm}\) from \(A\) lie on a circle with centre \(A\) and radius \(4\,\text{cm}\text{;}\) the points \(3\,\text{cm}\) from \(B\) lie on a circle with centre \(B\) and radius \(3\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Here \(4 - 3 \lt 5 \lt 4 + 3\text{,}\) so the circles cross at two points \(P\) and \(P'\text{,}\) one on each side of \(AB\) (Figure 7.6.9).
🔗
In triangle \(APB\text{,}\) \(3^2 + 4^2 = 25 = 5^2\text{,}\) so by the converse of Pythagoras \(\angle APB = 90^{\circ}\) at both points.
