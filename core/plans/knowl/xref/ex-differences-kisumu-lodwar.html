Exercise 1.

Take Kisumu to be at \((0^\circ 06'\,\text{S},\ 34^\circ 45'\,\text{E})\) and Lodwar at \((3^\circ 07'\,\text{N},\ 35^\circ 36'\,\text{E})\text{.}\) Find their difference in latitude and their difference in longitude.
🔗
Answer.
\(3^\circ 13'\text{;}\) \(0^\circ 51'\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Opposite sides of the equator: \(0^\circ 06' + 3^\circ 07' = 3^\circ 13'\text{.}\) Both east: \(35^\circ 36' - 34^\circ 45' = 34^\circ 96' - 34^\circ 45' = 0^\circ 51'\text{.}\)
