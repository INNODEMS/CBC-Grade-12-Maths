Example 2.1.14 A Minus Sign in the Bracket.

Expand \((2x - 1)^3\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

First, \((2x - 1)^2 = 4x^2 - 2x - 2x + 1 = 4x^2 - 4x + 1\text{.}\) Then multiply by \((2x - 1)\text{:}\)
🔗
\begin{equation*} (2x - 1)^3 = 8x^3 - 12x^2 + 6x - 1 \end{equation*}
The signs alternate, because the bracket contains a minus sign.
🔗
Check by putting \(x = 1\text{:}\) \((2 - 1)^3 = 1\) and \(8 - 12 + 6 - 1 = 1\text{.}\)
