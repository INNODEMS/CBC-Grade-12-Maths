Example 14.5.9 Percentage Error of an Estimate.

In Learner Experience 14.4.2 the trapezoidal estimate of the area under \(y = x^2 + 3\) from \(x = 0\) to \(x = 4\) was \(34\text{,}\) the mid-ordinate estimate \(33\text{,}\) and the exact area \(\frac{100}{3}\text{.}\) Find the absolute error and the percentage error of each estimate.
🔗

Solution.

Trapezoidal: absolute error \(\left|34 - \frac{100}{3}\right| = \frac{2}{3} \approx 0.67\text{,}\) and
\begin{equation*} \text{percentage error} = \frac{2/3}{100/3} \times 100\% = 2\% \end{equation*}
Mid-ordinate: absolute error \(\left|33 - \frac{100}{3}\right| = \frac{1}{3} \approx 0.33\text{,}\) and the percentage error is \(\frac{1/3}{100/3} \times 100\% = 1\%\text{.}\)
