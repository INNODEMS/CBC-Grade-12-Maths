Example 5.1.29 Rotation Through \(90^\circ\) Clockwise.

Find the matrix of the rotation of \(90^\circ\) clockwise about \(O\text{,}\) and the image of \((2, 5)\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

A quarter turn clockwise sends \((1, 0)\) to \((0, -1)\) and \((0, 1)\) to \((1, 0)\text{,}\) so
\begin{equation*} M = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \amp 1 \\ -1 \amp 0 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad M \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 5 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix} \end{equation*}
The image is \((5, -2)\text{.}\) Notice that this matrix is the anticlockwise matrix \(\begin{bmatrix} 0 \amp -1 \\ 1 \amp 0 \end{bmatrix}\) with its rows written as columns.
