Exercise 2.

Sketch \(y = (x + 1)^2(x - 2)\text{,}\) labelling the intercepts and stationary points.
🔗
Answer.
\(y = x^3 - 3x - 2\text{;}\) touches at \(x = -1\text{,}\) crosses at \(x = 2\text{;}\) \(y\)-intercept \(-2\text{;}\) maximum point \((-1, 0)\text{;}\) minimum point \((1, -4)\text{.}\)
