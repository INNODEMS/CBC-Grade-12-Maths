Exercise 1.

Find \(D_3\) and \(D_7\) for the goat masses in Example 12.3.8.
🔗
Answer.
\(D_3 \approx 26.6\,\text{kg}\text{,}\) \(D_7 \approx 34.1\,\text{kg}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Positions \(15\) and \(35\text{.}\) \(D_3 = 24.5 + \dfrac{15 - 10}{12} \times 5 = 26.58\text{;}\) \(D_7 = 29.5 + \dfrac{35 - 22}{14} \times 5 = 34.14\text{.}\)
