Exercise 7.

For \(y = 3\sin(2x + 70^\circ)\text{,}\) find the amplitude, the period, the phase angle, and the translation that maps \(y = 3\sin 2x\) onto it.
🔗
Answer.
Amplitude 3, period \(180^\circ\text{,}\) phase angle \(70^\circ\text{;}\) a translation of \(35^\circ\) to the left.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(2x + 70^\circ = 2(x + 35^\circ)\text{.}\)
