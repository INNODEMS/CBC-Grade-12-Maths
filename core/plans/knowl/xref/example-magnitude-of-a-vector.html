Example 10.4.8 Magnitude of a Vector.

Find the magnitude of (a) \(\begin{pmatrix} -8 \\ 4 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}\text{;}\) (b) \(5\mathbf{i} - 2\mathbf{j} + \mathbf{k}\text{,}\) exactly and to 2 decimal places.
🔗

Solution.

(a) \(\sqrt{(-8)^2 + 4^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{64 + 16 + 1} = \sqrt{81} = 9\text{.}\)
🔗
(b) \(\sqrt{5^2 + (-2)^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{25 + 4 + 1} = \sqrt{30} \approx 5.48\text{.}\)
