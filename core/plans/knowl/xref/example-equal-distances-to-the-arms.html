Example 7.4.10 Equal Distances to the Arms.

\(P\) is on the bisector of angle \(XOY\text{.}\) \(F\) on \(OX\) and \(G\) on \(OY\) are the feet of the perpendiculars from \(P\text{.}\) \(PF = 6\,\text{cm}\) and \(OP = 10\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Find \(PG\text{,}\) \(OF\) and \(OG\text{,}\) and angle \(XOY\) to the nearest degree.
🔗

Solution.

\(P\) is on the bisector, so it is the same distance from both arms: \(PG = PF = 6\,\text{cm}\text{.}\)
🔗
In the right-angled triangle \(OFP\text{:}\) \(OF^2 = OP^2 - PF^2 = 100 - 36 = 64\text{,}\) so \(OF = 8\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) In the same way \(OG = 8\,\text{cm}\text{.}\)
🔗
\(\sin \angle FOP = \dfrac{6}{10} = 0.6\text{,}\) so \(\angle FOP = 36.87^{\circ}\) and \(\angle XOY = 2 \times 36.87^{\circ} = 73.74^{\circ}\text{,}\) about \(74^{\circ}\text{.}\)
