Exercise 5.

A triangle has sides \(5\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) \(12\,\text{cm}\) and \(13\,\text{cm}\) (it is right-angled). Find the distance of its incentre from each side.
🔗
Answer.
\(2\,\text{cm}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Splitting the triangle into three triangles from the incentre, each of height \(r\text{:}\) \(\tfrac{1}{2}r(5 + 12 + 13) = \tfrac{1}{2} \times 5 \times 12\text{,}\) so \(15r = 30\) and \(r = 2\,\text{cm}\text{.}\)
