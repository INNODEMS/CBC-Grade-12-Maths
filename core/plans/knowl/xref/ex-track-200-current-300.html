Exercise 4.

A ship must make good a track of \(200^\circ\) at \(11\,\text{knots}\) through the water. A current flows towards \(300^\circ\) at \(2.5\,\text{knots}\text{.}\) Find the course to steer and the ground speed.
🔗
Answer.
\(187.1^\circ\text{;}\) \(10.3\,\text{knots}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The angle between the track and the current is \(100^\circ\text{.}\) \(\sin d = 2.5 \sin 100^\circ \div 11\text{,}\) \(d = 12.9^\circ\text{.}\) The current pushes clockwise, so steer anticlockwise: \(200^\circ - 12.9^\circ = 187.1^\circ\text{.}\) Ground speed \(11 \cos 12.9^\circ + 2.5 \cos 100^\circ = 10.72 - 0.43 = 10.3\,\text{knots}\text{.}\)
