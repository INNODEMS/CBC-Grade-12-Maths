Exercise 2.

Find the greatest and least values of \(f(x) = 12x - x^3\) for \(0 \le x \le 3\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
Greatest \(16\) (at \(x = 2\)); least \(0\) (at \(x = 0\))
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The stationary point at \(x = -2\) is outside the interval. \(f(0) = 0\text{,}\) \(f(2) = 16\text{,}\) \(f(3) = 9\text{.}\)
