Exercise 6.

A row of Pascal’s triangle starts 1, 9, ... Which power is it the row for, and what is its third number?
🔗
Answer.
Power 9; \(36\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The second number of the row for power \(n\) is \(n\text{,}\) so this is the row for power 9. Its third number is \(8 + 28 = 36\text{,}\) from the row for power 8.
