Exercise 5.

Find the vector \(\mathbf{x}\) if \(2\mathbf{x} + \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ -1 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 7 \\ 5 \\ -6 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(\mathbf{x} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 3 \\ -5 \end{pmatrix}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(2\mathbf{x} = \begin{pmatrix} 7 - 3 \\ 5 + 1 \\ -6 - 4 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 6 \\ -10 \end{pmatrix}\text{,}\) so \(\mathbf{x} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 3 \\ -5 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\)
