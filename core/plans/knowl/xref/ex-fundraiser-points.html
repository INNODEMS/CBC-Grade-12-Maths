Exercise 4.

On your graph from Learner Experience 4.2.2, is the plan of \(60\) mandazi and \(35\) samosas in R? Is the plan of \(80\) mandazi and \(32\) samosas? Check each answer by substituting.
🔗
Answer.
\((60, 35)\text{:}\) yes. \((80, 32)\text{:}\) no
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\((60, 35)\text{:}\) \(95 \le 100\text{,}\) \(900 + 1225 = 2125 \le 2520\text{,}\) \(35 \ge 30\text{:}\) in R. \((80, 32)\text{:}\) \(112 \gt 100\text{,}\) so it lies in the shaded part above \(x + y = 100\text{:}\) not in R.
