Find the magnitude of each vector, exactly, and to 2 decimal places where it is not a whole number. (a) \(\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 4 \\ 8 \end{pmatrix}\) (b) \(2\mathbf{i} - 2\mathbf{j} + \mathbf{k}\) (c) \(\begin{pmatrix} -3 \\ 0 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}\) (d) \(\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 3 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}\)
🔗
Answer.
(a) \(9\) (b) \(3\) (c) \(5\) (d) \(\sqrt{26} \approx 5.10\)
