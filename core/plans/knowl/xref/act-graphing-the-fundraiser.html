Learner Experience 4.2.2 Graphing the Fundraiser.

The Grade 12 class in Machakos makes \(x\) mandazi and \(y\) samosas for its fundraiser (Learner Experience 4.1.3). The constraints are
\begin{equation*} x + y \le 100, \quad 15x + 35y \le 2520, \quad y \ge 30, \quad x \ge 0. \end{equation*}
Draw axes with \(x\) from \(0\) to \(170\) and \(y\) from \(0\) to \(110\text{.}\)
🔗
  1. Draw the line \(x + y = 100\) through \((100, 0)\) and \((0, 100)\text{.}\) Should it be solid or dashed?
    🔗
    Solution.
    Solid: “at most” allows exactly \(100\) items, so points on the line are allowed.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Test the point \((0, 0)\) in \(x + y \le 100\text{.}\) Shade the side of the line where the inequality is false.
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(0 + 0 = 0 \le 100\) is true, so \((0, 0)\) is on the wanted side. Shade the other side, above and to the right of the line.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Find where \(15x + 35y = 2520\) crosses the axes. Draw it and shade its unwanted side in the same way.
    🔗
    Solution.
    With \(y = 0\text{,}\) \(x = 168\text{;}\) with \(x = 0\text{,}\) \(y = 72\text{.}\) Draw the solid line through \((168, 0)\) and \((0, 72)\text{.}\) \((0, 0)\) gives \(0 \le 2520\text{,}\) true, so shade the side away from the origin.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  4. Draw \(y = 30\text{.}\) Which side do you shade?
    🔗
    Solution.
    A solid horizontal line. The wanted side is \(y \ge 30\text{,}\) above the line, so shade below it.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  5. Shade the side of the \(y\)-axis where \(x \lt 0\text{.}\) Label the region left clear R and add a key.
    🔗
    Solution.
    R is the clear four-sided region shown in Figure 4.2.28. Its corners are found in Learner Experience 4.2.27.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  6. Is the plan \((50, 40)\) in R? Is \((50, 55)\text{?}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \((50, 40)\text{:}\) \(90 \le 100\text{,}\) \(750 + 1400 = 2150 \le 2520\text{,}\) \(40 \ge 30\text{:}\) yes, it is in R. \((50, 55)\text{:}\) \(105 \gt 100\text{,}\) so it lies in the shaded part: no.
    🔗
    🔗
