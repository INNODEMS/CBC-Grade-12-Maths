Exercise 1.

A box \(ABCDEFGH\) has \(AB = 20\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) \(BC = 12\,\text{cm}\) and \(AE = 9\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Find the angle between \(AG\) and (a) the base \(ABCD\text{;}\) (b) the face \(BCGF\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
(a) \(21.1^{\circ}\) (b) \(53.1^{\circ}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
(a) The projection is \(AC = \sqrt{400 + 144} = \sqrt{544}\text{,}\) so \(\tan GAC = \dfrac{9}{\sqrt{544}}\) and the angle is about \(21.1^{\circ}\text{.}\)
🔗
(b) \(A\) projects to \(B\) (the edge \(AB\) is at right angles to the face \(BCGF\)), so the projection is \(BG = \sqrt{144 + 81} = 15\,\text{cm}\) and the angle is at \(G\text{:}\) \(\tan AGB = \dfrac{20}{15}\text{,}\) about \(53.1^{\circ}\text{.}\)
