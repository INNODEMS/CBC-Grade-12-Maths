Example 8.3.10 Diagonals of a Water Tank.

A water tank at a school in Machakos is a cuboid \(ABCDEFGH\) with \(AB = 3.6\,\text{m}\text{,}\) \(BC = 2.4\,\text{m}\) and \(AE = 1.5\,\text{m}\text{.}\) Find the length of the base diagonal \(AC\) and of the space diagonal \(AG\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

Step 1: triangle \(ABC\) has a right angle at \(B\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} AC^2 = 3.6^2 + 2.4^2 = 18.72, \quad AC = \sqrt{18.72} \approx 4.33 \,\text{m} \end{equation*}
\(AC\) is the projection of \(AG\) on the base.
🔗
Step 2: triangle \(ACG\) has a right angle at \(C\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} AG^2 = 18.72 + 1.5^2 = 20.97, \quad AG = \sqrt{20.97} \approx 4.58 \,\text{m} \end{equation*}
We used \(AC^2 = 18.72\text{,}\) not the rounded \(4.33^2\text{,}\) so no accuracy was lost.
