Exercise 1.

\(AB = 4\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Show the region where \(PA \lt 3\,\text{cm}\) and \(PB \lt 3\,\text{cm}\) by shading the unwanted parts. Describe its boundary.
🔗
Answer.
The overlap of the two discs, bounded by two dashed arcs that meet at two points.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Draw circles of radius \(3\,\text{cm}\) about \(A\) and about \(B\text{,}\) both dashed. Shade outside each circle. The region left clear, inside both, is a lens shape. Its corners are on the perpendicular bisector of \(AB\text{,}\) \(2\,\text{cm}\) from \(A\) along \(AB\) and \(\sqrt{3^2 - 2^2} \approx 2.2\,\text{cm}\) either side of \(AB\text{.}\)
