Exercise 5.

A cyclist starts from rest and moves with constant acceleration \(a\text{.}\) She covers \(50\,\text{m}\) in the first \(10\) seconds. Find \(a\) by integrating.
🔗
Answer.
\(a = 1\,\text{m/s}^2\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(v = at\) and \(s = \frac{1}{2}at^2\) (both constants are \(0\)). At \(t = 10\text{:}\) \(50a = 50\text{,}\) so \(a = 1\text{.}\)
