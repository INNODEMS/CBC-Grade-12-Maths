Exercise 4.

A trench for a water pipe is \(40\,\text{m}\) long and \(1.2\,\text{m}\) wide. Its depth, measured every \(0.3\,\text{m}\) across, is \(0, 0.5, 0.7, 0.6, 0\) metres. Estimate the volume of soil removed.
🔗
Answer.
\(21.6\,\text{m}^3\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Cross-section: \(\tfrac{1}{2} \times 0.3 \times [0 + 0 + 2(0.5 + 0.7 + 0.6)] = 0.15 \times 3.6 = 0.54\,\text{m}^2\text{.}\) Volume \(= 0.54 \times 40 = 21.6\,\text{m}^3\text{.}\)
