Exercise 5.

\(4\) lorries, each making \(3\) trips a day, carry \(360\) tonnes of sand in \(6\) days. How many days do \(5\) lorries making \(4\) trips a day take to carry \(600\) tonnes?
🔗
Answer.
\(6\) days
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(6 \times \tfrac{600}{360} \times \tfrac{4}{5} \times \tfrac{3}{4} = 6\text{.}\)
