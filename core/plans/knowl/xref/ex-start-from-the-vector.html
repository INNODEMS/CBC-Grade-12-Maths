Exercise 5.

\(\overrightarrow{AB} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ -1 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}\) and \(B\) is \((5, 2, 0)\text{.}\) Find \(A\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(A(2, 3, -4)\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\mathbf{a} = \mathbf{b} - \overrightarrow{AB} = \begin{pmatrix} 5 - 3 \\ 2 + 1 \\ 0 - 4 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 3 \\ -4 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\)
