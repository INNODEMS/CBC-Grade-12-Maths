Exercise 1.

Find the greatest and least values of \(f(x) = x^3 - 6x^2 - 15x + 4\) for \(-2 \le x \le 9\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
Greatest \(112\) (at \(x = 9\)); least \(-96\) (at \(x = 5\))
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Stationary points at \(x = -1\) and \(x = 5\text{.}\) The candidates \(f(-2), f(-1), f(5), f(9)\) are \(2, 12, -96, 112\text{.}\)
