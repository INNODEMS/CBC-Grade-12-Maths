Share Ksh \(18\,900\) in the ratio \(\tfrac{1}{3} : \tfrac{1}{5} : \tfrac{1}{6}\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(10 : 6 : 5\text{;}\) Ksh \(9000\text{,}\) Ksh \(5400\) and Ksh \(4500\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Multiply by \(30\text{:}\) \(10 : 6 : 5\text{,}\) \(21\) parts. One part is \(18\,900 \div 21 = 900\text{.}\)
