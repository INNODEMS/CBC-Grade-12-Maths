Example 14.6.10 Finding the Initial Velocity.

A particle moves in a straight line with acceleration \(a = 6t - 4\,\text{m/s}^2\text{.}\) Its velocity is \(5\,\text{m/s}\) when \(t = 1\text{,}\) and it starts at the origin. Find \(v\) and \(s\) in terms of \(t\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

\(v = 3t^2 - 4t + c\text{.}\) At \(t = 1\text{:}\) \(5 = 3 - 4 + c\text{,}\) so \(c = 6\) and \(v = 3t^2 - 4t + 6\text{.}\) The particle’s starting velocity is \(6\,\text{m/s}\text{.}\)
🔗
\(s = t^3 - 2t^2 + 6t + d\text{,}\) and \(s = 0\) at \(t = 0\) gives \(d = 0\text{:}\) \(s = t^3 - 2t^2 + 6t\text{.}\)
