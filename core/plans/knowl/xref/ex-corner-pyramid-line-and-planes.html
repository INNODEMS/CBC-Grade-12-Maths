Exercise 5.

A pyramid \(VABC\) has a right angle \(BAC\) in its base, \(AB = AC = 10\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) and the edge \(VA = 7\,\text{cm}\) is vertical. Find the angle between \(VB\) and (a) the base \(ABC\text{;}\) (b) the face \(VAC\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
(a) \(35.0^{\circ}\) (b) \(55.0^{\circ}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
(a) \(V\) is above \(A\text{,}\) so the projection of \(VB\) is \(AB\text{:}\) \(\tan VBA = \dfrac{7}{10}\text{,}\) about \(35.0^{\circ}\text{.}\)
🔗
(b) \(BA\) is at right angles to both \(AC\) and \(AV\text{,}\) so it is perpendicular to the face \(VAC\text{,}\) and \(B\) projects to \(A\text{.}\) The projection of \(VB\) is \(VA\text{:}\) \(\tan BVA = \dfrac{10}{7}\text{,}\) about \(55.0^{\circ}\text{.}\)
