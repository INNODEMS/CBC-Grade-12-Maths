Exercise 6. Challenge: Back Where It Started.

A particle starts at the origin with velocity \(v = t^2 - 6t + 5\,\text{m/s}\text{.}\) Find every time \(T \gt 0\) at which it is back at the origin.
🔗
Hint.
The displacement from \(0\) to \(T\) is \(\dfrac{T^3}{3} - 3T^2 + 5T\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(T = \frac{9 \pm \sqrt{21}}{2}\text{,}\) about \(2.21\,\text{s}\) and \(6.79\,\text{s}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\frac{T^3}{3} - 3T^2 + 5T = \frac{T}{3}(T^2 - 9T + 15) = 0\text{.}\) With \(T \gt 0\text{:}\) \(T^2 - 9T + 15 = 0\text{,}\) so \(T = \frac{9 \pm \sqrt{21}}{2} \approx 2.21\) or \(6.79\text{.}\)
