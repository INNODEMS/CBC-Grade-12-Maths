Exercise 5.

A vehicle’s velocity is \(v = kt(20 - t)\,\text{m/s}\text{.}\) It starts from rest and stops at \(t = 20\text{,}\) having travelled \(200\,\text{m}\text{.}\) Find \(k\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(k = 0.15\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\int_0^{20} k(20t - t^2)\,dt = k\left[10t^2 - \frac{t^3}{3}\right]_0^{20} = k\left(4000 - \frac{8000}{3}\right) = \frac{4000k}{3} = 200\text{,}\) so \(k = 0.15\text{.}\)
