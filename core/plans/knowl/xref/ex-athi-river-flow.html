Exercise 1.

At a point near Kibwezi the Athi River is \(30\,\text{m}\) wide. Its depth, measured every \(5\,\text{m}\) across, is \(0, 1.5, 2.4, 2.8, 2.5, 1.6, 0\) metres, and the water flows at \(0.4\,\text{m/s}\text{.}\) Estimate the area of the cross-section and the volume of water that passes each second.
🔗
Answer.
\(54\,\text{m}^2\text{;}\) \(21.6\,\text{m}^3\) each second
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(A \approx \tfrac{1}{2} \times 5 \times [0 + 0 + 2(1.5 + 2.4 + 2.8 + 2.5 + 1.6)] = 2.5 \times 21.6 = 54\,\text{m}^2\text{.}\) Volume each second \(= 54 \times 0.4 = 21.6\,\text{m}^3\text{.}\)
