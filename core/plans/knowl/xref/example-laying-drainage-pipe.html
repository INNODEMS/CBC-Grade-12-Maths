Example 3.2.41 Laying Drainage Pipe.

\(12\) workers lay \(480\,\text{m}\) of drainage pipe in \(8\) days. How many days would \(16\) workers take to lay \(720\,\text{m}\text{?}\)
🔗

Solution.

More pipe needs more days: factor \(\tfrac{720}{480}\text{.}\) More workers need fewer days: factor \(\tfrac{12}{16}\text{.}\)
🔗
\(8 \times \tfrac{720}{480} \times \tfrac{12}{16} = 8 \times \tfrac{3}{2} \times \tfrac{3}{4} = 9\) days.
