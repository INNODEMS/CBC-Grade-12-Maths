Exercise 6. Challenge: A Missing Frequency in the Median Class.

The times, in minutes, that \(4 + 9 + x + 10 + 5\) learners took to finish a puzzle are grouped as \(0 \le t \lt 10\text{,}\) \(10 \le t \lt 20\text{,}\) \(20 \le t \lt 30\text{,}\) \(30 \le t \lt 40\) and \(40 \le t \lt 50\text{,}\) with frequencies \(4, 9, x, 10, 5\text{.}\) The median is \(26\) minutes and lies in the class \(20 \le t \lt 30\text{.}\) Find \(x\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
\(x\) appears twice: in \(n\) and as the frequency \(F\) of the median class.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(x = 10\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(n = 28 + x\text{,}\) \(L = 20\text{,}\) \(CF = 13\text{,}\) \(F = x\text{:}\) \(26 = 20 + \dfrac{(28 + x)/2 - 13}{x} \times 10\text{.}\) So \(6x = 10\left(\dfrac{28 + x}{2} - 13\right) = 5x + 10\text{,}\) giving \(x = 10\text{.}\) Check: \(n = 38\text{,}\) position \(19\) lies between the cumulative frequencies \(13\) and \(23\text{.}\)
