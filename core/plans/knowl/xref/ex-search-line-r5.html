Exercise 1.

R is the region \(7x + 4y \le 84\text{,}\) \(x + 8y \le 64\text{,}\) \(x \ge 0\text{,}\) \(y \ge 0\) (Exercise 4.2.2.2). Use a search line to find the maximum of \(P = 5x + 7y\) on R.
🔗
Answer.
\(89\) at \((8, 7)\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Draw \(5x + 7y = 35\) through \((7, 0)\) and \((0, 5)\) and slide it away from the origin. It leaves R at the vertex where \(7x + 4y = 84\) and \(x + 8y = 64\) meet: \((8, 7)\text{.}\) Gradient check: \(-\tfrac{5}{7}\) lies between \(-\tfrac{7}{4}\) and \(-\tfrac{1}{8}\text{.}\) \(P = 40 + 49 = 89\text{.}\)
