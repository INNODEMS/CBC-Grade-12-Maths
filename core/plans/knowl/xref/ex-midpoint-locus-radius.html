Exercise 1.

Chords of \(16\,\text{cm}\) are drawn in a circle of radius \(10\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Describe the locus of their midpoints.
🔗
Answer.
The circle with the same centre \(O\) and radius \(6\,\text{cm}\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(OM^2 = 10^2 - 8^2 = 36\text{,}\) so \(OM = 6\,\text{cm}\) for every such chord.
