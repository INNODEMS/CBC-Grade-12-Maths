Exercise 1.

Show that \(P(1, 1)\text{,}\) \(Q(3, 4)\) and \(R(7, 10)\) are collinear.
🔗
Answer.
\(\overrightarrow{PQ} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}\text{,}\) \(\overrightarrow{QR} = \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix} = 2\overrightarrow{PQ}\text{,}\) sharing \(Q\text{:}\) collinear.
