Exercise 5.

Write each locus in construction language.
🔗
  1. The locus of a point that is always as far from the gate \(G\) as from the tap \(T\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. The locus of a point \(2\,\text{m}\) from the tap \(T\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. The locus of a point equidistant from two parallel fences.
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Answer.
(a) The perpendicular bisector of \(GT\text{.}\) (b) A circle, centre \(T\text{,}\) radius \(2\,\text{m}\text{.}\) (c) The line parallel to both fences, halfway between them.
