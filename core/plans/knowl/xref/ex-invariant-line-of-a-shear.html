Exercise 5.

Show that every point on the \(x\)-axis is fixed by \(\begin{bmatrix} 1 \amp 2.5 \\ 0 \amp 1 \end{bmatrix}\text{.}\) Find the images of \((0, 2)\) and \((4, 2)\text{,}\) and explain why both moved the same distance.
🔗
Answer.
\((t, 0) \to (t, 0)\text{;}\) \((5, 2)\) and \((9, 2)\text{:}\) both moved 5 units, because both are 2 units from the invariant axis.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\begin{bmatrix} 1 \amp 2.5 \\ 0 \amp 1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} t \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} t \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\) \((0, 2) \to (0 + 2.5 \times 2,\ 2) = (5, 2)\) and \((4, 2) \to (4 + 5,\ 2) = (9, 2)\text{.}\) Each moves \(2.5 \times 2 = 5\) units: the distance moved depends only on the distance from the invariant axis.
