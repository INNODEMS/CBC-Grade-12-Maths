Example 7.2.8 A Security Light at a Gate.

A security light at the gate \(G\) of a school in Nakuru lights everything within \(15\,\text{m}\) of it. Draw the lit region on a plan with \(1\,\text{cm}\) representing \(5\,\text{m}\text{.}\) Is a car parked \(12\,\text{m}\) from the gate lit?
🔗

Solution.

Step 1: On the plan, \(15\,\text{m}\) is \(15 \div 5 = 3\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Mark \(G\) and draw the circle with centre \(G\) and radius \(3\,\text{cm}\text{.}\)
🔗
Step 2: Shade the outside of the circle, leaving the lit region clear. “Within \(15\,\text{m}\)” includes exactly \(15\,\text{m}\text{,}\) so draw the circle solid. Key: shaded, not wanted; unshaded, the lit region.
🔗
Step 3: The car is \(12 \div 5 = 2.4\,\text{cm}\) from \(G\) on the plan, less than \(3\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) so it is inside the region: the car is lit.
🔗
