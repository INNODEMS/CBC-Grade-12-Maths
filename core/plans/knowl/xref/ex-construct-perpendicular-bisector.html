Exercise 1.

Draw a line segment \(AB = 9\,\text{cm}\) and construct its perpendicular bisector with the compasses opened to \(6\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) How far from the midpoint \(M\) are the points where your arcs cross?
🔗
Answer.
About \(4.0\,\text{cm}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Each crossing point is \(6\,\text{cm}\) from \(A\text{,}\) and \(AM = 4.5\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) so its distance from \(M\) is \(\sqrt{6^2 - 4.5^2} = \sqrt{15.75} \approx 3.97\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) about \(4.0\,\text{cm}\text{.}\)
