Learner Experience 12.3.24 Top Ten Percent.

The marks out of 100 of \(80\) learners in a county mock examination are shown below.
🔗
  1. How many learners are in the top \(10\) per cent?
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(10\%\) of \(80\) is \(8\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  2. So how many learners are below the cut-off mark? In which class is the cut-off?
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(72\text{.}\) The cumulative frequencies are \(3, 9, 20, 38, 57, 69, 76, 80\text{,}\) so position \(72\) is in the class 80–89.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Estimate the cut-off mark, as for a quartile.
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(79.5 + \dfrac{72 - 69}{7} \times 10 = 83.8\text{.}\) Learners with more than about \(84\) marks get a certificate.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  4. Estimate the mark below which the bottom \(10\) per cent of learners lie.
    🔗
    Solution.
    Position \(8\text{,}\) in 30–39: \(29.5 + \dfrac{8 - 3}{6} \times 10 = 37.8\text{.}\)
