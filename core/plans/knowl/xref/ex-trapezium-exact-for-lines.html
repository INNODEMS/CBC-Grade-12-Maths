Exercise 5. Challenge: Exact for Straight Lines.

Show that one trapezium from \(x = a\) to \(x = b\) gives exactly \(\displaystyle\int_a^b (mx + k)\,dx\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
Show that both are equal to \((b - a)\left(\dfrac{m(a + b)}{2} + k\right)\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
Both equal \((b - a)\left(\frac{m(a + b)}{2} + k\right)\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Trapezium: \(\tfrac{1}{2}(b - a)\left[(ma + k) + (mb + k)\right] = (b - a)\left(\frac{m(a + b)}{2} + k\right)\text{.}\) Integral: \(\left[\frac{mx^2}{2} + kx\right]_a^b = \frac{m(b^2 - a^2)}{2} + k(b - a)\text{,}\) and \(b^2 - a^2 = (b - a)(b + a)\text{,}\) which gives the same.
