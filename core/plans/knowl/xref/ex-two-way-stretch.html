A two-way stretch is made of a stretch with factor \(2\) and the \(y\)-axis invariant and a stretch with factor \(5\) and the \(x\)-axis invariant. Write down its matrix, find the image of \((3, 2)\) and give its area scale factor.
🔗
Answer.
\(\begin{bmatrix} 2 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp 5 \end{bmatrix}\text{;}\) \((6, 10)\text{;}\) \(10\)
