Exercise 5.

A rectangular shamba near Nakuru measures \(30\,\text{m}\) by \(20\,\text{m}\text{.}\) A farmer draws it on a plan, using the matrix \(\begin{bmatrix} 1.5 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp 2 \end{bmatrix}\) to change the real measurements into plan units. What is the area of the shamba on the plan, in square plan units?
🔗
Answer.
\(1800\) square plan units
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The real area is \(30 \times 20 = 600\,\text{m}^2\text{.}\) The determinant is \(1.5 \times 2 = 3\text{,}\) so the area on the plan is \(3 \times 600 = 1800\) square plan units. (Check: the plan rectangle is \(45\) by \(40\text{.}\))
