Example 5.4.8 Area of an Image.

The triangle \(ABC\) with \(A(1, 1)\text{,}\) \(B(5, 1)\) and \(C(1, 4)\) is mapped by \(M = \begin{bmatrix} 5 \amp -1 \\ 1 \amp -1 \end{bmatrix}\text{.}\) Find the area of the image and describe its orientation.
🔗

Solution.

The triangle has a right angle at \(A\text{,}\) with sides \(4\) and \(3\text{,}\) so its area is \(\frac{1}{2} \times 4 \times 3 = 6\) square units.
🔗
\(\det M = 5 \times (-1) - (-1) \times 1 = -5 + 1 = -4\text{.}\) The area scale factor is \(|-4| = 4\text{,}\) so the image has area \(4 \times 6 = 24\) square units.
🔗
The determinant is negative, so the orientation is reversed. Check with the images:
\begin{equation*} \begin{bmatrix} 5 \amp -1 \\ 1 \amp -1 \end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} 1 \amp 5 \amp 1 \\ 1 \amp 1 \amp 4 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 4 \amp 24 \amp 1 \\ 0 \amp 4 \amp -3 \end{bmatrix} \end{equation*}
\(A, B, C\) runs anticlockwise, but \(A'(4, 0)\text{,}\) \(B'(24, 4)\text{,}\) \(C'(1, -3)\) runs clockwise (Figure 5.4.9).
