Exercise 5.

\(\begin{pmatrix} m \\ 6 \\ -4 \end{pmatrix}\) is parallel to \(\begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ n \\ 2 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\) Find \(m\) and \(n\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(m = -6\text{,}\) \(n = -3\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The third components are both known: \(-4 = 2k\text{,}\) so \(k = -2\text{.}\) Then \(m = -2 \times 3 = -6\) and \(6 = -2n\text{,}\) so \(n = -3\text{.}\)
