Exercise 1.

Write in terms of \(\mathbf{i}\text{,}\) \(\mathbf{j}\) and \(\mathbf{k}\text{:}\) (a) \(\begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ -1 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix}\) (b) \(\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 3 \\ -4 \end{pmatrix}\)
🔗
Answer.
(a) \(6\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 2\mathbf{k}\) (b) \(3\mathbf{j} - 4\mathbf{k}\)
