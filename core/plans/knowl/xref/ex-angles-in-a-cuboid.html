Exercise 1.

A cuboid \(ABCDEFGH\) has \(AB = 14\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) \(BC = 8\,\text{cm}\) and \(AE = 5\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Find (a) angle \(GAB\text{;}\) (b) angle \(CAH\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
(a) \(34.0^{\circ}\) (b) \(65.1^{\circ}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
(a) Angle \(ABG = 90^{\circ}\text{.}\) \(AG = \sqrt{196 + 64 + 25} = \sqrt{285}\text{,}\) so \(\cos GAB = \dfrac{14}{\sqrt{285}}\) and angle \(GAB \approx 34.0^{\circ}\text{.}\)
🔗
(b) Triangle \(ACH\text{:}\) \(AC^2 = 196 + 64 = 260\text{,}\) \(AH^2 = 64 + 25 = 89\text{,}\) \(CH^2 = 196 + 25 = 221\text{.}\) No right angle, so \(\cos CAH = \dfrac{260 + 89 - 221}{2\sqrt{260}\sqrt{89}}\) and angle \(CAH \approx 65.1^{\circ}\text{.}\)
