Exercise 2.

A posho mill bought for Ksh \(250\,000\) loses \(10\%\) of its value each year. Use the expansion of \((1 - x)^3\) to find its value after 3 years. Why is your answer exact?
🔗
Answer.
Ksh \(182\,250\text{;}\) \((1 - x)^3\) has only four terms
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\((1 - 0.1)^3 = 1 - 0.3 + 0.03 - 0.001 = 0.729\text{,}\) so the value is \(250\,000 \times 0.729\) = Ksh \(182\,250\text{.}\) The expansion of a cube has only four terms, and all four were used.
