Example 7.8.8 Midpoints of Equal Chords.

A circle has centre \(O\) and radius \(13\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Describe the locus of the midpoints of all chords of length \(24\,\text{cm}\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

Let \(M\) be the midpoint of a chord \(AB\) of \(24\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Then \(OM\) is perpendicular to \(AB\) and \(AM = 12\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) so
\begin{equation*} OM^2 = 13^2 - 12^2 = 169 - 144 = 25, \qquad OM = 5 \,\text{cm}. \end{equation*}
This is the same for every such chord. The locus is the circle with centre \(O\) and radius \(5\,\text{cm}\text{.}\)
