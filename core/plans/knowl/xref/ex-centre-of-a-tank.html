Exercise 3.

Only part of the round concrete base of an old water tank in Kakamega is left. Explain how to find its centre with a tape measure, pegs and string.
🔗
Answer.
Peg out two chords on the rim; find the perpendicular bisector of each; the centre is where they cross.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Put pegs \(A\text{,}\) \(B\) and \(C\) on the rim. For chord \(AB\text{,}\) find the points equidistant from \(A\) and \(B\) with two equal lengths of string, one from each peg, on both sides of \(AB\text{;}\) the line through them is the perpendicular bisector. Do the same for \(BC\text{.}\) Both lines pass through the centre, so it is where they cross.
