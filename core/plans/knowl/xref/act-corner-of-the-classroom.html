Learner Experience 10.1.3 The Corner of the Classroom.

A classroom is \(8\,\text{m}\) long, \(6\,\text{m}\) wide and \(3\,\text{m}\) high. Take a floor corner as the origin \(O\text{,}\) the \(x\)-axis along the front wall, the \(y\)-axis along the side wall and the \(z\)-axis straight up. Distances are in metres.
🔗
  1. The clock is on the front wall, \(4\,\text{m}\) along from the corner and \(2.5\,\text{m}\) up. Write its coordinates \((x, y, z)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \((4, 0, 2.5)\text{.}\) It is on the front wall, so it has not moved along the side wall: \(y = 0\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  2. The light is at the centre of the ceiling. Write its coordinates.
    🔗
    Solution.
    \((4, 3, 3)\text{:}\) half of \(8\text{,}\) half of \(6\text{,}\) and the height of the ceiling.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Which coordinate is \(0\) for every point on the floor? Which is \(0\) for every point on the front wall?
    🔗
    Solution.
    On the floor \(z = 0\text{;}\) on the front wall \(y = 0\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  4. How far must you move along each axis to go from the clock to the light? Write the three moves as a column.
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(0\) along \(x\text{,}\) \(3\) along \(y\) and \(0.5\) up: \(\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 3 \\ 0.5 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  5. Write the coordinates of the ceiling corner farthest from the origin.
    🔗
    Solution.
    \((8, 6, 3)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗

    Note 10.1.5.

    Groups who measured their own room report different numbers. Compare the reasoning, not the numbers.
