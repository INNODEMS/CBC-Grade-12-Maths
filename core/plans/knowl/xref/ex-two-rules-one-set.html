Exercise 6. Challenge: Two Rules, One Set.

Give two different set-builder descriptions of \(\{1, 4, 7, 10, 13\}\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
For example \(\{x : x \text{ is a natural number less than } 14 \text{ that leaves remainder } 1 \text{ when divided by } 3\}\) and \(\{3n + 1 : n \text{ is a whole number}, n \le 4\}\text{.}\)
