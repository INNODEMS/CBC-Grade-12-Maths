Exercise 2.

\(P\) is on the bisector of angle \(XOY\text{.}\) \(F\) on \(OX\) and \(G\) on \(OY\) are the feet of the perpendiculars from \(P\text{,}\) with \(PF = 8\,\text{cm}\) and \(OP = 17\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Find \(PG\) and \(OF\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(PG = 8\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) \(OF = 15\,\text{cm}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(PG = PF\) because \(P\) is on the bisector. \(OF^2 = 17^2 - 8^2 = 289 - 64 = 225\text{,}\) so \(OF = 15\,\text{cm}\text{.}\)
