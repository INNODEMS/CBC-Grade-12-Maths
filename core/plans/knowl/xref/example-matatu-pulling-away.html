Example 14.6.9 A Matatu Pulling Away.

A matatu pulls away from rest at a stage. For the first \(15\) seconds its acceleration is \(a = 1.8 - 0.12t\,\text{m/s}^2\text{.}\) Find its velocity and its distance from the stage when \(t = 15\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

Step 1: Velocity. \(v = \int (1.8 - 0.12t)\,dt = 1.8t - 0.06t^2 + c\text{.}\) It starts from rest, so \(v = 0\) at \(t = 0\) and \(c = 0\text{:}\) \(v = 1.8t - 0.06t^2\text{.}\)
🔗
Step 2: Displacement. \(s = \int (1.8t - 0.06t^2)\,dt = 0.9t^2 - 0.02t^3 + d\text{,}\) and \(s = 0\) at the stage, so \(d = 0\text{.}\)
🔗
Step 3: At \(t = 15\text{.}\) \(a = 1.8 - 1.8 = 0\text{,}\) so the matatu has stopped speeding up. \(v = 27 - 13.5 = 13.5\,\text{m/s}\) (\(48.6\,\text{km/h}\)) and \(s = 202.5 - 67.5 = 135\,\text{m}\text{.}\)
