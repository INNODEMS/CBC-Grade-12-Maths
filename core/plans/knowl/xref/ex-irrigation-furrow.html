Exercise 4.

An irrigation furrow at Mwea is \(2\,\text{m}\) wide. Its depth \(x\) metres from one edge is \(d = 0.3x(2 - x)\) metres. Use \(4\) strips to find the trapezoidal and mid-ordinate estimates of its cross-section area, and find the exact area by integration.
🔗
Answer.
\(T = 0.375\,\text{m}^2\text{,}\) \(M = 0.4125\,\text{m}^2\text{,}\) exact \(0.4\,\text{m}^2\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(h = 0.5\text{.}\) Depths \(0, 0.225, 0.3, 0.225, 0\text{:}\) \(T = 0.25 \times 2 \times 0.75 = 0.375\text{.}\) Mid-depths \(0.13125, 0.28125, 0.28125, 0.13125\text{,}\) total \(0.825\text{:}\) \(M = 0.4125\text{.}\) Exact: \(\int_0^2 (0.6x - 0.3x^2)\,dx = \left[0.3x^2 - 0.1x^3\right]_0^2 = 1.2 - 0.8 = 0.4\text{.}\)
