Learner Experience 10.7.2 Dividing a Stick.

A stick \(AB\) is \(30\,\text{cm}\) long, with \(A\) at the 0 cm mark and \(B\) at the 30 cm mark of a long ruler.
🔗
  1. Mark the point \(P\) on the stick with \(AP : PB = 2 : 3\text{.}\) How far is \(P\) from \(A\text{?}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    The stick is split into \(2 + 3 = 5\) equal parts of \(6\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) and \(AP\) is \(2\) of them: \(AP = 12\,\text{cm}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Where is the point with \(AP : PB = 1 : 4\text{?}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(\frac{1}{5}\) of the way along: \(6\,\text{cm}\) from \(A\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Now find a point \(Q\) on the ruler beyond \(B\) with \(AQ : QB = 3 : 1\text{.}\) How far is \(Q\) from \(A\text{,}\) and from \(B\text{?}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(AQ\) is three times \(QB\) and \(AQ - QB = AB = 30\text{,}\) so \(QB = 15\,\text{cm}\) and \(AQ = 45\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) \(Q\) is at the 45 cm mark. \(Q\) divides \(AB\) externally.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  4. Find a point \(R\) beyond \(A\) with \(AR : RB = 1 : 3\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(RB\) is three times \(AR\) and \(RB - AR = 30\text{,}\) so \(AR = 15\,\text{cm}\) and \(RB = 45\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) \(R\) is at the \(-15\,\text{cm}\) mark, beyond \(A\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  5. Now the line is in space: \(A(2, -1, 4)\) and \(B(12, 9, -6)\text{.}\) Find the point \(P\) dividing \(AB\) internally in the ratio \(2 : 3\text{,}\) and the point \(Q\) dividing \(AB\) externally in the ratio \(3 : 1\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(\overrightarrow{AB} = \begin{pmatrix} 10 \\ 10 \\ -10 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\) For \(P\text{,}\) go \(\frac{2}{5}\) of the way: \(\overrightarrow{OP} = \overrightarrow{OA} + \frac{2}{5}\overrightarrow{AB} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ -1 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 4 \\ -4 \end{pmatrix}\text{,}\) so \(P(6, 3, 0)\text{.}\) For \(Q\text{,}\) \(AQ = 45 = \frac{3}{2} \times 30\) in part (c), so go \(\frac{3}{2}\) of \(\overrightarrow{AB}\text{:}\) \(\overrightarrow{OQ} = \overrightarrow{OA} + \begin{pmatrix} 15 \\ 15 \\ -15 \end{pmatrix}\text{,}\) so \(Q(17, 14, -11)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
