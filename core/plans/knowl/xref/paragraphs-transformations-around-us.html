Transformations Around Us.

This chapter began with a question: where do we use transformations, and how can a matrix describe one? Here are some answers.
🔗
  • Phones and computers turn, flip, resize and slant pictures, and every one of those edits is a matrix applied to every point of the picture.
    🔗
    🔗
  • Games and animated films move characters by transforming the points of their outlines many times a second.
    🔗
    🔗
  • Kitenge and kanga prints, beadwork and woven baskets repeat one motif by rotating and reflecting it, as in the school banner.
    🔗
    🔗
  • Architects and engineers draw plans to scale, and stretch or shear drawings to show a building from different views.
    🔗
    🔗
  • Map makers shrink the land onto paper; a map that is stretched more one way than the other changes the areas it shows.
    🔗
    🔗
  • A robot arm in a factory turns about its joints, and its controller works out each new position with rotation matrices.
    🔗
