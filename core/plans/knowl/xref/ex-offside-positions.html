Exercise 5.

The second-last defender is \(8\,\text{m}\) from the goal line and the ball is \(15\,\text{m}\) from it. Attackers stand \(6\,\text{m}\text{,}\) \(8\,\text{m}\) and \(9\,\text{m}\) from the goal line, in the opponents’ half. Which are in an offside position?
🔗
Answer.
Only the attacker \(6\,\text{m}\) from the goal line.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The defender is nearer the goal line than the ball, so the offside region is the strip less than \(8\,\text{m}\) from the goal line. The attacker at \(8\,\text{m}\) is level (not offside) and the one at \(9\,\text{m}\) is outside the strip.
