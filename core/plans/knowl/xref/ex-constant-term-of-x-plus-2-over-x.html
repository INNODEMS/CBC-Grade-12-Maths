Exercise 8. Challenge: The Middle Term.

Find the term that does not contain \(x\) in the expansion of \(\left(x + \dfrac{2}{x}\right)^6\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
The powers of \(x\) in the terms are \(6, 4, 2, 0, \ldots\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(160\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The term without \(x\) is number 3 of the row for power 6: \(20 \times x^3 \times \left(\dfrac{2}{x}\right)^3 = 20 \times 8 = 160\text{.}\)
