Exercise 6. Challenge: Percentage Changes.

The number of workers on a job increases by \(60\%\) and the hours they work each day fall by \(25\%\text{.}\) By what percentage does the number of days needed change?
🔗
Answer.
It falls by \(16\tfrac{2}{3}\%\) (about \(16.7\%\)).
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Worker-hours a day are multiplied by \(1.6 \times 0.75 = 1.2\text{,}\) so the days are multiplied by \(\tfrac{1}{1.2} = \tfrac{5}{6}\text{,}\) a fall of \(\tfrac{1}{6}\text{,}\) that is \(16\tfrac{2}{3}\%\text{.}\)
