Exercise 1.

\(AB = 8\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Construct the locus of a point \(P\) on one side of \(AB\) such that \(\angle APB = 45^{\circ}\text{.}\) Give angle \(OAB\text{,}\) angle \(AOB\) and the radius of the arc.
🔗
Answer.
\(\angle OAB = 45^{\circ}\text{,}\) \(\angle AOB = 90^{\circ}\text{,}\) radius \(5.7\,\text{cm}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\angle OAB = 90^{\circ} - 45^{\circ} = 45^{\circ}\) and \(\angle AOB = 2 \times 45^{\circ} = 90^{\circ}\text{.}\) The radius is \(\dfrac{8}{2\sin 45^{\circ}} = 5.66\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) about \(5.7\,\text{cm}\text{.}\)
