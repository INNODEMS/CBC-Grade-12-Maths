Exercise 1.

At lunch, \(58\) learners were asked what they ate last week: \(30\) ate ugali, \(22\) rice and \(22\) chapati. Exactly \(4\) ate ugali and rice only, \(5\) ugali and chapati only, and \(3\) rice and chapati only; \(4\) ate none of the three. How many ate all three? How many ate ugali only?
🔗
Answer.
All three \(4\text{;}\) ugali only \(17\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Let \(x\) eat all three. Ugali only \(21 - x\text{,}\) rice only \(15 - x\text{,}\) chapati only \(14 - x\text{.}\) The sum of the eight regions is \((21 - x) + (15 - x) + (14 - x) + 4 + 5 + 3 + x + 4 = 66 - 2x = 58\text{,}\) so \(x = 4\) and ugali only is \(17\text{.}\)
