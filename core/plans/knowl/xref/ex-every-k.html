Exercise 7. Challenge: Every Value of k.

Show that for every \(k \gt 0\text{,}\) the curve \(y = x^3 - 3k^2x\) has a maximum point at \(x = -k\) and a minimum point at \(x = k\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
\(\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2} = 6x\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 3(x^2 - k^2)\) is \(0\) at \(x = \pm k\text{;}\) \(\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2} = -6k \lt 0\) at \(x = -k\) and \(6k \gt 0\) at \(x = k\text{.}\)
