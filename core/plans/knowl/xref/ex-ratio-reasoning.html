Exercise 6. Challenge: Reasoning with Ratios.

  1. \(X\) divides \(AB\) externally in the ratio \(2 : 1\text{.}\) In what ratio does \(B\) divide \(AX\text{?}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Explain why no point divides \(AB\) externally in the ratio \(1 : 1\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Hint.
In (a), \(\overrightarrow{AX} = 2\overrightarrow{AB}\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
(a) Internally \(1 : 1\text{:}\) \(B\) is the midpoint of \(AX\text{.}\) (b) The only point with \(AP = PB\) is the midpoint, which is inside \(AB\text{;}\) in the formula, \(m - n = 0\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
(a) The fraction is \(\frac{2}{2 - 1} = 2\text{,}\) so \(\overrightarrow{AX} = 2\overrightarrow{AB}\) and \(\overrightarrow{BX} = \overrightarrow{AB}\text{.}\) \(B\) is the midpoint of \(AX\text{:}\) it divides \(AX\) internally in the ratio \(1 : 1\text{.}\) (b) \(AP : PB = 1 : 1\) means \(P\) is the same distance from \(A\) and \(B\text{.}\) On the line \(AB\) the only such point is the midpoint, which divides \(AB\) internally. The external formula would need \(\frac{1}{1 - 1}\text{,}\) which has no value.
