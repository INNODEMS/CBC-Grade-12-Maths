Exercise 7. Challenge: A Hockey Stick.

Work out \(1 + 3 + 6 + 10 + 15\text{.}\) Where does the answer appear in the triangle?
🔗
Hint.
Look one row below the last number, on the other side of the slanting line.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(35\text{,}\) in the row for power 7
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(1 + 3 + 6 + 10 + 15 = 35\text{.}\) The numbers lie on the third slanting line, ending at 15 in the row for power 6; one row below, and one place inwards, is 35 in the row for power 7. The shape of the numbers used looks like a hockey stick.
