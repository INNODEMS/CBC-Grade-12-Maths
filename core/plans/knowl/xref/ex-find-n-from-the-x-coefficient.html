Exercise 7.

The coefficient of \(x\) in the expansion of \((1 + 2x)^n\) is \(16\text{.}\) Find \(n\text{,}\) and then the coefficient of \(x^2\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(n = 8\text{;}\) \(112\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The term in \(x\) is \(n(2x) = 2nx\text{,}\) so \(2n = 16\) and \(n = 8\text{.}\) The term in \(x^2\) is \(28(2x)^2 = 112x^2\text{.}\)
