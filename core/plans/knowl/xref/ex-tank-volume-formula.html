Exercise 5.

Water flows into a tank at \(\dfrac{dV}{dt} = 10t\) litres per minute. Write down \(V\) if the tank was empty at \(t = 0\text{,}\) and if it held \(150\,\text{litres}\) at \(t = 0\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(V = 5t^2\text{;}\) \(V = 5t^2 + 150\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\int 10t\,dt = 5t^2 + c\text{.}\) At \(t = 0\text{,}\) \(V = c\text{,}\) so \(c = 0\) for an empty tank and \(c = 150\) otherwise.
