In the cuboid \(ABCDEFGH\text{,}\) decide whether each pair of lines is parallel, intersecting or skew. (a) \(DH\) and \(BF\) (b) \(AC\) and \(BD\) (c) \(EG\) and \(BC\) (d) \(HB\) and \(DF\)
🔗
Answer.
(a) parallel (b) intersecting, at the centre of the base (c) skew (d) intersecting, at the centre of the cuboid
