Example 5.1.10 Recognising Standard Matrices.

Triangle \(DEF\) has vertices \(D(2, 1)\text{,}\) \(E(4, 1)\) and \(F(4, 3)\text{.}\) Find its images under \(\begin{bmatrix} 0 \amp 1 \\ -1 \amp 0 \end{bmatrix}\) and under \(\begin{bmatrix} -1 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp -1 \end{bmatrix}\text{,}\) and name each transformation.
🔗

Solution.

\begin{equation*} \begin{bmatrix} 0 \amp 1 \\ -1 \amp 0 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 2 \amp 4 \amp 4 \\ 1 \amp 1 \amp 3 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \amp 1 \amp 3 \\ -2 \amp -4 \amp -4 \end{bmatrix} \end{equation*}
The images are \((1, -2)\text{,}\) \((1, -4)\text{,}\) \((3, -4)\text{.}\) Every point \((x, y)\) goes to \((y, -x)\text{:}\) by the Grade 10 rule this is a rotation of \(90^\circ\) clockwise about \(O\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{equation*} \begin{bmatrix} -1 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp -1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 2 \amp 4 \amp 4 \\ 1 \amp 1 \amp 3 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} -2 \amp -4 \amp -4 \\ -1 \amp -1 \amp -3 \end{bmatrix} \end{equation*}
The images are \((-2, -1)\text{,}\) \((-4, -1)\text{,}\) \((-4, -3)\text{.}\) Every point \((x, y)\) goes to \((-x, -y)\text{:}\) a rotation of \(180^\circ\) about \(O\text{.}\)
