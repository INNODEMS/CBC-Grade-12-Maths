Example 14.6.28 Displacement and Distance.

A particle has velocity \(v = 3t^2 - 12t + 9\,\text{m/s}\text{.}\) Find its displacement and the distance it travels from \(t = 0\) to \(t = 4\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

It is at rest when \(3(t - 1)(t - 3) = 0\text{,}\) at \(t = 1\) and \(t = 3\text{.}\) With \(F(t) = t^3 - 6t^2 + 9t\text{,}\) \(F(0) = 0\text{,}\) \(F(1) = 4\text{,}\) \(F(3) = 0\text{,}\) \(F(4) = 4\text{:}\)
\begin{align*} \int_0^1 v\,dt \amp = 4, \qquad \int_1^3 v\,dt = -4, \qquad \int_3^4 v\,dt = 4 \end{align*}
The displacement is \(4 - 4 + 4 = 4\,\text{m}\text{.}\) The distance travelled is \(4 + 4 + 4 = 12\,\text{m}\text{.}\)
