Exercise 2.

It is 13:00 GMT. Find the local time on longitude \(75^\circ\,\text{W}\) and on longitude \(120^\circ\,\text{E}\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
08:00; 21:00
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(75 \times 4 = 300\,\text{min} = 5\,\text{h}\) behind: 08:00. \(120 \times 4 = 480\,\text{min} = 8\,\text{h}\) ahead: 21:00.
