Example 11.5.26 Straight Across the Likoni Channel.

A ferry crosses the Likoni channel at Mombasa at \(2.8\,\text{m/s}\) through the water. The current flows at \(0.7\,\text{m/s}\) along the channel. At what angle upstream must the ferry be steered to go straight across, and what is its ground speed?
🔗

Solution.

The current is at right angles to the required track.
\begin{equation*} \sin d = \frac{0.7}{2.8} = 0.25, \quad d = 14.5^\circ \end{equation*}
upstream. The ground speed is \(\sqrt{2.8^2 - 0.7^2} = \sqrt{7.35} = 2.7\,\text{m/s}\text{.}\)
