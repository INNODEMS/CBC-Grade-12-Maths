Example 10.8.8 Using the Ratio Theorem.

The points \(A\) and \(B\) have position vectors \(\mathbf{a} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ -1 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}\) and \(\mathbf{b} = \begin{pmatrix} 7 \\ 9 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\) Find the point \(P\) that divides \(AB\) internally in the ratio \(3 : 2\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

With \(m = 3\) and \(n = 2\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} \mathbf{p} = \frac{2\mathbf{a} + 3\mathbf{b}}{5} = \frac{1}{5}\left(\begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ -2 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix} 21 \\ 27 \\ -6 \end{pmatrix}\right) = \frac{1}{5}\begin{pmatrix} 25 \\ 25 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 5 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} \end{equation*}
so \(P\) is \((5, 5, 0)\text{.}\)
