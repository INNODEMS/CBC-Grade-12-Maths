Exercise 4.

Otieno found that an area is exactly \(50\text{,}\) and his trapezoidal estimate was \(52\text{.}\) He wrote: “percentage error \(= \frac{2}{52} \times 100\% = 3.85\%\text{.}\)” What is wrong? Give the correct percentage error.
🔗
Answer.
He divided by the estimate; divide by the exact value: \(4\%\text{.}\)
