Exercise 2.

The triangle with vertices \((0, 0)\text{,}\) \((4, 0)\) and \((0, 3)\) is mapped by \(\begin{bmatrix} 5 \amp 2 \\ 1 \amp 1 \end{bmatrix}\text{.}\) Find the area of the image.
🔗
Answer.
\(18\) square units
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The triangle has area \(\frac{1}{2} \times 4 \times 3 = 6\text{.}\) The determinant is \(5 - 2 = 3\text{,}\) so the image has area \(3 \times 6 = 18\) square units.
