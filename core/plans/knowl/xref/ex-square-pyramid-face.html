Exercise 1.

A right pyramid has a square base of side \(14\,\text{cm}\) and height \(9\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Find the angle between a face and the base.
🔗
Answer.
\(52.1^{\circ}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(OM = 7\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) so \(\tan VMO = \dfrac{9}{7}\) and the angle is about \(52.1^{\circ}\text{.}\)
