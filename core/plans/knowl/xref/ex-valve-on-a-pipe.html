Exercise 4.

A water pipe at a school runs straight from a tank \(T(0, 0, 6)\) to a tap \(R(9, 12, 0)\text{,}\) in metres. A valve \(V\) divides \(TR\) internally in the ratio \(1 : 2\text{.}\) Find the position of \(V\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(V(3, 4, 4)\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\mathbf{v} = \dfrac{2\mathbf{t} + \mathbf{r}}{3} = \dfrac{1}{3}\left(\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ 12 \end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix} 9 \\ 12 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}\right) = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 4 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\)
