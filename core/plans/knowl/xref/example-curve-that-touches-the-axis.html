Example 13.2.28 A Curve That Touches the Axis.

Sketch the curve \(y = x(x - 3)^2\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

The curve crosses the axis at \(x = 0\) and touches it at \(x = 3\text{.}\) Expand: \(y = x^3 - 6x^2 + 9x\text{,}\) so \(\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 3x^2 - 12x + 9 = 3(x - 1)(x - 3)\text{.}\)
🔗
At \(x = 1\text{,}\) \(y = 4\text{;}\) at \(x = 3\text{,}\) \(y = 0\text{.}\) \(\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2} = 6x - 12\) is \(-6\) at \(x = 1\) and \(6\) at \(x = 3\text{:}\) \((1, 4)\) is a maximum point and \((3, 0)\) a minimum point on the axis.
