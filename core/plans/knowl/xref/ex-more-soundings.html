Exercise 6. Challenge: More Soundings.

The depth of a river \(20\,\text{m}\) wide is \(d = 0.02x(20 - x)\) metres at \(x\) metres from one bank. Estimate the area of the cross-section with soundings every \(5\,\text{m}\text{,}\) then every \(2.5\,\text{m}\text{.}\) The true area is \(26.67\,\text{m}^2\) (to 2 decimal places). What happens, and why?
🔗
Hint.
Compare the two errors.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(25\,\text{m}^2\) and \(26.25\,\text{m}^2\text{;}\) both too small, the second much closer
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Every \(5\,\text{m}\text{:}\) depths \(0, 1.5, 2, 1.5, 0\text{,}\) so \(\tfrac{1}{2} \times 5 \times 2 \times 5 = 25\text{.}\) Every \(2.5\,\text{m}\text{:}\) depths \(0, 0.875, 1.5, 1.875, 2, 1.875, 1.5, 0.875, 0\text{,}\) so \(\tfrac{1}{2} \times 2.5 \times 2 \times 10.5 = 26.25\text{.}\) Both are too small, because the bed is shaped like a bowl and the chords lie above it. The errors are about \(1.67\) and \(0.42\text{:}\) halving the strip width made the error about a quarter as big.
