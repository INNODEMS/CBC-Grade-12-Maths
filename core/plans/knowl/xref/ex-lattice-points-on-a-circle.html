Exercise 6. Challenge: Whole-Number Points on a Circle.

How many points with whole-number coordinates are exactly \(5\) units from the origin? List them.
🔗
Hint.
Which two squares add to \(25\text{?}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(12\text{:}\) \((\pm 5, 0)\text{,}\) \((0, \pm 5)\text{,}\) \((\pm 3, \pm 4)\text{,}\) \((\pm 4, \pm 3)\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
We need \(x^2 + y^2 = 25\) with whole numbers. The only ways are \(25 + 0\) and \(9 + 16\text{.}\) \(25 + 0\) gives \((\pm 5, 0)\) and \((0, \pm 5)\text{:}\) four points. \(9 + 16\) gives \((\pm 3, \pm 4)\) and \((\pm 4, \pm 3)\text{:}\) eight points. That is \(12\) in all.
