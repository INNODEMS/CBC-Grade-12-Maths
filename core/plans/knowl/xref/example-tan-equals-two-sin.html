Example 9.3.29 Do Not Divide by \(\sin x\).

Solve \(\tan x = 2\sin x\) for \(0^\circ \le x \le 360^\circ\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

Write \(\tan x = \dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x}\) and multiply by \(\cos x\text{:}\) \(\sin x = 2\sin x\cos x\text{.}\) Factorise:
\begin{equation*} \sin x(1 - 2\cos x) = 0 \end{equation*}
\(\sin x = 0\) gives \(0^\circ\text{,}\) \(180^\circ\text{,}\) \(360^\circ\text{;}\) \(\cos x = \dfrac{1}{2}\) gives \(60^\circ\) and \(300^\circ\text{.}\) So \(x \in \{0^\circ, 60^\circ, 180^\circ, 300^\circ, 360^\circ\}\text{.}\)
