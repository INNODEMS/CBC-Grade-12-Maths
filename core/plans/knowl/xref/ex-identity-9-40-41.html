Exercise 2.

A right-angled triangle has sides \(9\text{,}\) \(40\) and \(41\text{,}\) and \(x\) is the angle opposite the side \(9\text{.}\) Write down \(\sin x\) and \(\cos x\text{,}\) and show that \(\sin^2 x + \cos^2 x = 1\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(\sin x = \dfrac{9}{41}\text{,}\) \(\cos x = \dfrac{40}{41}\text{;}\) \(\dfrac{81}{1681} + \dfrac{1600}{1681} = \dfrac{1681}{1681} = 1\text{.}\)
