Exercise 5.

A solar panel \(1.6\,\text{m}\) by \(1.0\,\text{m}\) on a flat roof in Garissa is hinged along its long lower edge and tilted at \(12^{\circ}\) to the roof. How high above the roof is its top edge?
🔗
Answer.
About \(0.21\,\text{m}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The hinge is the common line. The short side, \(1.0\,\text{m}\text{,}\) is at right angles to the hinge, so it makes \(12^{\circ}\) with the roof. The height is \(1.0 \times \sin 12^{\circ} \approx 0.21\,\text{m}\text{.}\)
