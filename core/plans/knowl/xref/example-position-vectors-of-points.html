Example 10.2.11 Position Vectors of Points.

(a) Write the position vector of \(A(3, -1, 4)\text{.}\) (b) The point \(P\) has position vector \(\mathbf{p} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 5 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\) Write the coordinates of \(P\) and say which coordinate plane it lies in.
🔗

Solution.

(a) \(\mathbf{a} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ -1 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}\text{:}\) the coordinates written as a column.
🔗
(b) \(P(0, 5, -2)\text{.}\) Its \(x\)-coordinate is \(0\text{,}\) so \(P\) lies in the \(yz\)-plane.
