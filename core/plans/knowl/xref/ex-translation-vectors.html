Exercise 1.

Give the translation vector that maps \(y = \sin x\) or \(y = \cos x\) onto (a) \(y = \sin(x + 70^\circ)\) (b) \(y = \sin(x - 120^\circ)\) (c) \(y = \sin x + 3\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
(a) \(\begin{pmatrix} -70^\circ \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}\) (b) \(\begin{pmatrix} 120^\circ \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}\) (c) \(\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}\)
