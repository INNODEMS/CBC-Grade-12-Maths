Exercise 7. Challenge (Extension): Height Equals Diameter.

Show that a closed cylinder of fixed volume \(V\) uses the least metal when \(h = 2r\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
Write the surface area in terms of \(r\) only.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(A = 2\pi r^2 + \dfrac{2V}{r}\text{;}\) \(\dfrac{dA}{dr} = 4\pi r - \dfrac{2V}{r^2} = 0\) gives \(V = 2\pi r^3\text{,}\) so \(h = \dfrac{V}{\pi r^2} = 2r\text{.}\)
