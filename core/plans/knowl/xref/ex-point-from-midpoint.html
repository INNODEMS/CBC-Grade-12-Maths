Exercise 5.

\(M(3, 1, 4)\) is the midpoint of \(AB\text{,}\) and \(A\) is \((1, -2, 5)\text{.}\) Find \(B\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(B(5, 4, 3)\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The moves from \(A\) to \(M\) are \(\begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 3 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\) \(B\) is the same moves on from \(M\text{:}\) \((3 + 2, 1 + 3, 4 - 1) = (5, 4, 3)\text{.}\)
