Note 8.6.8 Three-Dimensional Geometry Online.

Free 3D graphing tools on the internet can draw a cuboid or a pyramid and let you turn it to see lines and planes from every side. Use a well-known site that your teacher suggests. You should not need an account: never give your name, phone number, school or other personal details to use such a tool, and ask your teacher before installing anything on a phone or computer.
