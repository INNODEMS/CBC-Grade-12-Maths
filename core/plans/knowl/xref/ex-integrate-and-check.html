Exercise 2.

Find \(\displaystyle\int 2x^3\,dx\) and check your answer by differentiating.
🔗
Answer.
\(\frac{x^4}{2} + c\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\displaystyle\int 2x^3\,dx = \frac{2x^4}{4} + c = \frac{x^4}{2} + c\text{.}\) Check: \(\dfrac{d}{dx}\left(\frac{x^4}{2}\right) = \frac{4x^3}{2} = 2x^3\text{.}\)
