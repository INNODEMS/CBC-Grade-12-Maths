Exercise 4.

In Learner Experience 4.4.2, the price of beans falls, so the profit on beans is only Ksh \(35\,000\) a hectare. What is the best plan now?
🔗
Answer.
\(8\) hectares of maize and \(2\) of beans, Ksh \(390\,000\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(P = 40\,000x + 35\,000y\) at the vertices: \((0, 2)\colon 70\,000\text{,}\) \((8, 2)\colon 390\,000\text{,}\) \((4, 6)\colon 370\,000\text{,}\) \((0, 8.33)\colon\) about \(291\,667\text{.}\) The best plan is now \(8\) hectares of maize and \(2\) of beans, the least beans her promise allows.
