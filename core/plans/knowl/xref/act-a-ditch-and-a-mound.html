Learner Experience 14.3.22 A Ditch and a Mound.

A farmer in Nakuru digs a drainage ditch and piles the soil beside it. Across the ditch and the mound, the ground profile is \(y = \frac{x^2 - 4x}{4}\) metres for \(0 \le x \le 6\text{,}\) where \(y = 0\) is the original ground level.
🔗
  1. For which values of \(x\) is the ground below the original level? How deep is the ditch at its deepest?
    🔗
    Solution.
    For \(0 \lt x \lt 4\text{,}\) where \(x(x - 4) \lt 0\text{.}\) At \(x = 2\text{,}\) \(y = -1\text{:}\) \(1\,\text{m}\) deep.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Evaluate \(\displaystyle\int_{0}^{4} y\,dx\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(\left[ \frac{x^3}{12} - \frac{x^2}{2} \right]_{0}^{4} = \frac{16}{3} - 8 = -\frac{8}{3}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  3. What is the area of the cross-section of the ditch?
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(\frac{8}{3} \approx 2.67\,\text{m}^2\text{:}\) an area is the size of the integral.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  4. Evaluate \(\displaystyle\int_{4}^{6} y\,dx\text{.}\) How high is the mound?
    🔗
    Solution.
    \((18 - 18) - \left(\frac{16}{3} - 8\right) = \frac{8}{3}\text{.}\) At \(x = 6\text{,}\) \(y = 3\text{:}\) \(3\,\text{m}\) high.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  5. Evaluate \(\displaystyle\int_{0}^{6} y\,dx\text{.}\) What does it tell you? What is the total area between the profile and ground level?
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(0\text{:}\) the soil in the mound would exactly fill the ditch. The total area is \(\frac{8}{3} + \frac{8}{3} = \frac{16}{3} \approx 5.33\,\text{m}^2\text{.}\)
