Example 5.3.7 Finding the Object.

The matrix \(M = \begin{bmatrix} 4 \amp 2 \\ 3 \amp 2 \end{bmatrix}\) maps \(P\) onto \(P'(10, 8)\) and \(Q\) onto \(Q'(0, 1)\text{.}\) Find \(P\) and \(Q\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

Step 1: the inverse. \(\det M = 4 \times 2 - 2 \times 3 = 2\text{,}\) so
\begin{equation*} M^{-1} = \frac{1}{2}\begin{bmatrix} 2 \amp -2 \\ -3 \amp 4 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \amp -1 \\ -\frac{3}{2} \amp 2 \end{bmatrix} \end{equation*}
🔗
Step 2: apply it to the images.
\begin{equation*} \begin{bmatrix} 1 \amp -1 \\ -\frac{3}{2} \amp 2 \end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} 10 \amp 0 \\ 8 \amp 1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 2 \amp -1 \\ 1 \amp 2 \end{bmatrix} \end{equation*}
So \(P(2, 1)\) and \(Q(-1, 2)\text{.}\)
🔗
Step 3: check. \(\begin{bmatrix} 4 \amp 2 \\ 3 \amp 2 \end{bmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 10 \\ 8 \end{pmatrix}\text{,}\) which is \(P'\text{.}\)
