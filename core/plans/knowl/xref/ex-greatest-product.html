Exercise 1.

Two positive numbers \(x\) and \(y\) have \(x + y = 12\text{.}\) Find the greatest value of \(xy^2\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(256\text{,}\) when \(x = 4\) and \(y = 8\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(xy^2 = (12 - y)y^2 = 12y^2 - y^3\text{,}\) whose derivative \(24y - 3y^2 = 3y(8 - y)\) is \(0\) at \(y = 8\) (the second derivative \(24 - 6y = -24 \lt 0\)). Then \(x = 4\) and \(xy^2 = 256\text{.}\)
