In triangle \(OAB\text{,}\) \(\overrightarrow{OA} = \mathbf{a}\text{,}\) \(\overrightarrow{OB} = \mathbf{b}\) and \(M\) is the midpoint of \(AB\text{.}\) \(G\) divides \(OM\) internally in the ratio \(2 : 1\text{.}\) Find \(\overrightarrow{OM}\) and \(\overrightarrow{OG}\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(\frac{1}{2}(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b})\text{;}\) \(\frac{1}{3}(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b})\)
