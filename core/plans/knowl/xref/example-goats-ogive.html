Example 12.2.9 Goats at Garissa Market.

The masses, in kg (to the nearest kg), of \(50\) goats at Garissa livestock market are shown below. Draw a cumulative frequency curve.
🔗

Solution.

The masses are to the nearest kg, so the class 15–19 runs from \(14.5\) to \(19.5\,\text{kg}\text{,}\) and so on.
🔗
Plot \((14.5, 0)\) and then each upper class boundary against its cumulative frequency, and join the points.
