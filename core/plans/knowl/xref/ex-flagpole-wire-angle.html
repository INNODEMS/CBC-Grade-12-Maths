Exercise 4.

A vertical flagpole at a school in Nyeri is \(8\,\text{m}\) high. A wire runs from its top to a peg on level ground \(5.5\,\text{m}\) from its foot. Find the angle between the wire and the ground.
🔗
Answer.
\(55.5^{\circ}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The projection of the wire is the \(5.5\,\text{m}\) line from the peg to the foot of the pole. \(\tan \theta = \dfrac{8}{5.5}\text{,}\) so \(\theta \approx 55.5^{\circ}\text{.}\)
