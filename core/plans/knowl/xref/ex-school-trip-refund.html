Three schools paid Ksh \(30\,000\text{,}\) Ksh \(50\,000\) and Ksh \(70\,000\) for a joint trip to Hell’s Gate. A refund of Ksh \(12\,000\) is shared in proportion to what they paid. How much does each school get back?
🔗
Answer.
\(3 : 5 : 7\text{;}\) Ksh \(2400\text{,}\) Ksh \(4000\) and Ksh \(5600\)
