Exercise 4.

An athlete warming up at Iten accelerates steadily from rest to \(6\,\text{m/s}\) in \(4\) seconds, runs at \(6\,\text{m/s}\) for \(20\) seconds, then slows steadily to rest in \(6\) seconds. Draw the velocity-time graph and find the distance she runs.
🔗
Answer.
\(150\,\text{m}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\frac{1}{2} \times 4 \times 6 + 20 \times 6 + \frac{1}{2} \times 6 \times 6 = 12 + 120 + 18 = 150\,\text{m}\text{.}\)
