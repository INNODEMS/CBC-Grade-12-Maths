Example 7.3.10 Lines on a Grid.

Write each locus as the equations of lines.
🔗
  1. The points \(3\) units from the line \(y = 1\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. The points \(5\) units from the line \(x = -2\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. The points equidistant from the lines \(y = 1\) and \(y = 7\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗

Solution.

(a) Go \(3\) units up and \(3\) units down from \(y = 1\text{:}\) the lines \(y = 4\) and \(y = -2\text{.}\)
🔗
(b) Go \(5\) units right and \(5\) units left from \(x = -2\text{:}\) the lines \(x = 3\) and \(x = -7\text{.}\)
🔗
(c) The line halfway between them: \(y = \frac{1 + 7}{2} = 4\text{.}\)
🔗
