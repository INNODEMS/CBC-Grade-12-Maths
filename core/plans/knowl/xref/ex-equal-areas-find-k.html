Exercise 7. Challenge: Equal Areas.

The area under \(y = 3x^2 + k\) from \(x = 0\) to \(x = 2\) equals the area under the same curve from \(x = 2\) to \(x = 3\text{,}\) where \(k\) is a positive constant. Find \(k\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
Write both areas in terms of \(k\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(k = 11\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\int_{0}^{2} (3x^2 + k)\,dx = 8 + 2k\) and \(\int_{2}^{3} (3x^2 + k)\,dx = 19 + k\text{.}\) So \(8 + 2k = 19 + k\) and \(k = 11\text{.}\)
