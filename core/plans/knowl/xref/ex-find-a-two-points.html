Exercise 6. Challenge: Two Points, Two Unknowns.

A curve has \(\dfrac{dy}{dx} = ax^2 + 2\text{,}\) where \(a\) is a constant. It passes through \((0, 1)\) and \((3, 25)\text{.}\) Find \(a\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
First use \((0, 1)\) to find the constant of integration.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(a = 2\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(y = \frac{ax^3}{3} + 2x + c\text{.}\) At \((0, 1)\text{:}\) \(c = 1\text{.}\) At \((3, 25)\text{:}\) \(9a + 6 + 1 = 25\text{,}\) so \(a = 2\text{.}\)
