Example 9.4.26 A Translation Along the \(x\)-Axis.

Describe the transformation that maps \(y = \sin x\) onto \(y = \sin(x - 45^\circ)\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

The translation \(\begin{pmatrix} 45^\circ \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}\text{,}\) that is \(45^\circ\) to the right. The phase angle is \(-45^\circ\text{.}\)
