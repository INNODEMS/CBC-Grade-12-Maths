Learner Experience 8.4.2 A Pyramid from Manila Paper.

The net below makes a pyramid \(VABC\text{.}\) Its base \(ABC\) has a right angle at \(A\text{,}\) with \(AB = 9\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) \(AC = 12\,\text{cm}\) and \(BC = 15\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) The faces \(VAB\) and \(VAC\) also have right angles at \(A\text{,}\) and \(VA = 8\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) When the net is folded, the edge \(VA\) stands vertically above the corner \(A\text{,}\) like the corner of a box sliced off.
🔗
  1. Draw the net on manila paper, cut it out and fold it into the pyramid. Glue the edges with tabs.
    🔗
    Solution.
    The three faces that meet at \(A\) fold up so that \(VA\) is vertical. The face \(VBC\) closes the pyramid.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Measure angle \(VBA\text{,}\) the angle between the edges \(BV\) and \(BA\text{.}\) Then calculate it in triangle \(VAB\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Triangle \(VAB\) has a right angle at \(A\text{:}\) \(\tan VBA = \dfrac{8}{9}\text{,}\) so angle \(VBA \approx 41.6^{\circ}\text{.}\) Measurements of about \(40^{\circ}\) to \(43^{\circ}\) are good.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Measure angle \(BVC\text{,}\) the angle between the edges \(VB\) and \(VC\) at the top.
    🔗
    Solution.
    About \(68^{\circ}\text{.}\) Triangle \(BVC\) has no right angle, so tan, sin and cos alone will not give it.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  4. Calculate \(VB\) and \(VC\) by Pythagoras. Then use the cosine rule in triangle \(BVC\text{,}\) with \(BC = 15\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) to calculate angle \(BVC\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(VB = \sqrt{9^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{145} \approx 12.04\,\text{cm}\) and \(VC = \sqrt{12^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{208} \approx 14.42\,\text{cm}\text{.}\)
    \begin{equation*} \cos BVC = \frac{145 + 208 - 225}{2\sqrt{145}\sqrt{208}} = \frac{128}{2\sqrt{30\,160}} \approx 0.3685 \end{equation*}
    so angle \(BVC \approx 68.4^{\circ}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  5. Write your measured and calculated angles side by side. Why might they differ?
    🔗
    Solution.
    Measurements differ by a degree or two because of cutting, folding and reading the protractor. Report the values as found; the calculated values are the more accurate.
