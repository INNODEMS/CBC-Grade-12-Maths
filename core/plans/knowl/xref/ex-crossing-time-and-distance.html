Exercise 5. Challenge: Time to Cross.

A river is \(80\,\text{m}\) wide. A boat is steered straight across at \(1.6\,\text{m/s}\) through the water, and the current is \(0.9\,\text{m/s}\text{.}\) How long does the crossing take, and how far downstream does the boat land?
🔗
Hint.
The current does not change the time to cross.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(50\,\text{s}\text{;}\) \(45\,\text{m}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Across the river the boat moves at \(1.6\,\text{m/s}\text{:}\) \(80 \div 1.6 = 50\,\text{s}\text{.}\) Downstream it moves at \(0.9\,\text{m/s}\) for \(50\,\text{s}\text{:}\) \(45\,\text{m}\text{.}\)
