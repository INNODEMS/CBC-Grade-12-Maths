Exercise 7. Challenge: At Rest but Never Turning.

Show that a particle with \(s = t^3 - 3t^2 + 3t\) is at rest once but never changes direction.
🔗
Hint.
Factorise \(v\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(v = 3t^2 - 6t + 3 = 3(t - 1)^2\text{.}\) It is \(0\) only at \(t = 1\text{,}\) and never negative, so the particle always moves in the positive direction.
