Exercise 3.

Without calculating, say whether the trapezoidal rule overestimates, underestimates or gives the exact area for:
  1. \(y = 2x^3\) from \(x = 1\) to \(x = 3\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(y = 5 - 2x\) from \(x = 0\) to \(x = 2\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(y = 9 - x^2\) from \(x = 0\) to \(x = 3\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Answer.
(a) overestimates; (b) exact; (c) underestimates
🔗
🔗
Solution.
(a) \(\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} = 12x \gt 0\text{:}\) bends upwards. (b) A straight line. (c) \(\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} = -2\text{:}\) bends downwards.
