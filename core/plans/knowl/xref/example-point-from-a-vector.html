Example 10.2.13 Finding a Point from a Vector.

(a) \(A\) is \((1, -2, 3)\) and \(\overrightarrow{AB} = \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 5 \\ -6 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\) Find \(B\text{.}\)
🔗
(b) \(ABCD\) is a parallelogram with \(A(1, 0, 2)\text{,}\) \(B(4, 1, 0)\) and \(C(6, 5, 3)\text{.}\) Find \(D\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

(a) \(\mathbf{b} = \mathbf{a} + \overrightarrow{AB} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -2 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 5 \\ -6 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 3 \\ -3 \end{pmatrix}\text{,}\) so \(B\) is \((5, 3, -3)\text{.}\)
🔗
(b) In the parallelogram \(ABCD\text{,}\) the sides \(AD\) and \(BC\) are opposite, so \(\overrightarrow{AD} = \overrightarrow{BC}\text{.}\) Now \(\overrightarrow{BC} = \mathbf{c} - \mathbf{b} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 4 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}\text{,}\) so
\begin{equation*} \mathbf{d} = \mathbf{a} + \overrightarrow{AD} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 4 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 4 \\ 5 \end{pmatrix} \end{equation*}
and \(D\) is \((3, 4, 5)\text{.}\)
