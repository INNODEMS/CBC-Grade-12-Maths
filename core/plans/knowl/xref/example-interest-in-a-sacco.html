Example 2.3.28 Interest in a SACCO.

Ksh \(50\,000\) is saved in a SACCO in Nyeri at \(3\%\) a year, added at the end of each year. Estimate the amount after 4 years using (a) three terms (b) four terms of the expansion.
🔗

Solution.

The amount is \(50\,000(1.03)^4\text{.}\) With \(x = 0.03\text{,}\) \((1 + x)^4 = 1 + 4x + 6x^2 + 4x^3 + x^4\text{.}\)
🔗
(a) \(50\,000(1 + 0.12 + 0.0054) = 50\,000 \times 1.1254\) = Ksh \(56\,270\text{.}\)
🔗
(b) Adding \(4x^3 = 0.000108\text{:}\) \(50\,000 \times 1.125508\) = Ksh \(56\,275.40\text{.}\) The SACCO statement shows Ksh \(56\,275.44\text{.}\)
