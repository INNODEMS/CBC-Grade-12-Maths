Exercise 2.

A circle has centre \((1, -3)\) and radius \(17\text{.}\) Decide whether each point is inside, on or outside it: \((9, 12)\text{,}\) \((-7, 5)\text{,}\) \((16, 6)\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\((9, 12)\) on; \((-7, 5)\) inside; \((16, 6)\) outside.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Compare with \(17^2 = 289\text{:}\) \(8^2 + 15^2 = 289\) (on); \(8^2 + 8^2 = 128\) (inside); \(15^2 + 9^2 = 306\) (outside).
