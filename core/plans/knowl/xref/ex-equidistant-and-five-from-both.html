Exercise 1.

\(AB = 6\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Construct the points that are \(5\,\text{cm}\) from both \(A\) and \(B\text{.}\) How far are they from \(AB\text{?}\)
🔗
Answer.
Two points, one on each side of \(AB\text{,}\) each \(4\,\text{cm}\) from \(AB\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Both points are on the perpendicular bisector of \(AB\text{,}\) \(3\,\text{cm}\) from each end along \(AB\text{,}\) so their distance from \(AB\) is \(\sqrt{5^2 - 3^2} = 4\,\text{cm}\text{.}\)
