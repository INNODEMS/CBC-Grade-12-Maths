Exercise 2.

A plan of a plot in Embu is drawn on squares that stand for \(5\,\text{m}\) by \(5\,\text{m}\text{.}\) The plot covers \(40\) full and \(26\) part squares. Estimate its area.
🔗
Answer.
\(1325\,\text{m}^2\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(40 + \tfrac{1}{2} \times 26 = 53\) squares; one square is \(5 \times 5 = 25\,\text{m}^2\text{;}\) area about \(53 \times 25 = 1325\,\text{m}^2\text{.}\)
