Exercise 5.

A curve has \(\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 2x + k\text{,}\) where \(k\) is a constant. It passes through \((0, 1)\) and \((2, 9)\text{.}\) Find \(k\) and the equation of the curve.
🔗
Answer.
\(k = 2\text{;}\) \(y = x^2 + 2x + 1\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(y = x^2 + kx + c\text{.}\) At \((0, 1)\text{:}\) \(c = 1\text{.}\) At \((2, 9)\text{:}\) \(4 + 2k + 1 = 9\text{,}\) so \(k = 2\text{.}\)
