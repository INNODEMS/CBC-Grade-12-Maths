Example 8.5.9 A Ramp.

A wheelchair ramp at a health centre in Kakamega is a triangular prism \(ABCDEF\text{.}\) The base \(ABED\) is horizontal, the face \(BCFE\) is vertical and \(ACFD\) is the sloping face. \(AB = 3.6\,\text{m}\text{,}\) the rise \(BC = 0.3\,\text{m}\) and the width \(AD = 1.5\,\text{m}\text{.}\) Find the angle between the base and (a) the line \(AF\text{,}\) from a bottom corner to the far top corner; (b) the line \(AC\text{,}\) straight up the slope.
🔗
Solution.
(a) \(F\) is vertically above \(E\text{,}\) so the projection of \(AF\) on the ground is \(AE\text{.}\) \(AE = \sqrt{3.6^2 + 1.5^2} = \sqrt{15.21} = 3.9\,\text{m}\text{.}\) In triangle \(AEF\text{,}\) with the right angle at \(E\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} \tan FAE = \frac{0.3}{3.9} \end{equation*}
so the angle is about \(4.4^{\circ}\text{.}\)
🔗
(b) \(C\) is above \(B\text{,}\) so the projection of \(AC\) is \(AB\text{:}\) \(\tan CAB = \frac{0.3}{3.6}\text{,}\) and the angle is about \(4.8^{\circ}\text{.}\) \(AC\text{,}\) at right angles to the bottom edge \(AD\text{,}\) is the steepest line on the ramp. A common guideline for wheelchair ramps is a rise of at most \(1\) in \(12\text{,}\) about \(4.8^{\circ}\text{,}\) measured along a line like \(AC\text{:}\) this ramp just meets it.
