Exercise 3.

The masses, in kg, of \(8\) goats are \(32, 35, 29, 38, 34, 31, 36, 33\text{.}\) Find the mean mass using an assumed mean of \(33\,\text{kg}\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(33.5\,\text{kg}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(d = -1, 2, -4, 5, 1, -2, 3, 0\text{,}\) so \(\Sigma d = 4\) and \(\bar{x} = 33 + 4 \div 8 = 33.5\,\text{kg}\text{.}\)
