Exercise 3.

The masses, in g (to the nearest g), of \(60\) tomatoes from a farm in Kirinyaga are shown below. Find the median and the quartiles.
🔗
Mass (g) 50–59 60–69 70–79 80–89 90–99 100–109
Frequency (\(f\)) 6 10 17 13 8 6
Answer.
\(Q_1 = 68.5\,\text{g}\text{,}\) median \(\approx 77.7\,\text{g}\text{,}\) \(Q_3 \approx 88.7\,\text{g}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Cumulative frequencies \(6, 16, 33, 46, 54, 60\text{;}\) positions \(15, 30, 45\text{.}\) \(Q_1 = 59.5 + \dfrac{15 - 6}{10} \times 10 = 68.5\text{;}\) median \(= 69.5 + \dfrac{30 - 16}{17} \times 10 = 77.74\text{;}\) \(Q_3 = 79.5 + \dfrac{45 - 33}{13} \times 10 = 88.73\text{.}\)
