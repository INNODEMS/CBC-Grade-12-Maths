Exercise 6.

The milk collected each day at a cooling centre in Ol Kalou, \(N\) hundred litres, \(t\) months after the start of January, is modelled by \(N = 2t^3 - 21t^2 + 60t + 300\) for \(0 \le t \le 6\text{.}\) Find and classify the stationary points, and say what they mean.
🔗
Answer.
\((2, 352)\) maximum point; \((5, 325)\) minimum point. The most milk, about \(35\,200\,\text{litres}\) a day, comes at the start of March; the least, about \(32\,500\,\text{litres}\) a day, at the start of June.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\dfrac{dN}{dt} = 6t^2 - 42t + 60 = 6(t - 2)(t - 5)\text{.}\) \(N(2) = 16 - 84 + 120 + 300 = 352\) and \(N(5) = 250 - 525 + 300 + 300 = 325\text{.}\) \(\dfrac{d^2N}{dt^2} = 12t - 42\) is \(-18\) at \(t = 2\) and \(18\) at \(t = 5\text{.}\)
