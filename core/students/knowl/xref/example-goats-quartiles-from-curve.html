Example 12.3.24 Goats: Quartiles from the Curve.

Use the cumulative frequency curve of the goat masses (Figure 12.2.9) to estimate the median, the quartiles, the interquartile range and the quartile deviation.
🔗
Solution.
With \(n = 50\text{,}\) go across from \(12.5\text{,}\) \(25\) and \(37.5\text{.}\)
🔗
\(Q_1 \approx 25.5\,\text{kg}\text{,}\) median \(\approx 30.6\,\text{kg}\) and \(Q_3 \approx 35.3\,\text{kg}\text{,}\) the same as the values calculated in Example 12.3.5. So the interquartile range is about \(35.33 - 25.54 = 9.8\,\text{kg}\) and the quartile deviation about \(4.9\,\text{kg}\text{.}\) The middle half of the goats weigh between about \(25.5\) and \(35.3\,\text{kg}\text{.}\)
