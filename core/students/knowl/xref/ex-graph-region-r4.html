Exercise 3.

Draw the region R given by \(8x + 7y \le 112\text{,}\) \(2x + 7y \le 70\text{,}\) \(x \ge 0\) and \(y \ge 0\text{.}\) Label R.
🔗
Answer.
Boundaries through \((14, 0)\) and \((0, 16)\text{,}\) and through \((35, 0)\) and \((0, 10)\text{,}\) both solid; R is the four-sided region next to the origin
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(8x + 7y = 112\) passes through \((14, 0)\) and \((0, 16)\text{;}\) \(2x + 7y = 70\) passes through \((35, 0)\) and \((0, 10)\text{.}\) Both are solid, and \((0, 0)\) satisfies both, so shade the side of each away from the origin. Shade left of the \(y\)-axis and below the \(x\)-axis.
