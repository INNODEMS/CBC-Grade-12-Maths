Exercise 2.

Find the vertices of the region \(7x + 4y \le 84\text{,}\) \(x + 8y \le 64\text{,}\) \(x \ge 0\text{,}\) \(y \ge 0\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\((0, 0)\text{,}\) \((12, 0)\text{,}\) \((8, 7)\text{,}\) \((0, 8)\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Double the first equation: \(14x + 8y = 168\text{.}\) Subtract \(x + 8y = 64\text{:}\) \(13x = 104\text{,}\) so \(x = 8\) and \(y = 7\text{.}\) The axis intercepts that bound R are \((12, 0)\) on \(7x + 4y = 84\) and \((0, 8)\) on \(x + 8y = 64\text{.}\) The vertices are \((0, 0)\text{,}\) \((12, 0)\text{,}\) \((8, 7)\) and \((0, 8)\text{.}\)
