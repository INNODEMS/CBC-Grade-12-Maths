Note 7.2.3 Shading a Region.

In this book, when a diagram shows a region, the parts that are not wanted are shaded and the required region is left clear. A key always says so: “shaded, not wanted; unshaded, the required region”. This is the usual practice in Kenyan examinations. In Grade 11 the wanted region was shaded instead; both ways are correct when a key says which, so always read the key.
