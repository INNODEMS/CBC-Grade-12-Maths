Learner Experience 2.1.2 Multiplying Out on Manila Paper.

Work in your group on a sheet of manila paper.
  1. Draw a grid with \(a\) and \(b\) along the top and \(a\) and \(b\) down the side. Fill in each cell with the product of its row and column, and use it to expand \((a + b)^2\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Multiply your answer by \((a + b)\text{:}\) draw a grid with \(a^2\text{,}\) \(2ab\text{,}\) \(b^2\) along the top and \(a\text{,}\) \(b\) down the side. Mark the like terms with the same colour and the same shape, then collect them.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Expand \((a + b)^4\) the same way, by multiplying your answer to (b) by \((a + b)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. Write the coefficients of \((a + b)^1\text{,}\) \((a + b)^2\text{,}\) \((a + b)^3\) and \((a + b)^4\) in rows, one under another and centred. Describe three patterns you can see.
    🔗
    🔗
  5. Predict the coefficients of \((a + b)^5\) without multiplying.
