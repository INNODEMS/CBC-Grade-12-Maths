Example 1.6.3 Solar Lamps and Water Tanks.

Of \(70\) homes in a village, \(41\) have a solar lamp, \(33\) have a water tank and \(5\) have neither. How many have both?
🔗
Solution.
Let \(x\) homes have both. Then \((41 - x) + x + (33 - x) + 5 = 70\text{,}\) so \(79 - x = 70\) and \(x = 9\text{.}\) Nine homes have both.
