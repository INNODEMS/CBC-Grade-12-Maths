Learner Experience 8.6.3 Slicing a Salary.

Use Table 8.6.2. A worker’s taxable pay is Ksh \(50\,000\) a month.
🔗
  1. How much of the Ksh \(50\,000\) falls in each band?
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(24\,000\) in the \(10\%\) band, \(8333\) in the \(25\%\) band and \(50\,000 - 32\,333 = 17\,667\) in the \(30\%\) band.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Find the tax on each slice.
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(2400\text{;}\) \(8333 \times 0.25 = 2083.25\text{;}\) \(17\,667 \times 0.3 = 5300.10\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Find the total tax.
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(2400 + 2083.25 + 5300.10 = 9783.35\text{,}\) Ksh \(9783.35\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  4. Subtract the personal relief. This is the PAYE.
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(9783.35 - 2400 = 7383.35\text{,}\) Ksh \(7383.35\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  5. Is the PAYE \(30\%\) of \(50\,000\text{?}\) Explain.
    🔗
    Solution.
    No. \(30\%\) of \(50\,000\) is \(15\,000\text{.}\) Only the top slice, above \(32\,333\text{,}\) is taxed at \(30\%\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
