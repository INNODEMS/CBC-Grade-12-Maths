Exercise 5.

A lorry trip needs \(14\) hours of driving, and one driver may drive at most \(8\) hours a day. (a) How many days does the trip take with one driver? (b) How many hours does each drive if two drivers share the driving equally?
🔗
Answer.
(a) \(2\) days (b) \(7\) hours each, so the trip can be done in one day
