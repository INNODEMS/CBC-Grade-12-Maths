Exercise 1.

P is at \((12^\circ\,\text{N},\ 30^\circ\,\text{E})\) and Q is at \((25^\circ\,\text{N},\ 30^\circ\,\text{E})\text{.}\) Find the distance PQ along the meridian in nautical miles and in kilometres.
🔗
Answer.
\(780\,\text{nm}\text{;}\) \(1445.9\,\text{km}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Difference in latitude \(25^\circ - 12^\circ = 13^\circ = 780'\text{,}\) so \(780\,\text{nm}\text{.}\) \(\tfrac{13}{360} \times 40\,040 = 1445.9\,\text{km}\text{.}\)
