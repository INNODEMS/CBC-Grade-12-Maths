Exercise 2.

\(55\) hawkers each sell at least one of fruit, vegetables and eggs. \(32\) sell fruit, \(25\) vegetables and \(25\) eggs; \(11\) sell fruit and vegetables, \(12\) fruit and eggs, and \(9\) vegetables and eggs. How many sell all three?
🔗
Answer.
\(5\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
With \(x\) in the centre, the only regions are \(9 + x\text{,}\) \(5 + x\) and \(4 + x\) and the exactly-two regions are \(11 - x\text{,}\) \(12 - x\) and \(9 - x\text{.}\) The seven regions add to \(50 + x = 55\text{,}\) so \(x = 5\text{.}\)
