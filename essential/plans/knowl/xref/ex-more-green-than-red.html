Exercise 7. Challenge: More Green Than Red.

In Learner Experience 10.5.22, find the probability that the matatu meets more green lights than red.
🔗
Hint.
Which paths have more G than R?
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(0.648\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Three green (GGG, \(0.216\)) or exactly two green (GGR, GRG, RGG, \(3 \times 0.144 = 0.432\)): \(0.216 + 0.432 = 0.648\text{.}\)
