Learner Experience 9.1.3 Heights in Our Group.

A group of 10 Grade 12 learners at a school in Nyeri measured their heights, in centimetres.
🔗
  1. Find the total of the heights and the mean height.
    🔗
    Solution.
    Total \(1656\,\text{cm}\text{;}\) mean \(1656 \div 10 = 165.6\,\text{cm}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Subtract the mean from each height. Add the ten answers. What do you notice?
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(-3.6, -7.6, 5.4, -0.6, 2.4, -6.6, 8.4, -2.6, 0.4, 4.4\text{;}\) the sum is \(0\text{.}\) It is always \(0\text{,}\) because the mean balances the data: the amounts above it cancel the amounts below it.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Every learner in the group stands on a block \(5\,\text{cm}\) high. Without adding ten new heights, what is the new mean?
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(165.6 + 5 = 170.6\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) Adding \(5\) to each of the ten heights adds \(50\) to the total, and so \(5\) to the mean.
    🔗
