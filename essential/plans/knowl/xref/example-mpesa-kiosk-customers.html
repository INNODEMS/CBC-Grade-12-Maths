Example 10.2.30 Customers at an M-Pesa Kiosk.

Let \(X\) be the number of customers who arrive at an M-Pesa kiosk in Kisumu in a 5-minute period.
🔗
Find (a) \(p\text{,}\) (b) \(P(X \ge 2)\text{,}\) (c) \(P(X \lt 2)\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

(a) The probabilities add to 1: \(0.15 + 0.3 + 0.25 + p + 0.1 = 1\text{,}\) so \(0.8 + p = 1\) and \(p = 0.2\text{.}\)
🔗
(b) \(P(X \ge 2) = 0.25 + 0.2 + 0.1 = 0.55\text{.}\)
🔗
(c) \(P(X \lt 2) = 0.15 + 0.3 = 0.45\text{,}\) which is also \(1 - 0.55\text{.}\)
