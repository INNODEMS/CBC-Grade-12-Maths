Example 10.3.8 A Coin and a Die.

A coin is tossed and a die is rolled. Find the probability of a head and a number greater than 4.
🔗

Solution.

The coin and the die are independent. \(P(\text{head}) = \frac{1}{2}\) and \(P(\text{greater than } 4) = \frac{2}{6} = \frac{1}{3}\text{,}\) so
\begin{equation*} P(\text{head and greater than } 4) = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{6} \end{equation*}
Check with the list of \(12\) outcomes in Learner Experience 10.1.22: H5 and H6 give \(\frac{2}{12} = \frac{1}{6}\text{.}\)
