Example 6.1.12 There and Back on a Straight Road.

Kiprop jogs \(1.5\,\text{km}\) east along a straight road from his home to the market, then turns and jogs \(0.6\,\text{km}\) back west (Figure 6.1.11). Find the distance he jogs and his displacement from home.
🔗

Solution.

The distance counts both legs: \(1.5 + 0.6 = 2.1\,\text{km}\text{.}\)
🔗
The displacement compares the finish with home. He went \(1.5\,\text{km}\) east and came back \(0.6\,\text{km}\text{,}\) so he finishes \(1.5 - 0.6 = 0.9\,\text{km}\) east of home. His displacement is \(0.9\,\text{km}\) east: a size and a direction.
