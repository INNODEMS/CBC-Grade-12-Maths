Example 2.1.27 Sharing a Repair Bill.

Three neighbours keep \(4\text{,}\) \(10\) and \(12\) cows that drink from a shared borehole. They share a repair bill of Ksh \(26\,000\) in proportion to the number of cows. How much does each pay?
🔗

Solution.

The ratio \(4 : 10 : 12\) simplifies to \(2 : 5 : 6\text{,}\) which is \(13\) parts. One part is \(26\,000 \div 13 = 2000\text{.}\)
🔗
They pay Ksh \(4000\text{,}\) Ksh \(10\,000\) and Ksh \(12\,000\text{.}\)
