Example 6.5.8 Multiples of a Vector.

\(\mathbf{a} = \begin{pmatrix} -4 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\) Find \(3\mathbf{a}\text{,}\) \(-\mathbf{a}\) and \(\tfrac{1}{2}\mathbf{a}\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

Multiply every component:
🔗
\begin{equation*} 3\mathbf{a} = \begin{pmatrix} -12 \\ 18 \end{pmatrix}, \quad -\mathbf{a} = \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ -6 \end{pmatrix}, \quad \tfrac{1}{2}\mathbf{a} = \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} \end{equation*}
🔗
\(3\mathbf{a}\) and \(\tfrac{1}{2}\mathbf{a}\) point the same way as \(\mathbf{a}\text{;}\) \(-\mathbf{a}\) points the opposite way.
