Example 8.3.8 Five Years On.

A plot in Nanyuki bought for Ksh \(1\,200\,000\) appreciates at \(12\%\) a year. Find its value after \(5\) years and the appreciation, to the nearest shilling.
🔗

Solution.

\begin{equation*} V = 1\,200\,000 \times 1.12^5 = 2\,114\,810.02 \end{equation*}
The value is Ksh \(2\,114\,810\) and the appreciation is \(2\,114\,810 - 1\,200\,000 = 914\,810\text{,}\) Ksh \(914\,810\text{.}\)
