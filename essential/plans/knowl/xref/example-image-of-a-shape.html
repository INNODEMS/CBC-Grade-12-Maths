Example 6.7.8 Image of a Shape.

The quadrilateral \(PQRS\) has vertices \(P(-3, 1)\text{,}\) \(Q(0, 1)\text{,}\) \(R(0, 3)\) and \(S(-2, 4)\text{.}\) Find its image under the translation by the vector \(\begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ -3 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

Add \(4\) to each \(x\)-coordinate and \(-3\) to each \(y\)-coordinate:
🔗
\(P'(1, -2)\text{,}\) \(Q'(4, -2)\text{,}\) \(R'(4, 0)\text{,}\) \(S'(2, 1)\text{.}\)
