Example 8.3.9 Rate from Two Valuations.

A house in Nakuru was valued at Ksh \(2\,400\,000\) and a year later at Ksh \(2\,700\,000\text{.}\) Find the rate of appreciation.
🔗

Solution.

The increase is \(300\,000\text{,}\) so the rate is
\begin{equation*} \frac{300\,000}{2\,400\,000} \times 100 = 12.5\% \end{equation*}
Divide by the value at the start of the year, not the new value.
