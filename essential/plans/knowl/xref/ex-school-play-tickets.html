Exercise 1.

Adult tickets for a school play cost Ksh \(200\) and learner tickets cost Ksh \(70\text{.}\) The drama club needs takings of at least Ksh \(7000\text{.}\) Write an inequality for \(a\) adult and \(l\) learner tickets, and simplify it.
🔗
Answer.
\(200a + 70l \ge 7000\text{,}\) which simplifies to \(20a + 7l \ge 700\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Takings are \(200a\) from adults and \(70l\) from learners. “At least” gives \(\ge\text{:}\) \(200a + 70l \ge 7000\text{.}\) Divide every term by \(10\text{:}\) \(20a + 7l \ge 700\text{.}\)
