Exercise 3.

Solve \(-6 \le 4x + 2 \lt 18\) and list the integers that satisfy it.
🔗
Answer.
\(-2 \le x \lt 4\text{;}\) the integers are \(-2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3\) (six integers).
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Subtract \(2\text{:}\) \(-8 \le 4x \lt 16\text{.}\) Divide by \(4\text{:}\) \(-2 \le x \lt 4\text{.}\) \(-2\) is included and \(4\) is not.
