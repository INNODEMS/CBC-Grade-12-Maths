Exercise 5.

\(28\,\text{litres}\) of milk and water for chai are in the ratio \(5 : 2\text{.}\) How much milk must be added to make the ratio \(7 : 2\text{?}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(8\,\text{litres}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Milk \(20\,\text{litres}\) and water \(8\,\text{litres}\text{.}\) With \(8\,\text{litres}\) of water, \(7 : 2\) needs \(28\,\text{litres}\) of milk, so add \(8\,\text{litres}\text{.}\)
