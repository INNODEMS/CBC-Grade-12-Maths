Exercise 5.

Halima’s first walk has displacement \(\begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\) After a second walk her total displacement is \(\begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\) Find the displacement of the second walk.
🔗
Answer.
\(\begin{pmatrix} -4 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The first walk plus the second walk is the total, so the second walk is \(\begin{pmatrix} -1 - 3 \\ 4 - (-2) \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -4 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\) Check: \(\begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix} -4 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\)
