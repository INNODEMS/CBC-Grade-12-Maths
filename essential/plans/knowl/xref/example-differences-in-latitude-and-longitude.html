Example 7.1.30 Differences in Latitude and Longitude.

Take Addis Ababa to be at \((9^\circ 02'\,\text{N},\ 38^\circ 45'\,\text{E})\text{,}\) Dar es Salaam at \((6^\circ 48'\,\text{S},\ 39^\circ 17'\,\text{E})\) and Accra at \((5^\circ 33'\,\text{N},\ 0^\circ 12'\,\text{W})\text{.}\)
  1. Find the differences in latitude and in longitude of Addis Ababa and Dar es Salaam.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Find the difference in longitude of Accra and Nairobi \((1^\circ 17'\,\text{S},\ 36^\circ 49'\,\text{E})\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗

Solution.

  1. Latitude: one is north and one south of the equator, so add: \(9^\circ 02' + 6^\circ 48' = 15^\circ 50'\text{.}\) Longitude: both are east, so subtract. The minutes \(17' - 45'\) do not subtract, so borrow \(1^\circ = 60'\text{:}\)
    \begin{equation*} 39^\circ 17' - 38^\circ 45' = 38^\circ 77' - 38^\circ 45' = 0^\circ 32' \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Accra is west of Greenwich and Nairobi east, so add: \(0^\circ 12' + 36^\circ 49' = 36^\circ 61' = 37^\circ 01'\text{.}\)
