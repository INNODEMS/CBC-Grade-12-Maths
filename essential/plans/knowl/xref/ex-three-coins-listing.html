Exercise 4.

Three coins are tossed. List the \(8\) outcomes. Find the probability of (a) exactly two heads, (b) at least one tail.
🔗
Answer.
HHH, HHT, HTH, HTT, THH, THT, TTH, TTT; (a) \(\frac{3}{8}\) (b) \(\frac{7}{8}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
(a) HHT, HTH, THH. (b) Every outcome except HHH: \(1 - \frac{1}{8} = \frac{7}{8}\text{.}\)
