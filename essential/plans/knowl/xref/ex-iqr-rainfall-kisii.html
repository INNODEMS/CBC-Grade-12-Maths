Exercise 2.

The daily rainfall, in mm, on \(11\) days in Kisii was \(3.2, 4.5, 2.8, 5.1, 3.9, 4.2, 3.6, 4.8, 3.3, 4.0, 4.6\text{.}\) Find the range, the interquartile range and the quartile deviation.
🔗
Answer.
Range \(2.3\,\text{mm}\text{;}\) \(Q_1 = 3.3\text{,}\) \(Q_3 = 4.6\text{;}\) IQR \(1.3\,\text{mm}\text{;}\) QD \(0.65\,\text{mm}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
In order: \(2.8, 3.2, 3.3, 3.6, 3.9, 4.0, 4.2, 4.5, 4.6, 4.8, 5.1\text{.}\) Range \(5.1 - 2.8 = 2.3\text{.}\) Positions \(3\) and \(9\text{:}\) \(Q_1 = 3.3\text{,}\) \(Q_3 = 4.6\text{.}\)
