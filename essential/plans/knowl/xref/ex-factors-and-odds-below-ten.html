\(\xi = \{x : x \text{ is a whole number}, x \lt 10\}\text{,}\) \(P\) is the set of factors of \(6\) and \(Q\) is the set of odd numbers in \(\xi\text{.}\) List \(P - Q\text{,}\) \(Q - P\) and \((P \cup Q)'\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(P - Q = \{2, 6\}\text{;}\) \(Q - P = \{5, 7, 9\}\text{;}\) \((P \cup Q)' = \{0, 4, 8\}\)
