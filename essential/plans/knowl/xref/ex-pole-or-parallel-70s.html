Exercise 5.

P is at \((70^\circ\,\text{S},\ 25^\circ\,\text{E})\) and Q at \((70^\circ\,\text{S},\ 155^\circ\,\text{W})\text{.}\) Compare the distance from P to Q along the parallel with the distance over the South Pole.
🔗
Answer.
Parallel \(3693.8\,\text{nm}\text{;}\) over the pole \(2400\,\text{nm}\text{;}\) the route over the pole is shorter.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(25 + 155 = 180\text{:}\) opposite meridians. Along the parallel: \(10\,800 \cos 70^\circ = 3693.8\,\text{nm}\text{.}\) Over the pole: \(2 \times (90^\circ - 70^\circ) = 40^\circ = 2400'\text{,}\) so \(2400\,\text{nm}\text{.}\)
