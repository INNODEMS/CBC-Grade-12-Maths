Example 10.3.7 Getting to School.

Each learner at a school in Machakos travels to school in one way only. For a learner chosen at random, \(P(\text{walks}) = 0.4\text{,}\) \(P(\text{matatu}) = 0.3\) and \(P(\text{boda boda}) = 0.18\text{;}\) the rest cycle. Find (a) \(P(\text{bicycle})\text{,}\) (b) \(P(\text{walks or cycles})\text{,}\) (c) \(P(\text{does not travel by matatu})\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

The four ways are mutually exclusive and one of them must happen, so their probabilities add to 1.
🔗
(a) \(P(\text{bicycle}) = 1 - (0.4 + 0.3 + 0.18) = 1 - 0.88 = 0.12\text{.}\)
🔗
(b) Addition law: \(0.4 + 0.12 = 0.52\text{.}\)
🔗
(c) Complement: \(1 - 0.3 = 0.7\text{.}\)
