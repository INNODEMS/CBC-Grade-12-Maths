Exercise 5.

\(900\) learners use \(27\,000\,\text{litres}\) of water in \(5\) days. How much do \(1200\) learners use in \(7\) days?
🔗
Answer.
\(50\,400\,\text{litres}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
One learner uses \(27\,000 \div (900 \times 5) = 6\,\text{litres}\) a day, so \(1200 \times 7 \times 6 = 50\,400\text{.}\)
