Exercise 6. Challenge: Below Half.

A motorbike depreciates at \(15\%\) a year. After how many whole years is it first worth less than half its price?
🔗
Hint.
Find the first power of \(0.85\) that is less than \(0.5\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(5\) years
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(0.85^4 = 0.522\text{,}\) more than half; \(0.85^5 = 0.444\text{,}\) less than half. So after \(5\) years, whatever the price.
