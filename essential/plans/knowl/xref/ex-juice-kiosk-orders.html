Exercise 1.

At a juice kiosk in Nakuru, each customer orders one juice. A customer orders mango juice with probability \(0.25\) and passion juice with probability \(0.4\text{;}\) every other customer orders avocado juice. Find (a) \(P(\text{mango or passion})\text{,}\) (b) \(P(\text{avocado})\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
(a) \(0.65\) (b) \(0.35\)
