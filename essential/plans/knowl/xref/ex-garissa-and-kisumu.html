Exercise 1.

Take Garissa to be on longitude \(40^\circ\,\text{E}\) and Kisumu on \(35^\circ\,\text{E}\text{.}\) It is 09:00 local time in Garissa. What is the local time in Kisumu?
🔗
Answer.
08:40
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Difference in longitude \(40^\circ - 35^\circ = 5^\circ\text{,}\) and \(5 \times 4 = 20\) minutes of time. Kisumu is west of Garissa, so it is behind: \(09{:}00 - 20\) minutes gives 08:40.
