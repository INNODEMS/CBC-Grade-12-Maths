Exercise 5. Challenge: Where Great Circles Meet.

Explain why any two different great circles on a sphere cross at exactly two points, and why those two points are at opposite ends of a diameter.
🔗
Hint.
Both planes pass through the centre.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
The two planes meet in a line through the centre, which meets the sphere at the two ends of a diameter.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The planes of the two great circles are different, and both contain the centre \(O\text{.}\) Two different planes that share a point meet in a straight line, so they meet in a line through \(O\text{.}\) The circles cross where this line meets the sphere: at two points, each a distance \(R\) from \(O\) on opposite sides of it, that is, at the two ends of a diameter.
