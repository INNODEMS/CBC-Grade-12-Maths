Exercise 2.

Write the inequality for \(R\) in panel (c).
🔗
Answer.
\(3x + 7y \gt 21\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The line through \((7, 0)\) and \((0, 3)\) is \(3x + 7y = 21\text{,}\) dashed. \(R\) is away from the origin; the origin gives \(0\text{,}\) which is less than \(21\text{,}\) so \(R\) has \(3x + 7y \gt 21\text{.}\)
