Learner Experience 8.1.21 SACCO, Bank or Phone?

Wafula needs Ksh \(10\,000\) for \(6\) months.
🔗
  1. His SACCO charges \(12\%\) per annum simple interest. How much interest would he pay?
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(\dfrac{10\,000 \times 12 \times 0.5}{100} = 600\text{,}\) so Ksh \(600\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  2. A bank charges \(16\%\) per annum simple interest. How much interest would he pay?
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(\dfrac{10\,000 \times 16 \times 0.5}{100} = 800\text{,}\) so Ksh \(800\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  3. A mobile lender charges \(7.5\%\) of the loan for each month. How much would he pay for \(6\) months?
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(7.5\%\) of \(10\,000\) is \(750\) a month, so \(6 \times 750 = 4500\text{,}\) Ksh \(4500\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  4. What yearly rate is the mobile lender’s charge?
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(7.5 \times 12 = 90\%\) per annum.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  5. Which would you choose, and why might someone still use the mobile lender?
    🔗
    Solution.
    The SACCO is cheapest. Some people use the mobile lender because it is quick and needs no guarantor, but they pay a very high price for that.
