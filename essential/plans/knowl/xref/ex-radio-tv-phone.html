Exercise 3.

In a survey of \(80\) households in Kakamega, \(52\) have a radio, \(30\) a television and \(41\) a smartphone; \(18\) have a radio and a television, \(24\) a radio and a smartphone, and \(14\) a television and a smartphone; \(9\) have all three. How many have a radio only? Exactly two of the three? None of them?
🔗
Answer.
Radio only \(19\text{;}\) exactly two \(29\text{;}\) none \(4\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Centre \(9\text{.}\) Exactly two: \(18 - 9 = 9\text{,}\) \(24 - 9 = 15\text{,}\) \(14 - 9 = 5\text{,}\) total \(29\text{.}\) Only: radio \(52 - 9 - 15 - 9 = 19\text{,}\) television \(30 - 9 - 5 - 9 = 7\text{,}\) smartphone \(41 - 15 - 5 - 9 = 12\text{.}\) Inner regions \(19 + 7 + 12 + 29 + 9 = 76\text{,}\) so none \(= 80 - 76 = 4\text{.}\)
