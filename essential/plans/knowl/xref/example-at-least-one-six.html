Example 10.4.28 At Least One Six.

Two dice are rolled. Find the probability of at least one 6.
🔗

Solution.

\(P(\text{no } 6) = \frac{5}{6} \times \frac{5}{6} = \frac{25}{36}\text{,}\) so \(P(\text{at least one } 6) = 1 - \frac{25}{36} = \frac{11}{36}\text{.}\) Check by counting the \(11\) shaded cells of Figure 10.4.24.
