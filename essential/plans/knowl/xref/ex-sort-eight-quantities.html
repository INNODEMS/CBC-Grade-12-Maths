Exercise 1.

Sort these into scalar quantities and vector quantities: the length of a football pitch, \(100\,\text{m}\text{;}\) a river current of \(2\,\text{m/s}\) downstream; the wind’s force on a sail, \(300\) N towards the north; the cost of a litre of milk, Ksh \(60\text{;}\) a ship \(40\,\text{km}\) south-east of Mombasa, measured from Mombasa; the time to boil water, \(5\) minutes; the speed of sound, \(340\,\text{m/s}\text{;}\) the weight of a bag of cement.
🔗
Answer.
Scalars: the length, the cost, the time, the speed of sound. Vectors: the current, the wind’s force, the ship’s displacement, the weight.
