Exercise 6. Challenge: A Wet Weekend?

Kioko says: “The probability of rain on Saturday is \(0.6\) and on Sunday is \(0.6\text{,}\) so the probability of rain at the weekend is \(1.2\text{.}\)” What is wrong? Assuming the two days are independent, find the probability of rain on at least one of the two days.
🔗
Hint.
First find the probability of no rain on either day.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
The events are not mutually exclusive (it can rain on both days), and no probability can be \(1.2\text{.}\) The probability is \(0.84\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
P(no rain on either day) \(= 0.4 \times 0.4 = 0.16\text{,}\) so P(rain on at least one day) \(= 1 - 0.16 = 0.84\text{.}\)
