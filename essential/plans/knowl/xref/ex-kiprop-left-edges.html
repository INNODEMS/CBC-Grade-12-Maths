Exercise 4.

Kiprop estimated the area under \(y = x^2 + x + 1\) from \(x = 0\) to \(x = 3\) with \(3\) strips, using the heights at \(x = 0, 1, 2\text{.}\) What did he get, and what is wrong?
🔗
Answer.
\(11\text{;}\) he used the left edges of the strips, not the middles; the mid-ordinate estimate is \(16.25\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Kiprop: \(1 \times (1 + 3 + 7) = 11\text{.}\) These are the heights at the left edges of the strips, which for a rising curve are the lowest points of each strip. The mid-ordinates are at \(0.5, 1.5, 2.5\text{,}\) giving \(16.25\) (Exercise 5.3.1.1).
