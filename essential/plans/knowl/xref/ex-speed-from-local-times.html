Exercise 3.

A plane leaves longitude \(10^\circ\,\text{W}\) on the equator at 08:00 local time and flies due east along the equator to longitude \(26^\circ\,\text{E}\text{,}\) arriving at 15:24 local time. Find its speed in knots.
🔗
Answer.
\(432\,\text{knots}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Difference in longitude \(10^\circ + 26^\circ = 36^\circ\text{,}\) so \(2160\,\text{nm}\text{,}\) and local time gains \(36 \times 4 = 144\,\text{min} = 2\,\text{h}\ 24\,\text{min}\text{.}\) The local times differ by \(7\,\text{h}\ 24\,\text{min}\text{,}\) so the flight takes \(5\,\text{h}\text{.}\) Speed \(2160 \div 5 = 432\,\text{knots}\text{.}\)
