Example 8.6.27 A Nurse’s Pay.

A nurse in Mombasa has a gross pay of Ksh \(92\,000\) a month. Her pension contribution of Ksh \(4500\) is taken off before tax, and her other deductions total Ksh \(9800\text{.}\) Find her taxable pay, PAYE and net pay.
🔗

Solution.

Taxable pay \(= 92\,000 - 4500 = 87\,500\text{.}\)
\begin{equation*} \text{tax} = 4483.25 + (87\,500 - 32\,333) \times 0.3 = 4483.25 + 16\,550.10 = 21\,033.35 \end{equation*}
PAYE \(= 21\,033.35 - 2400 = 18\,633.35\text{.}\) Net pay \(= 92\,000 - 4500 - 18\,633.35 - 9800 = 59\,066.65\text{,}\) Ksh \(59\,066.65\text{.}\)
