Exercise 1.

A plane leaves longitude \(20^\circ\,\text{E}\) on the equator at 06:00 local time and flies due west along the equator at \(400\,\text{knots}\) to longitude \(10^\circ\,\text{W}\text{.}\) Find the local time when it arrives.
🔗
Answer.
08:30
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Difference in longitude \(20^\circ + 10^\circ = 30^\circ\text{,}\) so \(1800\,\text{nm}\text{.}\) Time \(1800 \div 400 = 4.5\,\text{h} = 4\,\text{h}\ 30\,\text{min}\text{;}\) starting clock \(10{:}30\text{.}\) West by \(30^\circ\text{:}\) \(120\,\text{min}\) behind, so 08:30 local time.
