Exercise 5.

A graph has a boda boda rider’s age \(a\) years across (\(0\) to \(60\)) and the rider’s speed in town \(s\,\text{km/h}\) up (\(0\) to \(80\)). \(R\) is a clear rectangle: on the left the solid line \(a = 18\text{,}\) on top the solid line \(s = 50\text{,}\) at the bottom \(s = 0\text{.}\) Write the inequalities and say what rules they show.
🔗
Answer.
\(a \ge 18\) and \(0 \le s \le 50\text{:}\) riders must be at least \(18\) years old, and must keep to \(50\,\text{km/h}\) or less in town.
