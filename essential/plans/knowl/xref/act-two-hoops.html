Learner Experience 1.4.2 Two Hoops.

Seven learners take part: Amani, Bahati, Cherono, Dalmas, Kevo, Zawadi and Lulu. The football players are \(F = \{\text{Amani}, \text{Cherono}, \text{Dalmas}, \text{Kevo}\}\) and the netball players are \(N = \{\text{Bahati}, \text{Cherono}, \text{Kevo}, \text{Zawadi}\}\text{.}\) Each learner stands inside every hoop that fits them.
🔗
  1. Who stands inside both hoops?
    🔗
    Solution.
    Cherono and Kevo.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  2. List everyone standing inside at least one hoop.
    🔗
    Solution.
    Amani, Bahati, Cherono, Dalmas, Kevo and Zawadi.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Who stands outside both hoops?
    🔗
    Solution.
    Lulu.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  4. \(n(F) + n(N) = 8\text{,}\) but only \(6\) learners stand inside the hoops. Why?
    🔗
    Solution.
    Cherono and Kevo are in both sets, so adding \(n(F)\) and \(n(N)\) counts them twice.
