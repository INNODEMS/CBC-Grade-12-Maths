\(\mathbf{p} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix}\text{,}\) \(\mathbf{q} = \begin{pmatrix} -5 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}\) and \(\mathbf{r} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\) Find \(\mathbf{p} + \mathbf{q} + \mathbf{r}\text{,}\) then \(\mathbf{q} + \mathbf{r} + \mathbf{p}\text{.}\) What do you notice?
🔗
Answer.
Both are \(\begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix}\text{:}\) the order does not matter.
