Exercise 6. Challenge: Two Walks to the Library.

Akinyi walks straight from the school gate to the library, \(300\,\text{m}\) east. Baraka walks \(200\,\text{m}\) west first, then turns and walks east to the library. Compare their distances and their displacements.
🔗
Hint.
Displacement depends only on the start and the finish.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
Akinyi walks \(300\,\text{m}\) and Baraka \(700\,\text{m}\text{;}\) both displacements are \(300\,\text{m}\) east.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Baraka walks \(200\,\text{m}\) west and then \(200 + 300 = 500\,\text{m}\) east, \(700\,\text{m}\) in all. Both start at the gate and finish at the library, so both displacements are \(300\,\text{m}\) east.
