Exercise 1.

Classify each pair as mutually exclusive, independent or neither, with a reason. (a) A learner chosen at random is in Grade 10 / is in Grade 12. (b) A coin shows heads / a spinner in another room shows red. (c) A card from cards numbered 1 to 20 is even / is a multiple of 4. (d) It is sunny in Garissa / Wafula’s die shows 3.
🔗
Answer.
(a) mutually exclusive (b) independent (c) neither (d) independent
🔗
🔗
Solution.
(a) No learner is in both grades. (b) and (d) Separate experiments that cannot affect each other. (c) 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20 are both, and every multiple of 4 is even, so knowing one changes the probability of the other.
