Exercise 5.

A bag holds red and blue beads only. The probability of taking a red bead is \(\frac{3}{8}\text{,}\) and there are \(15\) blue beads. How many red beads are there?
🔗
Answer.
\(9\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(P(\text{blue}) = 1 - \frac{3}{8} = \frac{5}{8}\text{,}\) so \(15\) beads are \(\frac{5}{8}\) of the bag. The bag holds \(15 \div 5 \times 8 = 24\) beads, of which \(24 - 15 = 9\) are red.
