Exercise 1.

A farmer keeps \(x\) goats and \(y\) sheep. Write each condition as an inequality.
🔗
  1. There are at most \(45\) animals.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. There are at least \(8\) goats.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. There are more than \(12\) sheep.
    🔗
    🔗
  4. There are fewer than \(28\) animals.
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Answer.
(a) \(x + y \le 45\text{;}\) (b) \(x \ge 8\text{;}\) (c) \(y \gt 12\text{;}\) (d) \(x + y \lt 28\)
