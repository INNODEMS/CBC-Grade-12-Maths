Exercise 6. Challenge: Three Years at an Equal Rate.

A plot rose in value from Ksh \(1\,000\,000\) to Ksh \(1\,331\,000\) in \(3\) years at an equal yearly rate. Find the rate.
🔗
Hint.
Use the cube root.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(10\%\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\left(1 + \dfrac{r}{100}\right)^3 = 1.331\) and \(\sqrt[3]{1.331} = 1.1\text{,}\) so \(r = 10\text{.}\)
