Exercise 5. Challenge: Two Years as One.

Interest is \(6.25\%\) per annum compound. What single percentage increase is the same as two years of this interest?
🔗
Hint.
Square the multiplier.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(12.9\%\) (not \(12.5\%\))
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(1.0625^2 = 1.12890625\text{,}\) an increase of \(12.890625\%\text{,}\) about \(12.9\%\text{.}\) It is more than \(2 \times 6.25 = 12.5\%\) because the second year’s interest is also paid on the first year’s interest.
