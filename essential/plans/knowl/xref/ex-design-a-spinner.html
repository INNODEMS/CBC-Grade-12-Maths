Exercise 6. Challenge: Design a Spinner.

A spinner has \(8\) equal sectors. Colour each sector red, blue or white so that \(P(\text{red}) = 2 \times P(\text{blue})\) and \(P(\text{not white}) = \frac{3}{4}\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
Count sectors: how many are not white?
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(4\) red, \(2\) blue, \(2\) white
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\frac{3}{4}\) of \(8\) is \(6\text{,}\) so \(6\) sectors are red or blue and \(2\) are white. Red has twice as many sectors as blue, so \(4\) red and \(2\) blue.
