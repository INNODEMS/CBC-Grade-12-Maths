Exercise 4.

Quiz marks out of \(20\) have mean \(12.5\) and standard deviation \(3.2\text{.}\) Each mark is changed to a percentage (multiplied by \(5\)), and then \(3\) bonus marks are added. Find the new mean and standard deviation.
🔗
Answer.
Mean \(65.5\text{;}\) standard deviation \(16\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Mean \(5 \times 12.5 + 3 = 65.5\text{.}\) The standard deviation is multiplied by \(5\) and not changed by adding: \(5 \times 3.2 = 16\text{.}\)
