Exercise 3.

Two points on the equator are at \(10^\circ\,\text{W}\) and \(65^\circ\,\text{E}\text{.}\) Find the distance between them along the equator in nautical miles and in kilometres.
🔗
Answer.
\(4500\,\text{nm}\text{;}\) \(8341.7\,\text{km}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Opposite sides of Greenwich: \(10^\circ + 65^\circ = 75^\circ = 4500'\text{,}\) so \(4500\,\text{nm}\text{.}\) \(\tfrac{75}{360} \times 40\,040 = 8341.7\,\text{km}\text{.}\)
