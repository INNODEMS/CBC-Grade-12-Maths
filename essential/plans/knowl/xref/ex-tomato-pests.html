The probability that a tomato plant is attacked by pests is \(0.3\text{.}\) Two plants grow in separate shambas, independently. Draw a tree diagram and find the probability that (a) both are attacked, (b) exactly one is attacked, (c) neither is attacked.
🔗
Answer.
(a) \(0.09\) (b) \(0.42\) (c) \(0.49\)
