Exercise 5.

Kamau walks along a straight east–west road, first east and then back west. He walks \(8\,\text{km}\) altogether and ends \(2\,\text{km}\) east of home. How far east did he go before turning?
🔗
Answer.
\(5\,\text{km}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
If he went \(x\) km east, he came back \(8 - x\) km, so \(x - (8 - x) = 2\text{,}\) giving \(2x = 10\) and \(x = 5\text{.}\) Check: \(5\,\text{km}\) east and \(3\,\text{km}\) back west is \(8\,\text{km}\text{,}\) ending \(2\,\text{km}\) east of home.
