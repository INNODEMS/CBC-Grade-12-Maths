Exercise 3.

Three bean seeds are planted. Each germinates with probability \(0.8\text{,}\) independently. Find the probability that (a) all three germinate, (b) exactly two germinate, (c) at least one germinates.
🔗
Answer.
(a) \(0.512\) (b) \(0.384\) (c) \(0.992\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
(a) \(0.8^3 = 0.512\text{.}\) (b) \(3 \times 0.8 \times 0.8 \times 0.2 = 0.384\text{.}\) (c) \(1 - 0.2^3 = 0.992\text{.}\)
