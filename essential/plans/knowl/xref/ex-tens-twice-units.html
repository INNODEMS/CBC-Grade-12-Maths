Exercise 5.

List the set of two-digit numbers whose tens digit is twice the units digit.
🔗
Answer.
\(\{21, 42, 63, 84\}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The units digit can be \(1\text{,}\) \(2\text{,}\) \(3\) or \(4\) (a units digit of \(5\) would need a tens digit of \(10\text{,}\) and a units digit of \(0\) gives the one-digit number \(0\)). So the numbers are \(21\text{,}\) \(42\text{,}\) \(63\) and \(84\text{.}\)
