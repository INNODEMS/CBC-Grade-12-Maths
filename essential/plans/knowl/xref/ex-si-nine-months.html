Exercise 3.

Find the simple interest on Ksh \(8400\) at \(10\%\) per annum for \(9\) months, and the amount.
🔗
Answer.
Interest Ksh \(630\text{;}\) amount Ksh \(9030\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(T = \dfrac{9}{12}\text{,}\) so \(I = \dfrac{8400 \times 10 \times 9}{100 \times 12} = 630\text{.}\) The amount is \(8400 + 630 = 9030\text{.}\)
