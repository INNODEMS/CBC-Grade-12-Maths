Example 2.2.7 Iron Sheets.

\(6\) iron sheets cost Ksh \(5100\text{.}\) Find the cost of \(14\) sheets.
🔗

Solution.

More sheets cost more: direct proportion.
🔗
\(6\) sheets cost Ksh \(5100\text{.}\)
🔗
1 sheet costs \(5100 \div 6 = 850\text{,}\) Ksh \(850\text{.}\)
🔗
\(14\) sheets cost \(14 \times 850 = 11\,900\text{,}\) Ksh \(11\,900\text{.}\)
