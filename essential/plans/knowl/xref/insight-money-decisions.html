Key Takeaway 8.6.29 Money Decisions.

What this chapter means for everyday money decisions:
  • Borrowing costs money: compare yearly rates before you borrow.
    🔗
    🔗
  • Saving grows money, and compound interest grows it faster the longer you leave it.
    🔗
    🔗
  • Things you own gain or lose value: land often appreciates; motorbikes, phones and machines depreciate.
    🔗
    🔗
  • Tax pays for schools, roads and hospitals. Every worker’s PAYE, paid honestly, is a share in the public services we all use.
