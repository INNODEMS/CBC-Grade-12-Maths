Exercise 6. Challenge: Design a Fair Game.

Two players roll two dice. Show that the rule “A wins if the total is even, otherwise B wins” gives a fair game. Then find a fair rule that uses the difference between the scores instead. Is the rule “A wins if the total is 7 or less” fair?
🔗
Hint.
Count cells in the grid.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
Even total: \(\frac{18}{36}\text{,}\) fair. “A wins if the difference is odd” is fair (\(10 + 6 + 2 = 18\) cells). “Total 7 or less” is not fair: \(\frac{21}{36}\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Even totals 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 fill \(1 + 3 + 5 + 5 + 3 + 1 = 18\) cells, half of \(36\text{.}\) Differences 1, 3, 5 fill \(10 + 6 + 2 = 18\) cells. Totals 2 to 7 fill \(1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6 = 21\) cells, so A wins with probability \(\frac{21}{36} = \frac{7}{12}\text{.}\)
