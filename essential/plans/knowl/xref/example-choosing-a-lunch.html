Example 10.1.29 Choosing a Lunch.

A school canteen offers githeri, pilau, or ugali with sukuma wiki, and a drink of tea or juice. A learner who likes everything chooses one food and one drink at random. List the possible meals, and find the probability that the learner chooses (a) pilau and juice, (b) a meal without githeri.
🔗

Solution.

Fix the food and run through the drinks: githeri and tea, githeri and juice, pilau and tea, pilau and juice, ugali and tea, ugali and juice. There are \(3 \times 2 = 6\) meals.
🔗
(a) One meal: \(\dfrac{1}{6}\text{.}\)
🔗
(b) Four meals have no githeri: \(\dfrac{4}{6} = \dfrac{2}{3}\text{.}\)
