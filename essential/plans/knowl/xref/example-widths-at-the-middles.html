Example 5.3.10 Widths at the Middles.

A banana plot lies beside a straight path \(50\,\text{m}\) long. Its width was measured at the middles of five strips, each \(10\,\text{m}\) wide, that is at \(5, 15, 25, 35\) and \(45\,\text{m}\) along the path: \(22, 26, 27, 24, 18\) metres. Estimate the area of the plot.
🔗

Solution.

The widths are already the mid-ordinates, with \(h = 10\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} A \approx 10 \times (22 + 26 + 27 + 24 + 18) = 10 \times 117 = 1170 \end{equation*}
The plot is about \(1170\,\text{m}^2\text{.}\) No widths at the ends of the path are needed for this rule.
