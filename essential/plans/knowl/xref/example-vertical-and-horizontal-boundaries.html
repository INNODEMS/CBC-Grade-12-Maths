Example 3.3.9 Vertical and Horizontal Boundaries.

On separate axes, show (a) \(y \lt 3\text{;}\) (b) \(x \ge -1\text{,}\) shading the unwanted region.
🔗

Solution.

(a) The boundary is the horizontal line \(y = 3\text{,}\) drawn dashed because the sign is \(\lt\text{.}\) The points with \(y \lt 3\) are below it, so shade above the line, where \(y \ge 3\text{.}\)
🔗
(b) The boundary is the vertical line \(x = -1\text{,}\) drawn solid because the sign is \(\ge\text{.}\) The points with \(x \ge -1\) are on and to the right of it, so shade to the left, where \(x \lt -1\text{.}\)
