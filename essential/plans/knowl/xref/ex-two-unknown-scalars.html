Exercise 6. Challenge: Two Unknown Scalars.

Find \(m\) and \(n\) such that \(m\begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} + n\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 7 \\ 11 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
Compare the top numbers, then the bottom numbers.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(m = 2\text{,}\) \(n = 3\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Top: \(2m + n = 7\text{.}\) Bottom: \(m + 3n = 11\text{.}\) From the first, \(n = 7 - 2m\text{;}\) then \(m + 21 - 6m = 11\text{,}\) so \(m = 2\) and \(n = 3\text{.}\) Check: \(\begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 9 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 7 \\ 11 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\)
