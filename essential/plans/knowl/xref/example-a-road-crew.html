Example 2.2.24 A Road Crew.

\(18\) workers, working \(8\) hours a day, gravel a road in \(15\) days. How many days would \(24\) workers take, working \(9\) hours a day?
🔗

Solution.

\(18\) workers working \(8\) hours a day take \(15\) days.
🔗
1 worker working \(8\) hours a day takes \(18 \times 15 = 270\) days (fewer workers, more days).
🔗
1 worker working 1 hour a day takes \(8 \times 270 = 2160\) days (fewer hours, more days).
🔗
So the job is \(2160\) worker-hours. \(24\) workers working \(9\) hours a day give \(24 \times 9 = 216\) worker-hours a day, so they take \(2160 \div 216 = 10\) days.
🔗
