Exercise 4. Challenge: Working Backwards.

\(B\) is the point \((-1, 5)\) and \(\overrightarrow{AB} = \begin{pmatrix} -3 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\) Where is \(A\text{?}\)
🔗
Hint.
Go from \(B\) the opposite way.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(A(2, 1)\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\overrightarrow{BA} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ -4 \end{pmatrix}\text{,}\) so from \(B(-1, 5)\) move \(3\) right and \(4\) down: \(A(2, 1)\text{.}\) Check: from \((2, 1)\) to \((-1, 5)\) is \(3\) left and \(4\) up.
