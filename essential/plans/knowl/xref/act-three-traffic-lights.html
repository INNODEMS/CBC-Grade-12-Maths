Learner Experience 10.5.22 Three Traffic Lights.

Nafula’s matatu passes three traffic lights on the way to town. Each light is green when the matatu reaches it with probability \(0.6\) and red otherwise, independently.
🔗
  1. Draw a tree diagram with three stages. How many paths are there?
    🔗
    Solution.
    Eight paths, from GGG to RRR (see Figure 10.5.25).
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Find the probability that all three lights are green.
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(0.6^3 = 0.216\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Find the probability that no light is green.
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(0.4^3 = 0.064\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  4. Find the probability that exactly one light is red.
    🔗
    Solution.
    Paths GGR, GRG, RGG, each \(0.6 \times 0.6 \times 0.4 = 0.144\text{:}\) \(3 \times 0.144 = 0.432\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  5. Find the probability that at least one light is red.
    🔗
    Solution.
    Everything except GGG: \(1 - 0.216 = 0.784\text{.}\)
