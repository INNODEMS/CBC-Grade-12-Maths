Example 8.4.8 A School Laptop.

A school buys a laptop for Ksh \(65\,000\text{.}\) It depreciates at \(20\%\) a year. Find its value after \(3\) years and the depreciation.
🔗

Solution.

The multiplier is \(1 - 0.2 = 0.8\text{.}\)
\begin{equation*} V = 65\,000 \times 0.8^3 = 65\,000 \times 0.512 = 33\,280 \end{equation*}
The value is Ksh \(33\,280\) and the depreciation is \(65\,000 - 33\,280 = 31\,720\text{,}\) Ksh \(31\,720\text{.}\)
