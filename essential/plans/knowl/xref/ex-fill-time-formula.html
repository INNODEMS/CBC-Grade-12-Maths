Exercise 6. Challenge: A Formula for the Time.

Show that an inlet that fills a tank in \(a\) hours and an outlet that empties it in \(c\) hours fill the empty tank in \(\dfrac{ac}{c - a}\) hours, when \(c \gt a\text{.}\) What happens when \(c \le a\text{?}\)
🔗
Answer.
The net rate is \(\dfrac{c - a}{ac}\text{;}\) if \(c \le a\) the tank never fills (the net rate is \(0\) or negative).
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\dfrac{1}{a} - \dfrac{1}{c} = \dfrac{c - a}{ac}\text{,}\) so the time is \(\dfrac{ac}{c - a}\text{.}\) If \(c = a\) the net rate is \(0\) and the level stays where it is; if \(c \lt a\) the outlet is faster and the net rate is negative: the tank never fills.
