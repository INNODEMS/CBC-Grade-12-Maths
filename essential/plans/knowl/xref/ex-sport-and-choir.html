Exercise 3.

Of \(40\) learners, \(26\) play a sport, \(18\) sing in the choir and \(7\) do neither. How many do both? How many play a sport only? How many sing in the choir only?
🔗
Answer.
Both \(11\text{;}\) sport only \(15\text{;}\) choir only \(7\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(n(S \cup C) = 40 - 7 = 33\text{,}\) so both \(= 26 + 18 - 33 = 11\text{.}\) Sport only \(= 26 - 11 = 15\) and choir only \(= 18 - 11 = 7\text{.}\)
