Example 6.3.8 Drawing from a Column.

Draw \(\mathbf{b} = \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 5 \end{pmatrix}\) starting at \((4, -1)\text{,}\) and \(\mathbf{c} = \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}\) starting at \((-3, 2)\text{.}\) Where does each arrow end?
🔗

Solution.

From \((4, -1)\text{,}\) move \(2\) left and \(5\) up: \(\mathbf{b}\) ends at \((2, 4)\text{.}\)
🔗
From \((-3, 2)\text{,}\) move \(4\) right and not up or down: \(\mathbf{c}\) ends at \((1, 2)\text{.}\)
