Example 10.5.30 A Three-Part Journey.

Kiprono’s journey to work has three parts: a boda boda, which is on time with probability \(0.9\text{;}\) a matatu, on time with probability \(0.8\text{;}\) and a train, on time with probability \(0.7\text{.}\) Assume the three parts are independent. Find the probability that (a) all three are on time, (b) exactly one is late, (c) at least one is late.
🔗

Solution.

(a) \(0.9 \times 0.8 \times 0.7 = 0.504\text{.}\)
🔗
(b) Only the boda boda late: \(0.1 \times 0.8 \times 0.7 = 0.056\text{.}\) Only the matatu: \(0.9 \times 0.2 \times 0.7 = 0.126\text{.}\) Only the train: \(0.9 \times 0.8 \times 0.3 = 0.216\text{.}\) Total \(0.398\text{.}\)
🔗
(c) \(1 - 0.504 = 0.496\text{.}\)
