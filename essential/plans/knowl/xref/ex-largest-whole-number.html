Exercise 6. Challenge: The Largest Whole Number.

Find the largest whole number \(n\) with \(3(n + 4) \lt 50 - n\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
Expand the bracket first.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(n = 9\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(3n + 12 \lt 50 - n\text{.}\) Add \(n\text{:}\) \(4n + 12 \lt 50\text{.}\) Subtract \(12\text{:}\) \(4n \lt 38\text{.}\) Divide by \(4\text{:}\) \(n \lt 9.5\text{.}\) The largest whole number less than \(9.5\) is \(9\text{.}\) Check: \(3(13) = 39 \lt 41\text{.}\)
