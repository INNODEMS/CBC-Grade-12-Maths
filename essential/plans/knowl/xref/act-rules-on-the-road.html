Learner Experience 3.1.3 Rules on the Road.

Work with a partner.
🔗
  1. A sign in Nakuru town shows a speed limit of \(50\,\text{km/h}\text{.}\) Write the rule for a driver’s speed, \(s\,\text{km/h}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(s \le 50\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  2. You must be at least \(18\) years old to vote. Write the rule for a voter’s age, \(a\) years.
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(a \ge 18\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Milk in a dairy fridge must be kept below \(5\,^{\circ}\text{C}\text{.}\) Write the rule for the temperature, \(t\,^{\circ}\text{C}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(t \lt 5\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  4. More than \(40\,000\) fans watched a match at Kasarani. Write this for the number of fans, \(n\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(n \gt 40\,000\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  5. Draw each rule on a number line.
    🔗
    Solution.
    Filled circles at \(50\) and at \(18\text{,}\) because these values are allowed; open circles at \(5\) and at \(40\,000\text{,}\) because they are not. The thick line goes left from \(50\) and from \(5\text{,}\) and right from \(18\) and from \(40\,000\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  6. Which of the speeds \(49\text{,}\) \(50\) and \(50.5\,\text{km/h}\) obey rule (a)?
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(49\) and \(50\text{.}\) A driver at exactly \(50\,\text{km/h}\) is within the limit; \(50.5\,\text{km/h}\) is over it.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  7. Discuss with your partner: why do rules (a) and (c) use different signs?
    🔗
    Solution.
    The speed limit allows \(50\) itself (“at most \(50\)”), so the sign is \(\le\text{.}\) “Below \(5\)” does not allow \(5\) itself, so the sign is \(\lt\text{.}\)
