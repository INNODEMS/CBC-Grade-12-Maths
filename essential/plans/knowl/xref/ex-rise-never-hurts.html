Exercise 6. Challenge: A Rise Never Hurts.

Explain why a pay rise of Ksh \(1000\) a month always raises take-home pay, whatever the band, if the other deductions do not change.
🔗
Hint.
What is the highest rate at which any of the extra Ksh \(1000\) can be taxed?
🔗
🔗
Answer.
At most \(35\%\) of the rise is taxed, so at least Ksh \(650\) of it is kept.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Each slice is taxed at its own band’s rate, and the highest rate is \(35\%\text{.}\) So the extra \(1000\) adds at most \(350\) to the tax, and the relief does not change. Take-home pay rises by at least \(1000 - 350 = 650\text{.}\)
