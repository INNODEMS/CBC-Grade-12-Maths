Learner Experience 8.6.22 A Teacher’s Payslip.

Ms Jeptoo teaches in Eldoret. Her monthly basic pay is Ksh \(55\,000\) and her allowances are Ksh \(13\,000\text{.}\) Use Table 8.6.2.
🔗
  1. Find her gross pay.
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(55\,000 + 13\,000 = 68\,000\text{,}\) Ksh \(68\,000\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Her pension contribution of Ksh \(3000\) is taken off before tax. Find her taxable pay.
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(68\,000 - 3000 = 65\,000\text{,}\) Ksh \(65\,000\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Find her PAYE.
    🔗
    Solution.
    Tax \(4483.25 + (65\,000 - 32\,333) \times 0.3 = 4483.25 + 9800.10 = 14\,283.35\text{;}\) PAYE \(14\,283.35 - 2400 = 11\,883.35\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  4. The other deductions on her payslip total Ksh \(6450\text{.}\) Find her net pay.
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(68\,000 - 3000 - 11\,883.35 - 6450 = 46\,666.65\text{,}\) Ksh \(46\,666.65\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  5. Digital: open the PAYE page of the Kenya Revenue Authority website. Are the bands and relief the same as in Table 8.6.2? Write down the date you checked.
    🔗
    Solution.
    It depends on the date. If a later Finance Act has changed the figures, learners redo part (c) with the new table.
