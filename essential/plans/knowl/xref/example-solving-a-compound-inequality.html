Example 3.1.30 Solving a Compound Inequality.

Solve \(7 \le 2x + 3 \lt 15\) and list the integers that satisfy it.
🔗

Solution.

\begin{align*} 7 \amp\le 2x + 3 \lt 15\\ 4 \amp\le 2x \lt 12 \amp\amp \text{(subtract 3 from every part)}\\ 2 \amp\le x \lt 6 \amp\amp \text{(divide every part by 2)} \end{align*}
🔗
The integers are \(2\text{,}\) \(3\text{,}\) \(4\) and \(5\text{:}\) \(2\) is included, \(6\) is not.
