Exercise 1.

A ship sails along the meridian \(50^\circ\,\text{E}\) from latitude \(10^\circ\,\text{S}\) to latitude \(2^\circ\,\text{N}\) in \(2\) days \(12\) hours. Find its speed in knots and in kilometres per hour.
🔗
Answer.
\(12\,\text{knots}\text{;}\) \(22.2\,\text{km/h}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Opposite sides of the equator, so add: \(10^\circ + 2^\circ = 12^\circ = 720'\text{,}\) so \(720\,\text{nm}\text{.}\) Time \(2 \times 24 + 12 = 60\,\text{h}\text{.}\) Speed \(720 \div 60 = 12\,\text{knots}\) \(= 12 \times 1.853 = 22.2\,\text{km/h}\text{.}\)
