Exercise 2.

Translate the triangle \(P(1, 2)\text{,}\) \(Q(4, 2)\text{,}\) \(R(1, 6)\) by \(\begin{pmatrix} -5 \\ -3 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\) Check that the side lengths are unchanged.
🔗
Answer.
\(P'(-4, -1)\text{,}\) \(Q'(-1, -1)\text{,}\) \(R'(-4, 3)\text{;}\) the sides are \(3\text{,}\) \(4\) and \(5\) in both triangles.
