Exercise 4.

In Exercise 4.4.2.1, the best vertex has \(x \approx 11.43\text{.}\) Why may the company not book \(11\) bandas and \(2\) dormitory blocks?
🔗
Answer.
They sleep only \(157\text{,}\) fewer than \(160\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(7 \times 11 + 40 \times 2 = 77 + 80 = 157 \lt 160\text{:}\) three tourists would have no bed. With an “at least” condition, rounding down can leave too little.
