Example 6.1.10 Vector or Scalar?

Is each quantity a vector or a scalar?
🔗
  1. A bag of cement of mass \(30\,\text{kg}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. A wind blowing towards the west at \(20\,\text{km/h}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. A temperature of \(25\,^{\circ}\text{C}\) in Mombasa.
    🔗
    🔗
  4. A boda boda’s speed of \(40\,\text{km/h}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  5. A pull of \(150\) N on a rope towards the north.
    🔗
    🔗
  6. A journey of \(2\) hours.
    🔗
    🔗
🔗

Solution.

  1. Scalar: mass has a size and a unit only.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Vector: the wind’s speed is given with a direction, so this is a velocity.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Scalar: a temperature has no direction.
    🔗
    🔗
  4. Scalar: a speed says how fast, not which way.
    🔗
    🔗
  5. Vector: a force with a direction.
    🔗
    🔗
  6. Scalar: time has no direction.
    🔗
    🔗
