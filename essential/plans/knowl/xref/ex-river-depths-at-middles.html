Exercise 3.

A river is \(15\,\text{m}\) wide. Its depths at the middles of five strips, each \(3\,\text{m}\) wide, are \(0.6, 1.4, 1.8, 1.5, 0.7\) metres. Estimate the area of the cross-section.
🔗
Answer.
\(18\,\text{m}^2\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(A \approx 3 \times (0.6 + 1.4 + 1.8 + 1.5 + 0.7) = 3 \times 6 = 18\,\text{m}^2\text{.}\)
