Exercise 1.

Estimate the area of the region bounded by the curve \(y = 4 + 0.3x - 0.1x^2\text{,}\) the \(x\)-axis and the lines \(x = 0\) and \(x = 6\text{,}\) by counting the unit squares.
🔗
Answer.
About \(23\) square units (\(20\) full, \(6\) part).
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(F = 20\text{,}\) \(P = 6\text{:}\) estimate \(20 + 3 = 23\) square units.
