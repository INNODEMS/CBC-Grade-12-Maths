Example 5.1.11 A Fish Pond in Kirinyaga.

A farmer in Kirinyaga draws the plan of her fish pond on a grid. Each square stands for \(2\,\text{m}\) by \(2\,\text{m}\text{.}\) Estimate the area of the pond.
🔗

Solution.

There are \(F = 18\) full squares and \(P = 26\) part squares, so
\begin{equation*} \text{estimate} = 18 + \tfrac{1}{2} \times 26 = 18 + 13 = 31 \text{ squares} \end{equation*}
One square stands for \(2 \times 2 = 4\,\text{m}^2\text{,}\) so the area of the pond is about \(31 \times 4 = 124\,\text{m}^2\text{.}\)
