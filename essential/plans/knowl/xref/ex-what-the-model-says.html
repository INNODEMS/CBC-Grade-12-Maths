Exercise 4.

The constraint \(x + 6y \le 64\) and the objective \(P = 300x + 800y\) come from Learner Experience 4.1.21. What do the numbers \(6\text{,}\) \(64\) and \(800\) mean?
🔗
Answer.
\(6\text{:}\) machine hours for one dress; \(64\text{:}\) machine hours available in a week; \(800\text{:}\) profit in shillings on one dress
