Example 6.6.9 Magnitude from Components.

Find the magnitudes of \(\begin{pmatrix} 8 \\ -15 \end{pmatrix}\) and \(\begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ -7 \end{pmatrix}\) (Figure 6.6.5).
🔗

Solution.

\begin{align*} \left|\begin{pmatrix} 8 \\ -15 \end{pmatrix}\right| \amp = \sqrt{8^2 + (-15)^2} = \sqrt{64 + 225} = \sqrt{289} = 17\\ \left|\begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ -7 \end{pmatrix}\right| \amp = \sqrt{16 + 49} = \sqrt{65} \approx 8.06 \end{align*}
