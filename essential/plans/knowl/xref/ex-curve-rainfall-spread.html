Exercise 2.

Use your curve from Exercise 9.2.2 to estimate the median, the interquartile range and the quartile deviation of the rainfall.
🔗
Answer.
Median \(\approx 25.8\,\text{mm}\text{;}\) IQR \(\approx 19.3\,\text{mm}\text{;}\) QD \(\approx 9.7\,\text{mm}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Across from \(20\text{,}\) \(10\) and \(30\text{:}\) \(25.8\text{,}\) \(16.25\) and \(35.56\,\text{mm}\text{.}\) IQR \(= 35.56 - 16.25 = 19.31\text{;}\) QD \(= 9.65 \approx 9.7\,\text{mm}\text{.}\)
