Exercise 2.

A plan uses squares that stand for \(20\,\text{m}\) along a road and \(10\,\text{m}\) across it. A plot covers \(31\) full and \(11\) part squares. Estimate its area in square metres and in hectares.
🔗
Answer.
\(7300\,\text{m}^2 = 0.73\) hectares
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(31 + 5.5 = 36.5\) squares; one square is \(20 \times 10 = 200\,\text{m}^2\text{;}\) area about \(36.5 \times 200 = 7300\,\text{m}^2 = 0.73\) hectares.
