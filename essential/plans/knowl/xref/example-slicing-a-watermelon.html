Example 7.1.10 Slicing a Watermelon.

A watermelon from a farm in Makueni is a sphere of radius \(13\,\text{cm}\text{.}\) It is cut with one straight cut \(5\,\text{cm}\) from its centre. Find the radius of the cut face.
🔗

Solution.

The centre of the watermelon, the centre of the cut face and a point on the edge of the cut make a right-angled triangle with hypotenuse \(13\,\text{cm}\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} r^2 = 13^2 - 5^2 = 169 - 25 = 144 \end{equation*}
so \(r = 12\,\text{cm}\text{.}\)
