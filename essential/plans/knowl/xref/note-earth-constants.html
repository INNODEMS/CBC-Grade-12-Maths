Note 7.1.2 Values Used in This Chapter.

  • The earth is treated as a sphere. (The real earth bulges a little at the equator: measured to the equator it is wider than measured through the poles, by about \(1\) part in \(300\text{.}\) We ignore this.)
    🔗
    🔗
  • The radius of the earth is \(R = 6370\,\text{km}\text{.}\) Its mean radius is about \(6371\,\text{km}\text{;}\) the value \(6370\,\text{km}\) is the one used in Kenyan secondary schools.
    🔗
    🔗
  • We take \(\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}\text{.}\) Then the length of a great circle is \(2\pi R = 2 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 6370 = 40\,040\,\text{km}\) exactly, and \(1^\circ\) of a great circle is \(40\,040 \div 360 = 111.2\,\text{km}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • One nautical mile is \(1.853\,\text{km}\) and one knot is one nautical mile per hour (Section 7.2 and Section 7.3).
