Exercise 6. Challenge: An Empty Region.

Draw \(x \gt 3\text{,}\) \(y \ge 0\) and \(2x + 7y \le 4\text{.}\) What do you notice?
🔗
Hint.
Where does \(2x + 7y = 4\) cross the \(x\)-axis?
🔗
🔗
Answer.
No region is left clear: no point satisfies all three.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(2x + 7y = 4\) crosses the \(x\)-axis at \((2, 0)\text{.}\) With \(y \ge 0\text{,}\) \(2x + 7y \le 4\) needs \(2x \le 4\text{,}\) so \(x \le 2\text{,}\) which contradicts \(x \gt 3\text{.}\)
