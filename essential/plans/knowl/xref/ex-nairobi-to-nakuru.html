Exercise 5.

A matatu travels about \(160\,\text{km}\) from Nairobi to Nakuru and must not go faster than \(80\,\text{km/h}\text{.}\)
  1. Write an inequality for the journey time, \(t\) hours, and solve it.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. The matatu leaves at 7:00 am. Can it arrive legally before 8:45 am?
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Answer.
(a) \(80t \ge 160\text{,}\) so \(t \ge 2\text{.}\) (b) No: the earliest legal arrival is 9:00 am.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
(a) In \(t\) hours at most \(80\,\text{km/h}\text{,}\) the matatu covers at most \(80t\,\text{km}\text{,}\) and it must cover \(160\,\text{km}\text{,}\) so \(80t \ge 160\) and \(t \ge 2\text{.}\) (b) The journey takes at least \(2\) hours, so it cannot arrive before 9:00 am. Arriving at 8:45 am would need an average speed above \(80\,\text{km/h}\text{,}\) which breaks the limit and puts passengers at risk.
