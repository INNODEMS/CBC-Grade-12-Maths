Exercise 5.

A bag holds \(3\) red counters and \(n\) blue counters. Two counters are drawn with replacement. The probability that both are red is \(\frac{9}{64}\text{.}\) Find \(n\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(n = 5\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\left(\frac{3}{3 + n}\right)^2 = \frac{9}{64}\text{,}\) so \(\frac{3}{3 + n} = \frac{3}{8}\) and \(n = 5\text{.}\)
