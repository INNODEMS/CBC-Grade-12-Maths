Example 4.2.9 A Boundary Through the Origin.

The hotel of Example 4.1.9 decides it wants more than three times as much chicken as beef: \(y \gt 3x\text{.}\) Graph this constraint.
🔗

Solution.

The boundary \(y = 3x\) passes through \((0, 0)\) and \((2, 6)\text{.}\) Draw it dashed, since the sign is \(\gt\text{:}\) an order with exactly three times as much chicken is not allowed.
🔗
The origin is on the line, so it cannot be the test point. Test \((1, 0)\text{:}\) is \(0 \gt 3 \times 1\text{?}\) No, it is false. So \((1, 0)\) is on the unwanted side: shade the side of the line containing \((1, 0)\text{,}\) below and to the right of it.
