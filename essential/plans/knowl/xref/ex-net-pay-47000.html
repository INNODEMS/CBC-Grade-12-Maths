Exercise 1.

A worker’s gross pay is Ksh \(47\,000\) a month. The pension contribution of Ksh \(2000\) is taken off before tax, and other deductions total Ksh \(3900\text{.}\) Find the PAYE and the net pay.
🔗
Answer.
PAYE Ksh \(5883.35\text{;}\) net pay Ksh \(35\,216.65\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Taxable pay \(45\,000\text{;}\) tax \(4483.25 + 12\,667 \times 0.3 = 8283.35\text{;}\) PAYE \(5883.35\text{.}\) Net pay \(47\,000 - 2000 - 5883.35 - 3900 = 35\,216.65\text{.}\)
