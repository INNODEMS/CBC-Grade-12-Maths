Exercise 1.

Use the heights of the \(60\) seedlings in Exercise 9.2.1 (cumulative frequencies \(4, 13, 28, 46, 56, 60\) for the classes 10–14 to 35–39 cm). Calculate the lower quartile, the median and the upper quartile.
🔗
Answer.
\(Q_1 \approx 20.2\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) median \(\approx 25.1\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) \(Q_3 \approx 29.2\,\text{cm}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Positions \(15\text{,}\) \(30\text{,}\) \(45\text{.}\) \(Q_1 = 19.5 + \dfrac{15 - 13}{15} \times 5 = 20.17\text{;}\) median \(= 24.5 + \dfrac{30 - 28}{18} \times 5 = 25.06\text{;}\) \(Q_3 = 24.5 + \dfrac{45 - 28}{18} \times 5 = 29.22\text{.}\)
