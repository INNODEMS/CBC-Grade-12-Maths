Learner Experience 6.4.2 Walking on the School Field.

  1. From \(S\text{,}\) walk \(6\,\text{m}\) east, then \(8\,\text{m}\) north, and mark the finish \(F\text{.}\) Draw the walk on squared paper with \(1\) square for \(1\,\text{m}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    See Figure 6.4.3.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Write each leg as a column vector (east and north positive).
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(\begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}\) and \(\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 8 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Write \(\overrightarrow{SF}\) as a column vector.
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(\begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ 8 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  4. Measure the straight-line distance \(SF\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    About \(10\,\text{m}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  5. Start at \(S\) again and walk \(8\,\text{m}\) north first, then \(6\,\text{m}\) east. Where do you finish?
    🔗
    Solution.
    At the same point \(F\text{:}\) the order of the legs does not matter.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  6. Add a third leg of \(2\,\text{m}\) west to the first walk. What is the resultant now?
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(\begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 8 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\)
