Example 3.1.11 Loading a Pick-up.

A pick-up can carry at most \(1200\,\text{kg}\text{.}\) It already carries \(380\,\text{kg}\) of timber. Bags of cement weigh \(50\,\text{kg}\) each. Write an inequality for the number of bags, \(n\text{,}\) and find the greatest number of bags it can carry.
🔗

Solution.

The total load is \(380 + 50n\,\text{kg}\text{,}\) and “at most” gives \(\le\text{:}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} 380 + 50n \amp\le 1200\\ 50n \amp\le 820 \amp\amp \text{(subtract 380)}\\ n \amp\le 16.4 \amp\amp \text{(divide by 50)} \end{align*}
🔗
\(n\) must be a whole number, so the pick-up can carry at most \(16\) bags. Check: \(17\) bags would make \(380 + 850 = 1230\,\text{kg}\text{,}\) which is over the limit.
