Example 8.6.10 A Bigger Salary.

Find the PAYE on a monthly taxable pay of Ksh \(120\,000\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

The slices are \(24\,000\) at \(10\%\text{,}\) \(8333\) at \(25\%\) and \(120\,000 - 32\,333 = 87\,667\) at \(30\%\text{.}\)
\begin{equation*} \text{tax} = 2400 + 2083.25 + 87\,667 \times 0.3 = 2400 + 2083.25 + 26\,300.10 = 30\,783.35 \end{equation*}
The PAYE is \(30\,783.35 - 2400 = 28\,383.35\text{,}\) Ksh \(28\,383.35\text{.}\)
