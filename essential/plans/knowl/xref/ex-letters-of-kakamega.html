Exercise 2.

The letters of the word KAKAMEGA are written on eight cards, one letter on each. One card is taken at random. Find the probability that it shows (a) A, (b) K, (c) a vowel.
🔗
Answer.
(a) \(\frac{3}{8}\) (b) \(\frac{1}{4}\) (c) \(\frac{1}{2}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The letters are K, A, K, A, M, E, G, A. There are three A’s, two K’s, and four vowels (A, A, A, E): \(\frac{3}{8}\text{,}\) \(\frac{2}{8} = \frac{1}{4}\) and \(\frac{4}{8} = \frac{1}{2}\text{.}\)
