Exercise 6. Challenge: Choosing a Test Point.

Explain why \((0, 0)\) cannot be the test point for \(5x - 9y \gt 0\text{,}\) and draw the region.
🔗
Hint.
Substitute \((0, 0)\) into \(5x - 9y\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\((0, 0)\) is on the boundary \(5x - 9y = 0\text{,}\) so it is on neither side. Test \((1, 0)\text{:}\) \(5 \gt 0\) is true. Draw a dashed line through \((0, 0)\) and \((9, 5)\) and shade the side containing \((0, 1)\text{.}\)
