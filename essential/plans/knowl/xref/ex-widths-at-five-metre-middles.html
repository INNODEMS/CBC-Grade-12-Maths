Exercise 2.

The widths of a plot were measured at the middles of six strips, each \(5\,\text{m}\) wide: \(14, 17, 19, 18, 15, 11\) metres. Estimate the area of the plot.
🔗
Answer.
\(470\,\text{m}^2\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(A \approx 5 \times (14 + 17 + 19 + 18 + 15 + 11) = 5 \times 94 = 470\,\text{m}^2\text{.}\)
