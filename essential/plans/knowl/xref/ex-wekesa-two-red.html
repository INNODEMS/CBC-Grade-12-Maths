Exercise 6. Challenge: Find the Mistake.

A bag holds \(2\) red and \(3\) white beads. Two are taken without replacement. Wekesa says the probability that both are red is \(\left(\frac{2}{5}\right)^2 = \frac{4}{25}\text{.}\) Find his mistake and the correct answer.
🔗
Hint.
What is left in the bag after a red bead is taken?
🔗
🔗
Answer.
He worked as if the first bead were put back. The answer is \(\frac{1}{10}\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
After a red bead is taken, \(1\) red and \(3\) white are left, so the second branch is \(\frac{1}{4}\text{:}\) \(\frac{2}{5} \times \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{10}\text{.}\)
