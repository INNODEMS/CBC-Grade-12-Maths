Example 6.6.10 Distance Between Two Places.

On a map with \(1\) unit to \(1\,\text{km}\text{,}\) a borehole is at \(B(-2, 3)\) and a water point at \(W(4, -5)\text{.}\) How far is the water point from the borehole in a straight line?
🔗

Solution.

End minus start: \(\overrightarrow{BW} = \begin{pmatrix} 4 - (-2) \\ -5 - 3 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ -8 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\)
🔗
\(BW = \sqrt{6^2 + (-8)^2} = \sqrt{100} = 10\,\text{km}\text{.}\)
