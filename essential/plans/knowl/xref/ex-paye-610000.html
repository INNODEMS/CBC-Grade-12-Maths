Exercise 5.

A company director’s taxable pay is Ksh \(610\,000\) a month. Find the PAYE.
🔗
Answer.
Tax Ksh \(180\,533.35\text{;}\) PAYE Ksh \(178\,133.35\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Up to \(500\,000\) the tax is \(144\,783.35\text{.}\) The next \(110\,000\) is in the \(32.5\%\) band: \(110\,000 \times 0.325 = 35\,750\text{.}\) Tax \(180\,533.35\text{;}\) PAYE \(180\,533.35 - 2400 = 178\,133.35\text{.}\)
