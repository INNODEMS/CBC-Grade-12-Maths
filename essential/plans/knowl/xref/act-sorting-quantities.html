Learner Experience 6.1.3 Sorting Quantities.

Your group has twelve cards:
🔗
  • the mass of a sack of maize, \(90\,\text{kg}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • the speed of a matatu, \(60\,\text{km/h}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • a displacement of \(2\,\text{km}\) north of the school
    🔗
    🔗
  • the temperature in Eldoret, \(18\,^{\circ}\text{C}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • a push of \(200\) N on a handcart, towards the east
    🔗
    🔗
  • a lesson time of \(40\) minutes
    🔗
    🔗
  • a velocity of \(15\,\text{m/s}\) towards the west
    🔗
    🔗
  • a distance of \(3\,\text{km}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • the weight of a learner, \(600\) N
    🔗
    🔗
  • the area of a shamba, \(0.5\) hectares
    🔗
    🔗
  • the price of a loaf, Ksh \(65\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • the capacity of a water tank, \(5000\,\text{litres}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
  1. Sort the cards into two piles: “size only” and “size and direction”.
    🔗
    Solution.
    Size only: mass, speed, temperature, lesson time, distance, area, price, capacity. Size and direction: displacement, push, velocity, weight.
    🔗
    🔗

    Note 6.1.4.

    Weight is the pull of the earth, so it always points down; that is why the weight card goes in the “size and direction” pile even though no direction is written on it.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Some cards measure similar things, one with a direction and one without. Find three such pairs.
    🔗
    Solution.
    Distance and displacement; speed and velocity; mass and weight.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Achieng walks \(200\,\text{m}\) north from the school gate to the library, then \(200\,\text{m}\) back to the gate. How far did she walk? Where is she compared with where she started?
    🔗
    Solution.
    She walked \(400\,\text{m}\text{,}\) but she is back at the start: her displacement is zero.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  4. Look at the two road signs in Figure 6.1.5. Which sign gives a quantity with a direction?
    🔗
    Solution.
    The direction sign: its arrow and distance give a displacement. The \(50\) sign is a speed limit, a size only.
