Exercise 5.

A farmer blends DAP and CAN fertiliser in the ratio \(7 : 5\) to make \(420\,\text{kg}\text{.}\) They cost Ksh \(72\) and Ksh \(54\) a kilogram. Find the mass of each and the cost of 1 kg of the blend.
🔗
Answer.
\(245\,\text{kg}\) and \(175\,\text{kg}\text{;}\) Ksh \(64.50\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Total cost \(245 \times 72 + 175 \times 54 = 17\,640 + 9450 = 27\,090\text{,}\) and \(27\,090 \div 420 = 64.50\text{.}\)
