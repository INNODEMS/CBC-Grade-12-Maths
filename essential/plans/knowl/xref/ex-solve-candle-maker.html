Exercise 1.

Solve the candle maker’s model of Exercise 4.1.2.1: maximise \(P = 300x + 500y\) subject to \(5x + 6y \le 136\text{,}\) \(x + 7y \le 120\text{,}\) \(x \ge 0\text{,}\) \(y \ge 0\text{,}\) whole numbers.
🔗
Answer.
\(8\) boxes of plain and \(16\) boxes of scented candles, Ksh \(10\,400\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
From \(x + 7y = 120\text{,}\) \(x = 120 - 7y\text{;}\) then \(5(120 - 7y) + 6y = 136\text{,}\) so \(29y = 464\text{,}\) \(y = 16\text{,}\) \(x = 8\text{.}\) The vertices are \((0, 0)\text{,}\) \((27.2, 0)\text{,}\) \((8, 16)\) and \(\left(0, \tfrac{120}{7}\right)\text{,}\) with \(P = 0\text{,}\) \(8160\text{,}\) \(10\,400\) and about \(8571\text{.}\) The best is \((8, 16)\text{,}\) already whole: Ksh \(10\,400\text{.}\)
