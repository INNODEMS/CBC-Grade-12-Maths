Exercise 1.

Find the range, the interquartile range and the quartile deviation of \(9, 3, 14, 5, 12, 1, 10\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
Range \(13\text{;}\) \(Q_1 = 3\text{,}\) \(Q_3 = 12\text{;}\) IQR \(9\text{;}\) QD \(4.5\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
In order: \(1, 3, 5, 9, 10, 12, 14\text{.}\) Range \(14 - 1 = 13\text{.}\) Positions \(2\) and \(6\) give \(Q_1 = 3\) and \(Q_3 = 12\text{,}\) so IQR \(= 9\) and QD \(= 4.5\text{.}\)
