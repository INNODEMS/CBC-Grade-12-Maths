Example 8.6.9 Just into the Second Band.

Find the PAYE on a monthly taxable pay of Ksh \(28\,000\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

The first \(24\,000\) is taxed at \(10\%\text{:}\) \(2400\text{.}\) The remaining \(4000\) is in the \(25\%\) band: \(4000 \times 0.25 = 1000\text{.}\) The tax is \(2400 + 1000 = 3400\text{,}\) and the PAYE is \(3400 - 2400 = 1000\text{,}\) Ksh \(1000\text{.}\)
