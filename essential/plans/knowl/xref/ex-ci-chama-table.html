Exercise 4.

A chama deposits Ksh \(50\,000\) at \(11\%\) per annum compound interest. Make a year-by-year table for \(3\) years and find the interest earned in the third year.
🔗
Answer.
Amounts \(55\,500\text{,}\) \(61\,605\text{,}\) \(68\,381.55\text{;}\) interest in the third year Ksh \(6776.55\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Multiply by \(1.11\) each year: \(55\,500\text{,}\) \(61\,605\text{,}\) \(68\,381.55\text{.}\) The third year’s interest is \(68\,381.55 - 61\,605 = 6776.55\text{.}\)
