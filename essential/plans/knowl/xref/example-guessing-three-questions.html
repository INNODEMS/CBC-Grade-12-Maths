Example 10.5.27 Guessing Three Questions.

Kerubo guesses the answers to three multiple-choice questions, each with four options. Find the probability that she gets (a) all three right, (b) none right, (c) exactly one right, (d) at least one right.
🔗

Solution.

Each guess is right with probability \(\frac{1}{4}\) and wrong with probability \(\frac{3}{4}\text{,}\) independently.
🔗
(a) \(\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^3 = \frac{1}{64}\text{.}\) (b) \(\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^3 = \frac{27}{64}\text{.}\)
🔗
(c) Three paths (RWW, WRW, WWR), each \(\frac{1}{4} \times \frac{3}{4} \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{9}{64}\text{:}\) \(3 \times \frac{9}{64} = \frac{27}{64}\text{.}\)
🔗
(d) \(1 - \frac{27}{64} = \frac{37}{64}\text{.}\)
