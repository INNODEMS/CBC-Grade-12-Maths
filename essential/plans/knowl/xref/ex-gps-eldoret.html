Exercise 5.

A phone in Eldoret shows the position 0.5143, 35.2698. Write this position in degrees and minutes (to the nearest minute), with N or S and E or W.
🔗
Answer.
\((0^\circ 31'\,\text{N},\ 35^\circ 16'\,\text{E})\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(0.5143 \times 60 = 30.9\text{,}\) so \(0^\circ 31'\text{;}\) positive, so north. \(0.2698 \times 60 = 16.2\text{,}\) so \(35^\circ 16'\text{;}\) positive, so east.
