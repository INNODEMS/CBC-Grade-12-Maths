Exercise 3.

Say whether each is a great circle, a small circle or half of a great circle.
  1. The parallel of latitude \(1^\circ 17'\,\text{S}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. The Greenwich meridian.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. The Greenwich meridian together with the \(180^\circ\) meridian.
    🔗
    🔗
  4. The parallel of latitude \(0^\circ\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  5. A circle on the earth’s surface of radius \(3000\,\text{km}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Answer.
(a) small; (b) half of a great circle; (c) great; (d) great (it is the equator); (e) small
🔗
🔗
Solution.
A great circle has its centre at the centre of the earth. (a) A parallel other than the equator has its centre on the axis above or below the earth’s centre: small. (b) A meridian runs from pole to pole only: half of a great circle. (c) The two meridians together make a whole circle through both poles: great. (d) The equator: great. (e) A great circle has radius \(R = 6370\,\text{km}\text{;}\) \(3000\,\text{km}\) is less, so small.
