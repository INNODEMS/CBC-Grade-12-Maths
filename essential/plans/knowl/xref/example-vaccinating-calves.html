Example 10.5.8 Vaccinating Calves.

On a farm in Nyandarua, \(0.8\) of the calves are vaccinated against East Coast fever. A vaccinated calf catches the disease with probability \(0.05\text{;}\) a calf that is not vaccinated catches it with probability \(0.4\text{.}\) A calf is chosen at random. Find the probability that it (a) is vaccinated and catches the disease, (b) catches the disease, (c) does not catch the disease.
🔗

Solution.

(a) Multiply along the path: \(0.8 \times 0.05 = 0.04\text{.}\)
🔗
(b) Two paths end in “ill”: \(0.8 \times 0.05 + 0.2 \times 0.4 = 0.04 + 0.08 = 0.12\text{.}\)
🔗
(c) \(1 - 0.12 = 0.88\text{,}\) or add the other two paths: \(0.76 + 0.12 = 0.88\text{.}\)
