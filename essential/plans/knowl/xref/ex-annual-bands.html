Exercise 6. Challenge: Monthly and Yearly Bands.

Show how the yearly bands and relief in Table 8.6.2 follow from the monthly ones. Why is the \(25\%\) band \(100\,000\) a year and not \(12 \times 8333 = 99\,996\text{,}\) and the \(30\%\) band \(5\,612\,000\) and not \(12 \times 467\,667 = 5\,612\,004\text{?}\)
🔗
Hint.
Divide the yearly band by \(12\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
Multiply by \(12\text{:}\) \(12 \times 24\,000 = 288\,000\text{,}\) \(12 \times 300\,000 = 3\,600\,000\text{,}\) \(12 \times 800\,000 = 9\,600\,000\) and relief \(12 \times 2400 = 28\,800\text{.}\) The \(25\%\) band is set at \(100\,000\) a year, and \(100\,000 \div 12 = 8333.33\) is rounded to \(8333\) a month. The \(30\%\) band ends at \(12 \times 500\,000 = 6\,000\,000\) a year, so it is \(6\,000\,000 - 288\,000 - 100\,000 = 5\,612\,000\text{;}\) the monthly \(467\,667 = 500\,000 - 32\,333\) carries the rounding of \(8333\text{.}\)
