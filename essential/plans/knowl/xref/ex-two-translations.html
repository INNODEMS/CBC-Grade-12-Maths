Exercise 6. Challenge: Two Translations.

Translate \(A(2, 1)\) by \(\begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}\) to \(A'\text{,}\) then translate \(A'\) by \(\begin{pmatrix} -5 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix}\) to \(A''\text{.}\) Which single translation takes \(A\) straight to \(A''\text{?}\)
🔗
Hint.
Add the two vectors.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(A'(5, 5)\text{,}\) \(A''(0, 7)\text{;}\) the translation by \(\begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(A'(2 + 3, 1 + 4) = (5, 5)\) and \(A''(5 - 5, 5 + 2) = (0, 7)\text{.}\) The single translation is the sum \(\begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix} -5 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix}\text{;}\) check: \((2 - 2, 1 + 6) = (0, 7)\text{.}\)
