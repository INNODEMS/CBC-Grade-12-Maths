Exercise 5.

Give two whole-number points in the region of Exercise 3.4.3 and one whole-number point that is not in it, with a reason.
🔗
Answer.
For example \((1, 1)\) and \((2, 1)\) are in it; \((4, 1)\) is not, because \(2(4) + 7(1) = 15\text{,}\) which is not less than \(14\text{.}\)
