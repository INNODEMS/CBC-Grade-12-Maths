Learner Experience 1.5.22 Three Subjects.

Twenty learners said which of Mathematics (\(M\)), Kiswahili (\(K\)) and Agriculture (\(Ag\)) they like.
🔗
  1. Draw a three-set Venn diagram and write the number of learners in each region.
    🔗
    Solution.
    With the region numbers of Figure 1.5.26 (\(M\text{,}\) \(K\text{,}\) \(Ag\) as \(A\text{,}\) \(B\text{,}\) \(C\)): regions \(1\) to \(8\) hold \(4, 1, 3, 2, 2, 2, 3, 3\text{.}\)

    🔗
    Solution.
    \(9\text{,}\) \(8\) and \(9\text{.}\)

  3. How many learners like exactly two of the subjects? How many like at least one?
    🔗
    Solution.
    Exactly two: \(1 + 2 + 2 = 5\text{.}\) At least one: \(20 - 3 = 17\text{.}\)
