Exercise 1.

The probability that Juma passes the theory part of his driving test is \(0.9\text{,}\) and the practical part \(0.75\text{,}\) independently. Find the probability that he passes (a) both, (b) at least one, (c) exactly one.
🔗
Answer.
(a) \(0.675\) (b) \(0.975\) (c) \(0.3\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
(a) \(0.9 \times 0.75 = 0.675\text{.}\) (b) \(1 - 0.1 \times 0.25 = 0.975\text{.}\) (c) \(0.9 \times 0.25 + 0.1 \times 0.75 = 0.225 + 0.075 = 0.3\text{.}\)
