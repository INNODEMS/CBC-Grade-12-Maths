Exercise 4.

A football of radius \(11\,\text{cm}\) rests in a circular ring of radius \(8\,\text{cm}\text{,}\) touching it all the way round. How high is the centre of the ball above the plane of the ring? Give your answer correct to 2 decimal places.
🔗
Answer.
\(\sqrt{57} = 7.55\,\text{cm}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The ring is a small circle of the ball. \(d^2 = 11^2 - 8^2 = 121 - 64 = 57\text{,}\) so \(d = \sqrt{57} = 7.55\,\text{cm}\text{.}\)
