Example 5.1.32 Under a Curve on Unit Squares.

Estimate the area of the region bounded by the curve \(y = 4.6 + 0.9x - 0.18x^2\text{,}\) the \(x\)-axis and the lines \(x = 0\) and \(x = 6\text{,}\) by counting the unit squares in Figure 5.1.33.
🔗

Solution.

Count column by column. The full squares are \(4 + 5 + 5 + 5 + 4 + 3 = 26\) and the part squares \(2 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 2 + 2 = 9\text{.}\) So
\begin{equation*} \text{estimate} = 26 + \tfrac{1}{2} \times 9 = 30.5 \text{ square units} \end{equation*}
