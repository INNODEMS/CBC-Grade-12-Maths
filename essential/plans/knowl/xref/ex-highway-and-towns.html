Exercise 5.

A lorry driver travels for \(p\) hours at \(80\,\text{km/h}\) on the highway and \(q\) hours at \(30\,\text{km/h}\) through towns. The driver covers at most \(360\,\text{km}\) in a day. Write the inequality and simplify it.
🔗
Answer.
\(80p + 30q \le 360\text{,}\) which simplifies to \(8p + 3q \le 36\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Distance is speed times time, so the distance is \(80p + 30q\,\text{km}\text{.}\) Divide every number by \(10\text{.}\) Keeping to the lower limit in towns protects pedestrians and other road users.
