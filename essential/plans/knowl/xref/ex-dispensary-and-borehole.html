Exercise 5.

On a map of a village near Kitui, the chief’s camp is the origin and \(1\) unit is \(1\,\text{km}\text{.}\) The dispensary is at \(D(4, -2)\) and the borehole at \(B(-3, 1)\text{.}\) Write the position vectors of \(D\) and \(B\text{,}\) and the vector \(\overrightarrow{DB}\text{.}\) Describe \(\overrightarrow{DB}\) in words.
🔗
Answer.
\(\begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}\text{,}\) \(\begin{pmatrix} -3 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}\text{;}\) \(\overrightarrow{DB} = \begin{pmatrix} -7 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}\text{:}\) \(7\,\text{km}\) west and \(3\,\text{km}\) north.
