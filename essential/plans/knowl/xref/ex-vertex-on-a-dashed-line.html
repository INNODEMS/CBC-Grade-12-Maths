Exercise 5. Challenge: A Vertex on a Dashed Line.

In the fundraiser, the class changes “at least \(30\) samosas” to “more than \(30\) samosas”, so \(y \ge 30\) becomes \(y \gt 30\text{.}\) Which vertices of R are no longer in R? Can the class still make a plan close to them?
🔗
Hint.
Which boundary becomes dashed?
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\((0, 30)\) and \((70, 30)\text{;}\) yes, for example \((69, 31)\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The line \(y = 30\) becomes dashed, so its points, including the vertices \((0, 30)\) and \((70, 30)\text{,}\) are not in R. Whole-number plans just above the line still work: \((69, 31)\) has \(100\) items, costs \(1035 + 1085 = 2120 \le 2520\) and has \(31 \gt 30\) samosas. The other two vertices are on solid lines and stay in R.
