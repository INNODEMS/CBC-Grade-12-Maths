Learner Experience 8.3.2 A Plot Near Kitengela.

A family bought a plot near Kitengela in 2021. These are its values in four years (invented for this activity).
🔗
  1. Find the increase in value each year, in Ksh.
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(80\,000\text{,}\) \(88\,000\) and \(96\,800\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Write each increase as a percentage of the value at the start of that year.
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(\dfrac{80\,000}{800\,000} \times 100 = 10\%\text{,}\) \(\dfrac{88\,000}{880\,000} \times 100 = 10\%\) and \(\dfrac{96\,800}{968\,000} \times 100 = 10\%\text{:}\) \(10\%\) each year.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  3. If the value keeps rising at the same rate, predict its value in 2026.
    🔗
    Solution.
    2026 is \(5\) years after 2021: \(800\,000 \times 1.1^5 = 1\,288\,408\text{,}\) Ksh \(1\,288\,408\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
  4. Ask at home: is land in your area gaining value? Why?
    🔗
    Solution.
    Answers vary: new roads, water, electricity, a growing town or a new school nearby can all raise land values.
