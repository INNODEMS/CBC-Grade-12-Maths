Exercise 3.

A SACCO member leaves Ksh \(220\,000\) in a fixed deposit at \(9.5\%\) per annum compound interest for \(5\) years. Find the amount and the interest, to the nearest shilling.
🔗
Answer.
Amount Ksh \(346\,333\text{;}\) interest Ksh \(126\,333\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(A = 220\,000 \times 1.095^5 = 346\,332.52\text{,}\) so Ksh \(346\,333\text{.}\) The interest is \(346\,333 - 220\,000 = 126\,333\text{.}\)
