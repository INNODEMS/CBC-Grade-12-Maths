Exercise 2.

Use the Kericho rainfall data in Exercise 9.2.2 (frequencies \(5, 8, 12, 9, 4, 2\) for \(0 \le r \lt 10\) to \(50 \le r \lt 60\,\text{mm}\)). Calculate the quartiles and the median.
🔗
Answer.
\(Q_1 \approx 16.3\,\text{mm}\text{,}\) median \(\approx 25.8\,\text{mm}\text{,}\) \(Q_3 \approx 35.6\,\text{mm}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Cumulative frequencies \(5, 13, 25, 34, 38, 40\text{;}\) positions \(10, 20, 30\text{.}\) \(Q_1 = 10 + \dfrac{10 - 5}{8} \times 10 = 16.25\text{;}\) median \(= 20 + \dfrac{20 - 13}{12} \times 10 = 25.83\text{;}\) \(Q_3 = 30 + \dfrac{30 - 25}{9} \times 10 = 35.56\text{.}\)
