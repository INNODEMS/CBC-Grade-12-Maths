Example 10.2.28 Score on a Spinner.

The spinner in Figure 10.2.26 has eight equal sectors labelled 1, 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 4. Let \(X\) be the score. Write down the probability distribution of \(X\) and find \(P(X \ge 2)\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

Count sectors out of \(8\text{:}\)
🔗
Check: \(\frac{3}{8} + \frac{2}{8} + \frac{2}{8} + \frac{1}{8} = 1\text{.}\) Then \(P(X \ge 2) = \frac{2}{8} + \frac{2}{8} + \frac{1}{8} = \frac{5}{8}\text{.}\)
