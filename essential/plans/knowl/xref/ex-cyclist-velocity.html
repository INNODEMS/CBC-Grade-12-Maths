Exercise 4.

A cyclist rides with velocity \(\begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}\) m/s (east and north parts). (a) What is her velocity if she rides twice as fast in the same direction? (b) What is her velocity if she rides back the opposite way at the first speed?
🔗
Answer.
(a) \(\begin{pmatrix} 8 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix}\) m/s (b) \(\begin{pmatrix} -4 \\ -3 \end{pmatrix}\) m/s
