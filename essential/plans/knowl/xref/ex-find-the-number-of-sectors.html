Exercise 5.

A coin is tossed and a spinner with \(n\) equal sectors numbered 1 to \(n\) is spun. The probability of a head and a 1 is \(\frac{1}{16}\text{.}\) Find \(n\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(n = 8\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
There are \(2n\) outcomes and one of them is a head and a 1, so \(\frac{1}{2n} = \frac{1}{16}\) and \(n = 8\text{.}\)
