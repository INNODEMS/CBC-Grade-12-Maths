Example 5.1.34 A Flower Bed in Nakuru.

A flower bed in a park in Nakuru lies between a straight path and a curved hedge. On the plan, each square stands for \(5\,\text{m}\) along the path and \(2\,\text{m}\) across it. Estimate the area of the flower bed.
🔗

Solution.

There are \(22\) full and \(9\) part squares, so the estimate is \(22 + 4.5 = 26.5\) squares. One square stands for \(5 \times 2 = 10\,\text{m}^2\text{,}\) so the area is about \(26.5 \times 10 = 265\,\text{m}^2\text{.}\)
