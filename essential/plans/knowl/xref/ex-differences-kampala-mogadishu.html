Exercise 2.

Take Kampala to be at \((0^\circ 19'\,\text{N},\ 32^\circ 35'\,\text{E})\) and Mogadishu at \((2^\circ 03'\,\text{N},\ 45^\circ 19'\,\text{E})\text{.}\) Find both differences.
🔗
Answer.
Latitude \(1^\circ 44'\text{;}\) longitude \(12^\circ 44'\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Both north: \(2^\circ 03' - 0^\circ 19' = 1^\circ 63' - 0^\circ 19' = 1^\circ 44'\text{.}\) Both east: \(45^\circ 19' - 32^\circ 35' = 44^\circ 79' - 32^\circ 35' = 12^\circ 44'\text{.}\)
