Exercise 1.

Graph \(4x + 9y \le 36\text{,}\) shading the unwanted side.
🔗
Answer.
A solid line through \((9, 0)\) and \((0, 4)\text{,}\) shaded on the side away from the origin
🔗
🔗
Solution.
With \(y = 0\text{,}\) \(x = 9\text{;}\) with \(x = 0\text{,}\) \(y = 4\text{.}\) The line is solid (\(\le\)). \((0, 0)\) gives \(0 \le 36\text{,}\) true, so shade the side away from the origin.
