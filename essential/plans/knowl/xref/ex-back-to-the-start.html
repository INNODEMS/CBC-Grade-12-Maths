Exercise 6. Challenge: Back to the Start.

\(\mathbf{a} = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix}\) and \(\mathbf{b} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\) Find \(\mathbf{c}\) so that \(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c} = \mathbf{0}\text{.}\) Draw \(\mathbf{a}\text{,}\) \(\mathbf{b}\) and \(\mathbf{c}\) head to tail. Why do they make a closed triangle?
🔗
Hint.
The route ends where it began.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(\mathbf{c} = \begin{pmatrix} -4 \\ -6 \end{pmatrix}\text{;}\) the resultant is zero, so the last head meets the first tail.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} = \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix}\text{,}\) so \(\mathbf{c}\) must be its reverse, \(\begin{pmatrix} -4 \\ -6 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\) Head to tail, the three arrows end where they began, so they close up into a triangle.
