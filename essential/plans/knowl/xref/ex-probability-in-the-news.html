Exercise 6.

Find two places where probability is used in Kenya, for example a weather forecast on radio or television, or a road-safety message. Write each as a probability statement and say what decision it helps someone make.
🔗
Answer.
Answers vary. A good answer gives the source, a statement such as “the probability of rain in Kisumu tomorrow is \(0.7\)”, and a decision, such as whether to plant or to carry an umbrella.
