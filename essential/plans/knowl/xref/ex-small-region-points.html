Exercise 3.

Draw the region \(x \ge 1\text{,}\) \(y \ge 1\text{,}\) \(2x + 7y \lt 14\) and list its whole-number points.
🔗
Answer.
\((1, 1)\text{,}\) \((2, 1)\) and \((3, 1)\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
With \(y = 1\text{:}\) \(2x \lt 7\text{,}\) so \(x \lt 3.5\text{:}\) \(x = 1, 2, 3\text{.}\) With \(y = 2\text{:}\) \(2x \lt 0\text{,}\) impossible with \(x \ge 1\text{.}\)
