Exercise 3.

The probability of rain on market day is \(0.3\text{.}\) If it rains, the probability that the market is busy is \(0.4\text{;}\) if it does not rain, it is \(0.8\text{.}\) Find the probability that the market is busy.
🔗
Answer.
\(0.68\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(0.3 \times 0.4 + 0.7 \times 0.8 = 0.12 + 0.56 = 0.68\text{.}\)
