Exercise 6. Challenge: Magnitudes of Multiples.

\(\left|\begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 12 \end{pmatrix}\right| = 13\text{.}\) Without using Pythagoras, find \(\left|\begin{pmatrix} 10 \\ 24 \end{pmatrix}\right|\) and \(\left|\begin{pmatrix} -15 \\ -36 \end{pmatrix}\right|\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
Write each vector as a multiple of \(\begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 12 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(26\text{;}\) \(39\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\begin{pmatrix} 10 \\ 24 \end{pmatrix} = 2\begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 12 \end{pmatrix}\text{,}\) so its magnitude is \(2 \times 13 = 26\text{.}\) \(\begin{pmatrix} -15 \\ -36 \end{pmatrix} = -3\begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 12 \end{pmatrix}\text{,}\) so its magnitude is \(|-3| \times 13 = 39\text{.}\)
