Exercise 5.

A mobile lender charges Ksh \(180\) on a one-month loan of Ksh \(2400\text{.}\) Find the rate per month and the equivalent simple rate per annum.
🔗
Answer.
\(7.5\%\) per month; \(90\%\) per annum
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\dfrac{180}{2400} \times 100 = 7.5\%\) per month, and \(7.5 \times 12 = 90\%\) per annum.
