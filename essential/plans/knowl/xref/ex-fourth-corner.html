Exercise 6. Challenge: The Fourth Corner.

\(ABCD\) is a parallelogram with \(A(1, 1)\text{,}\) \(B(5, 2)\) and \(C(6, 5)\text{.}\) Use \(\overrightarrow{AD} = \overrightarrow{BC}\) to find \(D\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
Opposite sides of a parallelogram are equal vectors.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(D(2, 4)\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\overrightarrow{BC} = \begin{pmatrix} 6 - 5 \\ 5 - 2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\) So \(D\) is \(1\) across and \(3\) up from \(A(1, 1)\text{:}\) \(D(2, 4)\text{.}\)
