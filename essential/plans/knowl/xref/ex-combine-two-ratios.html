Exercise 6. Challenge: Combining Two Ratios.

\(a : b = 3 : 4\) and \(b : c = 6 : 5\text{.}\) Find \(a : b : c\text{,}\) then share Ksh \(6200\) in this ratio.
🔗
Hint.
Make the two values of \(b\) the same: what is the lowest common multiple of \(4\) and \(6\text{?}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(9 : 12 : 10\text{;}\) Ksh \(1800\text{,}\) Ksh \(2400\) and Ksh \(2000\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Make \(b\) equal to \(12\) in both: \(a : b = 9 : 12\) and \(b : c = 12 : 10\text{,}\) so \(a : b : c = 9 : 12 : 10\text{.}\) That is \(31\) parts, and one part is \(6200 \div 31 = 200\text{.}\)
