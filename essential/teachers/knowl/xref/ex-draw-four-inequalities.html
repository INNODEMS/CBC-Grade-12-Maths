Exercise 1.

Draw \(x \ge 0\text{,}\) \(y \ge -1\text{,}\) \(x \lt 4\) and \(4x + 9y \le 36\) on one set of axes, shading the unwanted sides, and label \(R\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(R\) has corners \((0, -1)\text{,}\) \((4, -1)\text{,}\) \((4, \tfrac{20}{9})\) and \((0, 4)\text{.}\) The edge on \(x = 4\) is dashed; the others are solid.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Draw \(x = 0\text{,}\) \(y = -1\) and \(4x + 9y = 36\) solid (the signs include equality) and \(x = 4\) dashed. For \(4x + 9y = 36\) use \((0, 4)\) and \((9, 0)\text{.}\) Test \((0, 0)\text{:}\) \(0 \le 36\) is true, so shade the side away from the origin. Shade left of \(x = 0\text{,}\) below \(y = -1\) and right of \(x = 4\text{.}\) On \(x = 4\text{:}\) \(16 + 9y = 36\text{,}\) so \(y = \tfrac{20}{9}\text{.}\) On \(y = -1\) the sloping line is at \(x = 11.25\text{,}\) beyond \(x = 4\text{,}\) so the bottom corners are \((0, -1)\) and \((4, -1)\text{.}\)
