Example 6.4.9 A Boda Boda Route.

A boda boda rider leaves the stage and rides \(3\,\text{km}\) east, \(2\,\text{km}\) north, \(5\,\text{km}\) west and \(1\,\text{km}\) south along roads on a grid (Figure 6.4.8). Find the resultant displacement from the stage and the distance ridden.
🔗
Solution.
With east and north positive, the four legs are \(\begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}\text{,}\) \(\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix}\text{,}\) \(\begin{pmatrix} -5 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}\) and \(\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\) Adding:
🔗
\begin{equation*} \begin{pmatrix} 3 + 0 - 5 + 0 \\ 0 + 2 + 0 - 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} \end{equation*}
🔗
The rider finishes \(2\,\text{km}\) west and \(1\,\text{km}\) north of the stage. The distance ridden is \(3 + 2 + 5 + 1 = 11\,\text{km}\text{.}\)
🔗
A rider keeps to the marked roads for safety, so it is the distance ridden, not the resultant, that decides the fuel used and the time taken.
