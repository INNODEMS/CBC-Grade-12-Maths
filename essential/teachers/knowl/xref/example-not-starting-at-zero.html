Example 5.3.8 Not Starting at Zero.

Use the mid-ordinate rule with \(4\) strips to estimate the area bounded by \(y = 30 + 2x - x^2\text{,}\) the \(x\)-axis and the lines \(x = 1\) and \(x = 5\text{.}\) The trapezoidal estimate with the same strips is \(102\text{,}\) and a computer gives the exact area as \(102.67\) (to 2 decimal places).
🔗
Solution.
\(h = (5 - 1) \div 4 = 1\text{.}\) The first strip runs from \(1\) to \(2\text{,}\) so its middle is at \(1.5\text{,}\) not \(0.5\text{.}\) The middles are \(x = 1.5, 2.5, 3.5, 4.5\text{,}\) and the mid-ordinates are \(30.75, 28.75, 24.75, 18.75\text{.}\)
\begin{equation*} A \approx 1 \times (30.75 + 28.75 + 24.75 + 18.75) = 103 \end{equation*}
The mid-ordinate estimate \(103\) is a little too big and the trapezoidal estimate \(102\) a little too small.
