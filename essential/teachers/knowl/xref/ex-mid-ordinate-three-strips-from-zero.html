Exercise 1.

Use the mid-ordinate rule with \(3\) strips to estimate the area bounded by \(y = x^2 + x + 1\text{,}\) the \(x\)-axis and the lines \(x = 0\) and \(x = 3\text{.}\) The exact area is \(16.5\text{.}\) Is your estimate too big or too small?
🔗
Answer.
\(16.25\text{;}\) too small
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(h = 1\text{;}\) middles \(0.5, 1.5, 2.5\text{;}\) mid-ordinates \(1.75, 4.75, 9.75\text{.}\) \(A \approx 1 \times 16.25 = 16.25 \lt 16.5\text{:}\) the curve bends upwards, so the mid-ordinate rule underestimates.
