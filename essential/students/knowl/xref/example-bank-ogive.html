Example 9.2.10 Waiting at the Bank.

The waiting times of \(50\) customers at a bank in Kisumu are shown below. Draw a cumulative frequency curve and use it to estimate how many customers waited less than \(12\) minutes, and how many waited more than \(12\) minutes.
🔗
Solution.
These classes are written with their boundaries, so the upper class boundaries are \(5, 10, 15, 20, 25\text{.}\) The cumulative frequencies are \(6, 20, 38, 46, 50\text{,}\) so the points are \((0, 0)\text{,}\) \((5, 6)\text{,}\) \((10, 20)\text{,}\) \((15, 38)\text{,}\) \((20, 46)\) and \((25, 50)\text{.}\)
🔗
Go up from \(12\) minutes to the graph and across: about \(27\) customers waited less than \(12\) minutes. (By calculation along the segment from \((10, 20)\) to \((15, 38)\text{:}\) \(20 + 18 \times \dfrac{2}{5} = 27.2\text{.}\)) So about \(50 - 27 = 23\) customers waited more than \(12\) minutes.
