Example 10.2.6 Testing Maize Seed.

An agro-dealer in Eldoret tests \(250\) maize seeds from a new batch, and \(215\) germinate. Estimate the probability that a seed from the batch germinates. A farmer plants \(2000\) of these seeds. About how many would you expect to germinate?
🔗
Solution.
Relative frequency \(= \dfrac{215}{250} = 0.86\text{.}\) Expected number \(= 2000 \times 0.86 = 1720\) seeds.
