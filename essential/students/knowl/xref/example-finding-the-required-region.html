Example 3.4.5 Finding the Required Region.

Show the region \(x \ge 1\text{,}\) \(y \gt 0\text{,}\) \(3x + 8y \le 24\text{,}\) and list its whole-number points.
🔗
Solution.
\(x = 1\) is a solid vertical line; shade to its left. \(y = 0\) is the \(x\)-axis, dashed; shade below it. \(3x + 8y = 24\) crosses the axes at \((8, 0)\) and \((0, 3)\text{;}\) it is solid, and \((0, 0)\) gives \(0 \le 24\text{,}\) true, so shade the side away from the origin.
🔗
\(R\) is the clear triangle with corners \((1, 0)\text{,}\) \((8, 0)\) and \((1, 2.625)\text{;}\) its bottom edge is dashed.
🔗
Whole-number points: \(y\) must be at least \(1\text{.}\) For \(y = 1\text{,}\) \(3x \le 16\text{,}\) so \(x = 1\) to \(5\text{.}\) For \(y = 2\text{,}\) \(3x \le 8\text{,}\) so \(x = 1\) or \(2\text{.}\) That is \(7\) points: \((1, 1)\text{,}\) \((2, 1)\text{,}\) \((3, 1)\text{,}\) \((4, 1)\text{,}\) \((5, 1)\text{,}\) \((1, 2)\) and \((2, 2)\text{.}\)
